01 · The gap between rooms

Strategies stall between the offsite and daily execution.

A strategy agreed in a workshop sits on slides once the room clears. Leadership meets without current operational facts, while activity leads work without knowing whether priorities changed.

In the strategy room

Leaders debate old slides

Without structured updates from activity leads, quarterly reviews anchor on the loudest voice. Teams notice delays late, when changing course is harder.

With activity leads

Managers wait for clear calls

Managers write status decks nobody reads, or pause while waiting for a decision. When leadership changes course, the update stays in meeting minutes.

02 · Three lines, not a status deck

Three fields from Contributors, instead of a weekly status deck.

Strapivo separates strategic calls from task management. Contributors send three fields before each session, and Vivo flags a contradiction whenever two focus activities fall off track.

03 · The weekly rhythm

Outside signals and inside updates meet on one agenda.

Dynamic Strategy pairs external Radar signals with internal Contributor updates, then sends every numbered decision back to the people responsible.
