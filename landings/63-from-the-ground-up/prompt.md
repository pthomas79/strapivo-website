# 63. Execution talks back

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Managing directors and COOs bridging leadership and the people running activities
- Colours: House palette, plus calm olive (#3E7355) for on-track status and warm amber (#C8861A) for "At risk" asks.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Strategies rarely fail in the boardroom alone; they fail in the silence between leadership and the managers running initiatives. Unlike concept 36 (the dark-to-light five-level altimeter descent), this is a bright, pragmatic landing page built around Strapivo's lightweight **Contributor relay**: before each strategic session, the people responsible for activities send three things — a status (on track, at risk, off track, or done), one line on the result, and an optional ask of leadership. That ask goes straight onto the drafted agenda, and leadership's numbered decision comes straight back to them. Execution informs strategy without turning leadership into task managers.

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero. Left column: headline "Execution informs strategy, without turning leadership into project managers." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System that connects the leadership team's agreed strategy, live market signals and concise weekly check-ins from the people running activities. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a clean **two-card relay diagram** (labelled "Illustrative example").
- Top card ("Contributor check-in · 2 minutes"): Activity, Status (`At risk`), Result (one line), Ask of leadership ("Approve temporary second distributor for Q2").
- Clean Azure relay arrow downward to Bottom card ("Thursday 60-minute session -> SDS-2026-014"): Decision (`Adapt`), Reply linked back to the Contributor.

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. The gap between the offsite and the floor. Leadership meets without current ground truth; managers run activities without knowing whether priorities moved. Include one executive quote from the brief.
2. Three lines, not a status deck. Drawing 2: a clean breakdown of the four roles (Owner, Admin, Member, Contributor) highlighting how little overhead Contributors carry (status, one line on the result, optional ask) and how two off-track focus activities trigger Vivo's "Light still green?" prompt.
3. The full weekly rhythm. External Radar signals plus internal Contributor updates feed the 24-hour drafted agenda; the 60-minute session uses sealed first takes and five options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet); every change is announced cleanly with its decision number.
4. Human authority, EU-only infrastructure and four-to-five-hour onboarding.
5. Closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Practical, grounded, bright. Crisp white cards on Paper, clear directional lines, zero jargon, comfortable reading on both desktop and phone. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
