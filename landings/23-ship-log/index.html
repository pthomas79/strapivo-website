Dynamic Strategy

Small, early corrections. Occasionally a change of course.

Most strategies are agreed at an offsite and left in a drawer until the annual review. Strapivo keeps yours on the chart. Vivo, Strapivo's AI, logs market signals against the goals you set. Your leadership team decides each correction in a regular 60-minute session.

The drawer

Between offsites, nothing moves.

The team agrees a strategy in a workshop and files it. The market keeps moving. Nobody checks the course until the quarterly or annual review, when a correction costs the most.

News arrives unconnected to the goals the team set. Meetings anchor on the loudest voice. Decisions end up in slides nobody reads, and a year later nobody can say what was decided, why, or who disagreed.

Failure mode 1

Inertia

The team holds the January heading whatever the water does.

Failure mode 2

Thrash

The team changes course at every headline and gets nowhere.

What leadership teams tell us

  • 01"We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."
  • 02"We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands."
  • 03"I know there's a threat coming. I don't know what it costs us to wait."
  • 04"I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings."
  • 05"We're not changing strategy every week, but we are revising our tactics."

Standing orders

The routine behind every entry

Your strategy goes in as an Ambition, success criteria, objectives, activities and risks, all tied to your Business Model Canvas. Then the same routine runs each week, fortnight or month.

  1. WatchMarket intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday. A red-line exception interrupts that schedule.
  2. TriageVivo sorts each signal to a criterion. A delegated person marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Vivo uses dismissed signals to learn what counts as noise.
  3. MoveVivo proposes new risk positions. A person confirms. A risk left in the top right for 14 days lands in the session lead's "Needs you" list.
  4. AgendaVivo drafts it 24 hours before the session, old business first. The room approves it as step one.
  5. Session60 minutes by default. Per item: evidence, sealed first takes, conversation with Vivo, decision.
  6. RecordVivo reads back each decision with any dissent. The session lead confirms it and it gets a permanent number.
  7. EscalateDecisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver, as a memo or in the board pack.

New heading

Pivot

A detour

Take advantage

Detour activities are added. The criterion does not change.

Parked or let go

Not yet

The question is "what's missing?" It is parked with a trigger that brings it back, or let go with a rationale.
