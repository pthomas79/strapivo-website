02

Authority model

Roles and escalation

Four roles set what a person can see and change. Two further permissions go to named people. A decision beyond the team's authority is escalated to an approver.

2.1

Roles

Owner
Accepts or rejects changes to accepted truth, including the placements Vivo proposes during setup.
Admin
Runs the workspace and its members.
Member
Works in the Strategy Room. Gives a sealed first take on each session item and takes part in the decision.
Contributor
Is responsible for activities. Before each session, reports a status, one line on the result and an optional ask of leadership.
2.2

Permissions given to named people

Can lead sessions
Edits the drafted agenda, runs the session and confirms each entry in the Record of Decisions. Receives the "Needs you" list. A risk that sits in the top right for 14 days with no decision lands on that list.
Can triage signals
Marks each signal relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.
2.3

Escalation and acceptance

CaseRoute
Decision beyond the team's authorityGoes to an approver in the app, as an exported memo, or into the board pack.
Change to accepted truthWaits for an Owner.
Pivot of a success criterionTakes effect when someone with authority confirms it.
Any confirmed changeThe session lead is prompted to announce what changed and why.

The full permission matrix per role is available for your review. Ask us

03

Audit trail

What is recorded

Strapivo keeps the reason with the result. A reviewer can follow a change back to its signal, its date and the person who confirmed it.

Signal
A dated observation with a source and a source class, primary or secondary. States are New, Relevant, Watching and Dismissed. Each hand-off of a signal is recorded.
Risk move
Every confirmed move joins the trail with its signal, date and confirmer. The Tracking view shows movement since the last session, over 30 days or since origin. Each risk carries a trend word, Easing, Stable or Worsening, and its time held, such as "top right · 27 days".
Decision
One of five options: Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet. Each decision gets a permanent number such as SDS-2026-014.
Dissent
Recorded by name, with its reason.
Review date
Hold marks the success criterion as reviewed on that date.
Open question
Not yet ends as Parked, with at least one trigger that brings the question back, or as Let go, with a rationale.

06

Questions procurement asks

Answers

Each answer is short. Where the answer is a document, we send it on request.

6.01Can Vivo change our strategy without a person?

No. Vivo proposes and drafts. An Owner accepts changes to accepted truth, a person confirms every risk move, and the session lead confirms every Record entry. See section 01.

6.02Where is our data hosted?

On EU-only infrastructure. For providers and locations, ask us and we send the list.

6.03Which certifications do you hold?

This page lists none. Ask us for the current position and the supporting documents.

6.04Is our data used to train AI models?

Ask us. We answer this in writing for your file.

6.05Does Strapivo record our meetings?

No. Vivo does not listen to the room and does not write minutes. The session lead confirms each Record entry after Vivo reads it back.

6.06Does it connect to our calendar, Teams or task tools?

No. Strapivo sends calendar files by email and exports the plan as Markdown and CSV. Nothing writes back into your other systems.

6.07How do we get our data out?

By export, in the formats in section 05. For deletion at the end of a contract, ask us.

6.08How much of our time does onboarding take?

We do the reading first. Four to five hours, and then you run it.

6.09Can we try it before we sign a contract?

There is no self-serve trial. Start the conversation and we take your reviewers through the product and this sheet.
