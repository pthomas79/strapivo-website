# 106. Planted

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A bulb under the soil, a dated label, a shoot in May
- Direction: A botanical cross-section. Cream sky (#EFE8DA), dark soil (#3B2A22), a papery tan bulb, a green shoot, one Azure flower. A book serif, with one handwriting face for the plant label.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG, full screen. A ground line crosses the screen at 44% of its height. Above it is sky. Below it is soil, with a few lighter flecks. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits on the sky, top left.

- In the soil, right of centre, one bulb: a teardrop about 20vh tall, tan with two darker skin lines, and fine roots below it.
- Beside it, a wooden plant label pushed into the ground with its top above the line. It reads, in handwriting: "Nordic expansion. Back in May."
- Along the top of the sky, seven months in small capitals: NOV DEC JAN FEB MAR APR MAY. A short bar sits under the current one. The word "Illustrative" ends the row.

First screen: November.

Pin the stage for about 280vh. The bar steps from month to month. From December to February a thin white line of frost lies on the ground and the sky cools to #E4E6E3. From March a green shoot grows up from the bulb (animate `stroke-dashoffset`). It breaks the ground line in April. By May it stands 26vh above the ground with two leaves and one Azure flower (#0082E6), drawn as a simple tulip cup.

**The words.**
- h1: "Not yet is not no."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team decides its strategy questions and parks the rest with a way back.
- In the soil, small, in cream: "Parked with a trigger: a date, new evidence, or one person's view."
- In May: "Back on the agenda."
- Last line: "So that it feels like parking, not losing."

**Phone.** The ground line sits at 52%. Bulb and label are centred. Show only the current month, large.

**Still.** May: shoot, leaves and flower above the ground, the label in place.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
