# 71. The ring

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: One ring that changes meaning as you scroll
- Direction: White ground, one Azure ring (#0082E6), ink text. One clean grotesque, large.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** One Azure ring, about 70 percent of the screen height, alone on white. It is the O of the Strapivo logo. The stage is pinned while the visitor scrolls. The ring never leaves. Its meaning changes four times, and each change is a small, exact transformation of the same stroke:

1. Radar. A sweep line turns inside the ring and one dot appears near the edge.
2. Clock. The ring becomes a dial. An arc fills to sixty minutes.
3. Seal. The ring closes around a decision number, SDS-2026-014, set small in mono.
4. Ring again, plain, with the button.

**The words.** One caption per state, eight words at most, set beside or under the ring.
- h1: "Strategy finally has a home."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo keeps a leadership team's strategy in one place and current.
- States: "It watches the world outside." "It calls the session." "You decide. It keeps the record."

**Phone.** The ring is 80 percent of the width, captions sit below it, the stage pins the same way.

**Still.** The plain ring, the headline, the sentence, the button. With no JavaScript, show the four states as four rings down the page.
