# 100. Unfold

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A folded sheet that opens, fold by fold, to fill the screen
- Direction: A soft neutral ground, one sheet of heavy paper with real creases and shadow, ink and one Azure accent. A calm serif or sans, generous in size on the sheet.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A sheet of paper, folded three times into a small rectangle, lies in the middle of the screen with a soft shadow under it. A date is written on the outside: "Agreed in March."

As the visitor scrolls, it opens. Each fold is a hinged panel in CSS 3D that swings flat, with the shading of a crease that has been pressed hard: one side lighter, one darker, a thin line where the paper bent. Three unfoldings, each slower than you expect.

Open, the sheet nearly fills the screen and the creases stay visible. On it is one plain diagram, drawn large: the Ambition at the top, four focus areas under it, and a thin Azure line from one of them to a small ring marked "This week".

**The words.**
- h1: "Your strategy was folded away the day it was finished."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy open and returns to it every week.
- On the open sheet, small: "Opened today."
- Under the sheet: "Open it every week. It stays true."

Name the focus areas plainly and mark the sheet "Illustrative".

**Phone.** The sheet is folded twice, not three times, and opens to fill the width.

**Still.** The sheet fully open, creases showing.
