How a week runs

Something changes. You talk. Maybe you act.

  1. 01 Signal

    The Radar flags a shift

    Strapivo reads over 4,000 sources a day against your focus areas. Vivo matches each signal to one of your goals and gives one line of reason.

  2. 02 Discuss

    The team works it through

    Vivo drafts the agenda a day ahead. In the session, everyone gives a first take, then you think it through with Vivo.

  3. 03 Decide

    You decide, or you park it

    Hold, adapt, pivot, take advantage or not yet. The decision gets a number and joins the record.

Who decides

Vivo proposes. People decide.

Leadership teams want help, not a co-author with the pen. Vivo drafts, asks and reads back. It never changes your strategy on its own.

  • Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory you approved.
  • A pivot takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.
  • One blog post can't raise an alarm. A move into the top right needs a primary source.
  • Strapivo is built on your Business Model Canvas, not a generic prompt with your PDF attached.
  • EU-only infrastructure. GDPR by design. Start with public, non-sensitive material and widen from there.

Getting started

Four to five hours, and then you run it.

  1. We do the reading first: your strategy documents, decks and workshop reports.
  2. You walk into session one with a draft you can correct, not a blank page.
  3. We show you what we got wrong.
  4. You teach it what relevant means.
  5. We run one session together. After that, your team runs them.
