# 11 · The long read

Range: Confident (concept 11 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Executives who read the weekend business press
Colours: Paper with a warm tint #F7F4EE for the article ground, ink, Azure for links and figures. One additional colour: burnt sienna #B4532A for the kicker and rules.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/11-editorial-longread/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

A magazine feature, not a product page. Headline in the register of a business weekly: "The strategy went into a drawer" (find a better one if you can). A standfirst, a dateline, an estimated reading time, then about 1,000 words of well-built argument in a narrow measure with real paragraphs.

The article's arc: the ritual of annual strategy and why it fails quietly; noise against signal; the two failure modes, inertia and thrash; what a continuous practice looks like (the radar, the hour-long session, the five options, the record); what the machine should and should not be allowed to do; an ending that lands on a specific image, not a moral. The product enters in the second half and is described as a reporter would describe it.

Signature element: editorial typography using only Inter and JetBrains Mono. A very large headline, a multi-column opening on wide screens, pull quotes set large (unattributed executive voices from the brief), three numbered figures with captions drawn as spare SVG diagrams (the radar, a session agenda, a record entry), a sidebar box titled 'The five options'. A quiet call to action at the end, styled like an editor's note.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
