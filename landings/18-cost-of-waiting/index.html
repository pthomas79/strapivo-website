Inertia, the first bank

The strategy goes into a drawer.

"We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."What leadership teams tell us

A leadership team agrees a strategy in a workshop. The slides go out. Then the daily work of the quarter takes priority.

The world keeps moving. A competitor cuts a price. A key supplier gets bought. Each change is small, and nobody owns the job of reading it against what the team agreed. The strategy stays as written while the market moves.

Then the quarterly or annual review arrives. By then a small correction has grown into a large one, and large corrections cost money and credibility.

  • Sounds like"Between offsites, nothing moves."
  • Board asksWhat changed since we last met? The honest answer is that nobody checked.
  • Real costHidden. It grows while the team waits.

Thrash, the second bank

Every headline becomes a meeting.

"We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands."What leadership teams tell us

The opposite failure looks like vigilance. News arrives as noise, with no link to what the team said must succeed. One blog post becomes an agenda item. One analyst report reopens a decision closed in spring.

Meetings drift. The loudest voice sets the anchor. Decisions disappear into slides and minutes nobody reads, so six months later nobody can say what was decided, why, or who disagreed. The argument starts again.

The team spends its energy on reactions and still misses the targets it agreed.

  • Sounds like"Didn't we already decide this?"
  • Board asksWhy did the plan change three times? The honest answer is that nobody filtered.
  • Real costVisible. Attention spreads across every goal and none gets enough.

Dynamic Strategy

Steer like a ship. Small, early corrections. Occasionally a change of course.

Strapivo keeps an agreed strategy honest between workshops. The leadership team sets one Ambition, seven to twelve success criteria with a green, yellow or red light, and up to four focus areas. Then it meets on a rhythm and corrects course in small steps.

"We're not changing strategy every week, but we are revising our tactics."

What keeps a team off each bank

Against inertia

A radar that watches for you

The Radar watches the outside world for each focus area. Its search brief is the criterion, its objectives and its risks. Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday. A red-line exception interrupts that schedule.

Trend word and time held

Each risk carries a trend word, Easing, Stable or Worsening, and how long it has held its position. The Tracking view shows movement since the last session like a radar shadow. You see pressure build before anything has to move.

The 14-day nudge

A risk that sits in the top right for 14 days with no decision lands in the session lead's "Needs you" list. Nothing stays dangerous and undiscussed.

"Light still green?"

When two or more focus activities are off track, or a risk on that criterion enters the top right, Vivo asks the question. Vivo never changes the light. A person does.

Light still green? Two focus activities under "Win the mid-market" are off track, and a risk on this criterion entered the top right on Tuesday.

Against thrash

Triage before discussion

Vivo makes a first pass on every signal: which criterion, relevant or not, and one line of reason. A delegated person marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.

One blog post can't raise an alarm

Vivo proposes a risk move as a ghost on the radar with a one-line reason, and a person confirms or rejects it. A move into the top right needs at least one primary source.

Four focus areas, no more

"Fill a few glasses to the brim instead of all twelve a little." Try to flag a fifth and Strapivo asks: "You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?"

Hold is a decision

Choosing Hold marks the criterion as reviewed on that date. Doing nothing on purpose goes on the record and counts.

Parked or let go

"Not yet" asks what's missing. The question is Parked with at least one trigger that brings it back, such as new evidence, a date or the next session. Or it is Let go, with a rationale. In the product's words: "So that it feels like parking, not losing."

Closed questions stay closed

Every decision joins the Record of Decisions with a permanent number, its reason and any dissent by name. When someone reopens the question, the team reads the entry instead of restarting the argument.
