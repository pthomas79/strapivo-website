The problem

Decisions get lost in slides and minutes.

The leadership team agrees a strategy at an offsite. Then markets, suppliers and competitors change. The team meets, argues and decides something, and the decision goes into a slide deck that nobody opens again. Six months later the board asks why the plan changed. Nobody can say who decided, on what evidence, or who warned against it.

Strapivo is the Strategic Design System. Dynamic Strategy is how a leadership team keeps an agreed strategy current between offsites, with small corrections made early and a change of course when the evidence supports one. The Record of Decisions holds every one of those corrections.

  1. i"We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands."
  2. ii"I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings."
  3. iii"We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."
  4. iv"Between offsites, nothing moves."

Notes to the register

  1. 1The read-back

    Before an entry gets its number, Vivo reads it back to the room, with the question, the decision, the rationale and the dissent. The session lead confirms it. The number never changes.

    Makes the No. column possible

  2. 2Sealed first takes

    Each person states a position before the discussion starts. Strapivo reveals all of them at once, so nobody adjusts their view to the first or loudest speaker. The record keeps the takes next to the decision.

    Makes the first takes in each entry possible

  3. 3Dissent by name

    Vivo includes every dissent in the read-back, with the person's name and reason. Months later, anyone can check the warning against what happened.

    Makes the Dissent column possible

  4. 4Parking triggers

    "Not yet" asks what is missing. A parked question needs at least one trigger: new evidence, a person's view, a date or the next session. When a trigger fires, the question returns to the agenda. A question the team lets go carries a rationale.

    Makes "Parked until" possible

  5. 5Escalation and Owner acceptance

    Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver in the app, into an exported memo or into the board pack. A change to accepted truth, such as new wording for a success criterion, waits for an Owner.

    Makes "Awaiting Owner" and "Escalated" possible

How an entry is made

How a dated signal becomes a numbered decision

Each entry in the register comes out of a strategy session in the Strategy Room. The sessions run 60 minutes by default, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, with extra sessions on demand.

  1. 01Watch

    The Radar watches the outside world for each focus area. It files signals as dated observations, each with a source marked primary or secondary.

  2. 02Triage

    Vivo assigns each signal to a success criterion and gives one line of reason. A person on your team marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.

  3. 03Agenda

    Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session from what changed, with old business first. The session lead edits it. The room approves it as the first item.

  4. 04First takes

    On each item, the room sees the evidence and every person gives a sealed position. Strapivo reveals the takes together and the discussion starts.

  5. 05Decide

    The room picks one of five options. Vivo can prompt on the cost of doing nothing, scenarios, alternatives or a black swan.

  6. 06Record

    Vivo reads the entry back, dissent included. The session lead confirms it and the entry gets its number, such as SDS-2026-014.

  7. 07Escalate and announce

    Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver. Strapivo prompts the lead to announce what changed and why.

Key to the Decision column

Five options, each with a defined result

A leadership team has two ways to fail. Inertia means doing nothing while markets change. Thrash means reacting to every headline. Each option leaves the strategy in a known state.

Who holds the pen

Vivo proposes. People decide.

A board needs to know who wrote each entry and on what evidence. Strapivo follows these rules.

  1. Vivo drafts. A person confirms.

    Vivo drafts the agenda, the risk moves and each entry. Nothing enters the register until the session lead confirms the read-back.

  2. Vivo never changes a light.

    When two focus activities go off track, Vivo asks "Light still green?". The person who owns the success criterion answers.

  3. A single blog post cannot raise an alarm.

    A risk moves into the top right of the radar only with at least one primary source, and a person confirms the move. A risk that stays there 14 days without a decision goes to the session lead's Needs you list.

  4. Every recommendation has a source.

    Each recommendation Vivo makes traces back to the strategic memory your team approved. Changes to that accepted truth wait for an Owner.

  5. You can export the record.

    Export the plan and the board pack as Markdown, the plan as CSV, and escalation memos as Markdown or copied text. Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure, with GDPR by design.
