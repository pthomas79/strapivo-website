# 20 · The manifesto

Range: Confident (concept 20 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Leadership teams who want a point of view before a product
Colours: Alternating full-bleed fields of Paper, Navy and Azure. No additional colour.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/20-manifesto/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

Ten statements about how strategy should be run, set as large as the screen allows. Each statement fills most of a viewport on its own colour field, with two or three sentences of plain explanation and the mechanism in Strapivo that makes it true.

Write the ten statements yourself from the brief. They must be specific positions, not platitudes. Material to draw on: strategy needs a home; a strategy nobody opens is a document; watch what you said must succeed and ignore the rest; an agenda should be built from what changed; write your position down before the loudest voice speaks; there are only five answers; "Not yet" is a decision and it needs a trigger; dissent belongs on the record; the machine proposes and people decide; fill a few glasses to the brim. Number them 01 to 10 in mono.

Signature element: typography at poster scale and the rhythm of the colour fields. Headlines up to 12vw at weight 500 with tight tracking. Each field enters with a simple vertical wipe. A small fixed index (01 to 10) lets the visitor jump between statements. The page ends with an eleventh field that is the call to action. Explain the product only through the statements. No separate features section.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
- Finish with the two steps in section 9 of the brief: screenshot and fix, then the unslop pass using `/home/hiasinho/Work/prot-strapivo-dynamic-strategy/.claude/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`.
