An ordinary month

Four things Strapivo does while you run the company.

  1. Week 1 · Mon, Thu

    Vivo reads.Over 4,000 sources a day.

  2. Week 2 · Tuesday

    A supplier letter.Vivo links it to your margin goal.

  3. Week 3 · Thursday

    One hour with your team.The agenda is already drafted.

  4. Week 4 · Any day

    The decision, written down.With who disagreed, and why.

  1. It reads for you.

    Vivo, the AI in Strapivo, reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports to you on Monday and Thursday. It searches for news about the few goals you picked as focus, what you want to achieve under each, and the risks you named.

  2. It tells you what touches your plan.

    Each news item arrives with the goal it affects and one line on why. You, or someone you hand this job to, mark it relevant or not. What you dismiss teaches Vivo what counts as noise.

    If a risk looks bigger, Vivo proposes a new position and a person confirms it. A move into the danger zone needs at least one primary source. A single blog post can't put a risk there.

  3. It sets the agenda for an hour with your team.

    The day before, Vivo drafts the agenda from what changed. Each item says in one line why it is there. You edit it, and the room approves it as the first step.

    Before anyone speaks, each person writes down a first view. The views are revealed together, so nobody adjusts their view to whoever spoke first.

  4. It writes down what you decided and why.

    For each item you choose one of five answers: hold, adapt, pivot, take advantage, or not yet. Vivo reads the decision back, including who disagreed and why. You confirm it, and it gets a permanent number.

    "Not yet" ends in one of two ways. You park it with a trigger that brings it back, or you let it go and write down why.

What Strapivo is not.

  • Not another to-do tool.

    Your team keeps working where it works now. Before each session, the person responsible for each piece of work reports one line on how it is going, and can ask for help.

  • Not a dashboard.

    There are no charts to feed. Each goal has one light, green, yellow or red, and a person sets it. When two focus activities slip, Vivo asks "Light still green?" It never changes the light itself.

  • Not an AI that rewrites your plan.

    Vivo proposes. You decide. Every suggestion traces back to the plan you approved, and a change to the plan waits for an owner to confirm it.
