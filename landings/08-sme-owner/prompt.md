# 08 · For the owner-managed company

Range: Conservative (concept 8 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Managing directors and owners of SMEs with no strategy department
Colours: Paper, ink, Azure. One additional colour: warm ochre #C8963E, used for highlights and the hand-drawn underlines.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/08-sme-owner/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

Written for a managing director who runs strategy on Sunday evenings because nobody else does. Plain words, shorter page, no corporate vocabulary. It does not talk about portfolios or business units. It talks about the plan you agreed in spring and the Tuesday when a customer, a supplier or a competitor changed something.

Structure: a hero that names their situation in one line; "You don't have a strategy department. You have a Monday." (or better); what Strapivo does in an ordinary month, told as four moments (it reads for you, it tells you what touches your plan, it sets the agenda for an hour with your team, it writes down what you decided and why); what it takes to start ("We do the reading first." "Four to five hours, and then you run it." Bring a workshop report, a document, an email or nothing at all); what it is not (not another to-do tool, not a dashboard, not an AI that rewrites your plan).

Signature element: warmth without cuteness. Generous type, short lines, simple hand-drawn-looking SVG underlines and circles in ochre around the key phrases, one simple drawing of a month as four boxes. A page a busy owner reads to the end on a phone.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
