# 60. Not a dashboard

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: COOs and business unit leaders fatigued by OKR tools and BI dashboards
- Colours: House palette, plus muted ash (#E4E8EE) for the two rejected categories and crisp white (#FFFFFF) for the Strategy Room frame.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Definition by subtraction. Many COOs resist new strategy software because they already drown in task boards, OKR spreadsheets and BI dashboards. Strapivo explicitly rejects both: it is not a to-do list and not a KPI dashboard. It never even says KPI, OKR or key result. It uses one plain vocabulary: Ambition, success criteria, objectives, activities and results.

**Hero.** Direct, bold typographic header on Paper with concise copy and primary CTA, sitting directly over a crisp, full-width three-frame architectural contrast visible above the fold. Headline: "Not a to-do list. Not a KPI dashboard. A room for strategic decisions." Two plain sentences explaining what Strapivo is: the Strategic Design System where leadership teams keep an agreed strategy current against live market shifts and decide what changes each week.
Drawing 1 (the hero three-frame contrast):
- Left frame (muted ash, subtly struck label "NOT THIS"): a dense list of 40 tiny task checkboxes. Caption: "Task tools track busyness, not direction."
- Centre frame (crisp white, bold Azure top rule, label "STRAPIVO · STRATEGY ROOM"): four clean levels — Ambition, 4 Focus Criteria with lights, Objectives, Activities & Results — linked to a weekly 60-minute decision.
- Right frame (muted ash, subtly struck label "NOT THIS"): a wall of backward-looking bar charts. Caption: "Dashboards report last quarter after the window closed."

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why KPI dashboards and task trackers miss strategy. Dashboards tell you what already happened inside the building; task trackers manage tickets. Neither tells you when an external shift breaks an assumption in your plan.
2. One plain vocabulary. Drawing 2: a clean vertical-or-horizontal hierarchy strip showing the five exact terms Strapivo uses (Ambition -> Success criteria -> Focus areas, max 4 -> Objectives -> Activities with a result, timeframe and person responsible) with one sharp sentence per level. State clearly that Strapivo never uses KPI, OKR or key result.
3. How execution meets strategy without micromanagement. Contributors report status, one line on the result and an optional ask before the 60-minute session. Leadership decides Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet, and records SDS-2026-014.
4. Human authority, EU-only infrastructure and four-to-five-hour onboarding (bring whatever documents you already have).
5. Closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Direct, crisp, unsentimental. High visual clarity, strong geometric alignment, zero decorative icons. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page. Do not name any real software vendor.
