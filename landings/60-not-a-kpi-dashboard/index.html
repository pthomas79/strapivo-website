Why dashboards and task boards miss strategy

Internal metrics do not warn you when the market shifts outside

COOs already run tools for tickets and financial reports. Neither holds your agreed strategy or checks when an external shift breaks an assumption.

KPI dashboards

They report what already happened inside the building

By the time margin or delivery speed drops on a quarterly chart, the cheaper moment to act has passed. Past numbers confirm damage too late.

Task and OKR trackers

They track completed tickets while the plan goes stale

Teams close dozens of tickets every week on plans whose market premises changed months earlier. Completed tasks never prove the direction still holds.

The Strategy Room

Strapivo connects your approved strategy to live market signals. When an outside shift affects a focus area, Radar flags it before your next weekly session.

One plain vocabulary

Five plain terms for every strategy

Strapivo never uses KPI, OKR or key result. Every team works with five plain terms so priorities stay narrow and every activity has one named owner.

Excluded from Strapivo: KPI OKR Key result
  1. 01 1 per portfolio

    Ambition

    One portfolio target with optional turnover, margin and year. The team reads every trade-off against it.

  2. 02 7 to 12 goals

    Success criteria

    Seven to twelve goals required for the ambition. Each goal has a green, yellow or red light.

  3. 03 Maximum 4

    Focus areas

    At most four criteria under active watch. Add a fifth and Strapivo asks whether you want to dilute focus.

  4. 04 2 to 4 each

    Objectives

    Two to four concrete outcomes sitting under each success criterion.

  5. 05 1 owner each

    Activities

    One action, result, timeframe and person responsible. Status is on track, at risk, off track or done.

Execution meets strategy

Contributors send two lines. Leadership makes the call.

Contributors do not build status decks. Before the 60-minute session they send a status, one result line and an optional ask. Leadership reviews it and records the call.

Hold

Mark the criterion reviewed today and stay on course.

Adapt

Revise objectives or activities without changing the strategy.

Pivot

Draft new criterion wording. An Owner must confirm it.

Take advantage

Add detour activities for an opening without changing the plan.

Not yet

Park the call with a return trigger, or let go with a rationale.
