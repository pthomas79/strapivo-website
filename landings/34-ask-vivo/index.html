01 · Strategic memory

Vivo reads your strategy before it answers

Strategic memory is what is true inside your company, written down and approved by your leadership team. Vivo answers from it. If an answer can't point to it, Vivo says so.

It is hardwired to your business model on the Business Model Canvas. It is not a generic prompt with your PDF attached.

02 · The trail

Every answer cites something you approved

Every recommendation Vivo makes traces back to the strategic memory you approved. You can follow any sentence to its source, its date and the person who accepted it.

  1. No source, no claim.When memory holds nothing on a question, Vivo says so and offers to propose an entry. The Auditor's answer on AI above shows it.
  2. Signals carry a date and a class.Each signal has a source and a source class, primary or secondary. A risk moves into the top right only with at least one primary source, so that one blog post can't raise an alarm.
  3. Dismissed signals count too.When someone dismisses a signal, Vivo learns what your team counts as noise.
  4. Decisions keep their number.Every decision gets a permanent number, such as SDS-2026-014, with its date, its reason and any dissent by name.
03 · Where Vivo stops

Where Vivo's authority ends

Vivo proposes; people decide.

Vivo may

  • Draft the agenda 24 hours before a session, with a one-line reason for every item.The session lead edits it. The room approves it.
  • Propose a new position for a risk as a ghost on the radar.One line of reason, linked to the signal.
  • Ask "Light still green?" when two focus activities go off track.It never changes the light itself.
  • Draft new objectives and activities after an Adapt.Nothing changes until someone accepts the draft.
  • Read back each decision for the Record of Decisions.The session lead confirms it.

Only a person may

  • Set the light on a success criterion.Green, yellow or red.
  • Confirm or reject a risk move.The confirmer's name joins the trail.
  • Confirm a Pivot.Only someone with authority can do it.
  • Accept a change to accepted truth.That waits for an Owner.
  • Approve a decision beyond the team's authority.It goes to an approver in the app, as a memo, or into the board pack.

04 · Dynamic Strategy

The conversation is one step in a session that ends with a decision

Dynamic Strategy keeps an agreed strategy honest between offsites. The team makes small, early corrections and changes course now and then. Each session runs for 60 minutes by default, weekly, fortnightly or monthly.

  1. 01Agenda

    Vivo drafts it from what changed. Old business first, then new business.

  2. 02Evidence

    The signals, risk moves and contributor reports behind each item.

  3. 03First takes

    Each person gives a sealed position before discussion. All are revealed together, so the loudest voice can't anchor the room.

  4. 04 · This pageConversation with Vivo

    Pick a role. Ask about the cost of doing nothing, a scenario, a challenge or a black swan.

  5. 05Decision

    Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet.

  6. 06Record

    Vivo reads the entry back. The session lead confirms it. It gets a number.
