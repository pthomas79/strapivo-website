1

Ambition

Everything is read against one Ambition.

Each portfolio has one Ambition. You can give it measurable targets, such as turnover, margin and a year. Strapivo reads every signal, risk and decision against it. When your team disagrees, it disagrees about the same thing.

Success criteria

Up to twelve success criteria, each with a light.

Success criteria are the goals that must hold for the Ambition to happen. Most teams set seven to twelve. Each one carries today's light, green, yellow or red. When two focus activities fall off track, Vivo, the AI in Strapivo, asks Light still green? It never changes a light itself. A person does.

4

"Fill a few glasses to the brim instead of all twelve a little."

In focus

Four of them get the team's full attention.

You flag up to four success criteria as focus. For each one, the Radar watches the outside world, using the criterion, its objectives and its risks as the search brief. Try to add a fifth and Strapivo asks you to think again. You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?

Hours before

The agenda arrives a day early.

Twenty-four hours before each session, Vivo drafts the agenda from what changed. That covers new signals, risks moving into the danger zone, parked questions that are due back and activities at risk. Old business comes first. Every item carries one line of reason. The session lead edits the draft, and the room approves it as step one.

Minutes

One hour, as often as you need it.

A session lasts 60 minutes by default. It runs weekly, fortnightly or monthly, with extra sessions when something can't wait. Each item follows the same order: evidence, sealed first takes, a conversation with Vivo, a decision. Everyone gives a first take before discussion and all are revealed together, so nobody hears the senior view before giving their own.

Answers

Every item ends in one of five answers.

Hold marks the criterion as reviewed. Adapt has Vivo draft new objectives and activities. Pivot rewrites the criterion, and only someone with authority can confirm it. Take advantage adds activities and leaves the criterion as it is. Not yet parks the question with a trigger that brings it back, such as new evidence or a date.

Days

No risk stays dangerous and undiscussed.

Each risk sits on a radar of impact against likelihood. The top right is the danger zone, and a move there needs at least one primary source, so one blog post can't raise an alarm. If a risk stays in the top right for 14 days with no decision, it lands in the session lead's "Needs you" list.

Record

Each decision gets one permanent number.

At the end of each item, Vivo reads the entry back for the Record of Decisions, including who disagreed and why. The session lead confirms it. Months later, anyone can find what was decided, why, and what happened next. Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver, as a memo or into the board pack.

Hours to start

That is your team's time to get started.

We do the reading first. You walk into session one with a draft you can correct, not a blank page. We show you what we got wrong, and you teach Vivo what relevant means. We run one session with you. After four to five hours, you run it yourselves.

Trust
