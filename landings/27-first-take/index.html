The session

Each agenda item runs the same four steps.

  1. Evidence

    The signals, the risk moves and the reports from the people responsible for activities.

  2. First takes

    Everyone seals a position before discussion. All of them open at once.

  3. Conversation

    The room discusses the split. Vivo can add the cost of waiting, a challenge or scenarios.

  4. Decision

    Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Not yet ends as parked with a trigger, or let go with a reason.

Length

60 minutes by default. Weekly, fortnightly or monthly, plus extra sessions when something cannot wait.

Agenda

Vivo drafts it 24 hours before, from what changed. Old business first. The room approves it as the first step.

Pivots

Vivo can draft new wording for a criterion. It takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.

Dynamic Strategy

The session is one step in a loop that runs between sessions.

A strategy agreed in a workshop usually sits unused until the annual review. Dynamic Strategy keeps it in use between meetings. The team makes small corrections early and changes course when the evidence calls for it.

  1. WatchThe Radar reads over 4,000 sources a day against each focus area.
  2. TriageVivo sorts each signal first. A named person marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure.
  3. MoveVivo proposes a new radar position for a risk. A person confirms or rejects it.
  4. AgendaVivo drafts it from what changed. The session lead edits it.
  5. SessionEvidence, sealed first takes, conversation with Vivo.
  6. DecideOne of five options per item.
  7. RecordA permanent number, the reason, and dissent by name.
  8. EscalateDecisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver. The lead announces what changed and why.
