## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/27-first-take/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

The visitor experiences a sealed first take before being told what one is. The page opens with a case: a fictional company (labelled as an illustrative example), its Ambition in one line, one success criterion, and a fresh signal with a source. Then the question: what should the leadership team do? Five options: Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet. One line of rationale, optional. A button: 'Seal my take.'

Only after sealing does the page reveal the room: five fictional team members' takes shown together, with their one-line rationales, split across the options. The visitor's take sits among them. Then the lesson, in copy: nobody saw anyone else's position first, so the discussion starts from five real views and not from the chair's. Show what happens next in the product: the conversation with Vivo, the decision, and the dissent of those who disagreed, recorded by name with their reason.

The rest of the page explains the session and the loop around it, briefly, and ends with the call to action. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the page says so.

Signature element: the seal and the reveal. Before sealing, the other takes are visibly present but closed, five sealed slips. The reveal turns them over together, once, calmly. With JavaScript off the page shows the case, the five takes and the explanation in order.
