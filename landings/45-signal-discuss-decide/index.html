01 Signal

Something changed. You see it early.

The Radar watches the outside world for each of your focus areas. A focus area is one of the few goals your team said matter most right now.

  • Vivo, the AI in Strapivo, sorts each new signal. It names the goal it touches and gives one line of reason.
  • A person on your team marks what is relevant. What they dismiss teaches Vivo what counts as noise.
  • When a risk grows, Vivo proposes a new place for it on the radar. A person confirms the move or rejects it.
  • A move into the danger zone needs at least one primary source, such as the original report. One blog post can't raise an alarm.

02 Discuss

You talk about it, one item at a time.

Your team meets in a strategy session. It lasts 60 minutes, every week, every two weeks or every month.

  • The agenda arrives 24 hours before. Vivo drafts it from what changed, and every item has one line that says why it is there.
  • The session lead, the person who runs the meeting, edits the agenda. The team approves it as the first step.
  • On each item, everyone writes a first take before anyone speaks. The takes stay sealed, then open together. So the loudest voice can't sway the others first.
  • Then you talk. Vivo can bring evidence, the cost of waiting and other options.

03 Decide

You decide. Hold is a decision too.

Every item ends with one of five decisions. Sometimes you act. Sometimes you don't, and you say so.

Hold
Keep the plan. The goal is marked as reviewed on that date.
Adapt
Change the objectives and activities under the goal. Vivo drafts the changes.
Pivot
Change the goal itself. This needs someone with authority to confirm it.
Take advantage
Add activities to use an opening. The goal stays the same.
Not yet
Park the question with at least one trigger that brings it back, such as new evidence, a date or the next session.

Then

And next week it runs again.

A yearly review is too slow for today's world. A weekly loop lets you correct course early, in small steps.

Record of Decisions
