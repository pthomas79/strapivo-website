# 42. It calls the meeting

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: CEOs and session leads tired of strategy meetings that have nothing to decide
- Colours: House palette, plus a calm green (#1F7A5A) for 'accepted' and 'confirmed' states. Pure white (#FFFFFF) for the one invitation card, which may carry a soft shadow.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

The deck calls Strapivo "the first proactive strategy system to invite you to a strategy session when your attention is needed." This page shows that invitation.

**Hero.** Headline in the spirit of "The strategy system that calls the meeting." The sentence under it explains the mechanism: Strapivo watches the market against the success criteria your leadership team agreed, and when something touches the plan it puts it on the agenda and tells the people who decide why they are needed. The visual is one invitation, drawn in HTML as a clean email card with a calendar file attached. It names the session (Thursday, 09:00, 60 minutes), who drafted it (Vivo, for the session lead) and a short "Why you are invited" block with one concrete reason from a fictional company, for example a supplier risk that moved into the top right on Monday on the strength of one primary source. Label the card "Illustrative example".

**The page.** The rest of the page reads the invitation line by line, one section per line.
1. Why you are invited. The Radar, signals, and a risk move that a person confirmed.
2. What is on the agenda. Vivo drafts it 24 hours before from what changed. Old business comes first. Every item carries a one-line reason. The room approves it as step one.
3. What happens in the room. Evidence, sealed first takes, the conversation with Vivo, one of five options.
4. What you leave with. A numbered entry in the Record of Decisions, with dissent by name.
5. When nothing changed. Be honest here. Sessions run weekly, fortnightly or monthly. A quiet week gives a short agenda. A risk that sits in the danger zone for 14 days with no decision lands in the session lead's "Needs you" list. A red-line exception interrupts the schedule.

Then trust, getting started, and a closing call to action written as the reply to an invitation.

**Truth.** Strapivo sends calendar files by email. Do not claim calendar integration, and do not draw the interface of any real mail or calendar product.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
