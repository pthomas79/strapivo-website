1Why you are invited

A risk moved, and a person confirmed it.

For each focus area, the Radar reads over 4,000 sources a day against the success criterion, its objectives and its risks. Vivo sorts each signal first and gives a one-line reason. A person you delegate decides.

When the evidence shifts, Vivo proposes a new position for the risk. A person confirms or rejects it. A move into the top right needs at least one primary source, so one blog post can't raise an alarm.

2On the agenda

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before.

Old business comes first. Every item carries one line on why it is there.

  • Old businessParked questions whose trigger arrived, items carried forward, activities at risk or off track
  • New businessRelevant signals, risks that entered the top right, asks from the people responsible

Before Thursday's session

  1. Wed 09:00Vivo drafts the agenda from what changed.
  2. WedThe session lead edits it.
  3. Thu 09:00The room approves it as step one.

3In the room

Every item gets the same four steps.

  1. Evidence

    The signals and the risk's trail, with sources and dates.

  2. First takes

    Everyone records a position first. Vivo reveals them together, so nobody adjusts their view to the loudest voice.

  3. Conversation

    Ask Vivo for the cost of doing nothing, a scenario, a challenge or a black swan.

  4. Decision

    The room picks one of five options.

Five options Hold Adapt Pivot Take advantage Not yet

Not yet ends as parked, with a trigger that brings the question back, or as let go, with a reason.

4You leave with

A decision with a number and the dissent.

Vivo reads each entry back before it goes into the Record of Decisions. The session lead confirms it. Dissent goes in by name, with the reason. Months later, anyone can find what was decided and why.

Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver. Strapivo prompts the lead to announce the change.

5Next session

Some weeks, nothing changes.

Sessions run weekly, fortnightly or monthly, plus extra ones when you need them. Strapivo doesn't invent business to fill the hour.

Quiet week

A short agenda

When less changed, the agenda has fewer items. The session still happens.

Stuck risk

Needs you

A risk that sits in the top right for 14 days without a decision lands on the session lead's Needs you list.

Red line

No waiting

A red-line exception interrupts the schedule.

Trust

Vivo proposes. People decide.

  • Only people set a criterion's light.

    When focus activities slip, Vivo asks the team: light still green?

  • Authority stays where it is.

    A pivot takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it. Changes to accepted truth wait for an Owner.

  • Every recommendation has a source.

    It traces back to the strategic memory you approved.

  • EU-only infrastructure.

    GDPR by design. Start with public, non-sensitive material and widen from there.
