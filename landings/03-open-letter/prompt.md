# 03 · The open letter

Range: Conservative (concept 3 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: The one person accountable for the direction of a business
Colours: Paper, ink, Azure. One additional colour: a warm off-white #FBF8F2 for the page ground.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/03-open-letter/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

A letter from Strapivo to the person who owns the strategy. First person plural, signed 'The Strapivo team', no individual's name. Long-form, single column, large type (22 to 24 px body on desktop), nothing but words and one small diagram.

It opens on the moment every strategy owner knows: the offsite ended well, the document was approved, and three months later nobody has opened it. It then explains, in prose, what Strapivo does about it: the radar that watches what you said must succeed, the agenda built from what changed, the sealed first takes, the five options, the numbered record. It is honest about what the AI does not do: "help, not a co-author with the pen."

Signature element: the typography of a letter. A salutation, paragraphs with real rhythm, three or four sentences pulled out at display size in Azure, a sign-off, and a postscript that holds the call to action. A thin progress line at the top shows reading position. No section headings in the usual sense: use small mono markers (I, II, III) instead.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
