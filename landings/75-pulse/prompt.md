# 75. Pulse

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: One trace across the screen: flat, then beating once a week
- Direction: Deep black-green or near black, one glowing trace in a warm white or Azure. Mono for the few labels. No fake hospital interface.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A single horizontal trace, like the line on a monitor, runs the full width of the screen with a bright head that travels left to right. Nothing else.

First screen: the trace is flat. Once, far along, there is a single blip, labelled "Annual review". Then flat again.

As the visitor scrolls, the trace finds a rhythm: one clean beat at even intervals, fifty-two of them where there was one. Label one beat: "Session, 60 minutes." One beat is larger and carries a tag: "SDS-2026-014".

Keep it to the line. No grid of vitals, no numbers panel, no beeping.

**The words.**
- h1: "Give your strategy a pulse."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo brings a leadership team back to its strategy every week, not once a year.
- Under the flat line: "Agreed in spring. Reviewed next spring."
- Under the beat: "Something changed. You talked. You decided."

**Phone.** The trace runs top to bottom or stays horizontal in shorter strips. Choose what looks best.

**Still.** Two traces, one above the other: flat with one blip, and the steady beat.
