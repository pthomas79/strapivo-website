00.AThe problem

Every other function got software. Strategy never did.

00.BWhat it is

Strapivo holds your strategy in one place and keeps it current. We call the method Dynamic Strategy. A leadership team checks the agreed plan against the market every few weeks. It makes small corrections early and changes course when the signals justify it.

This page describes how it works. There are no pictures on it.

01 / 07Structure

Your strategyis in a drawer.

01.A

The leadership team agrees a strategy at a workshop. Then the document goes into a drawer. The market keeps moving, and nobody checks the plan against it until the quarterly review. By then a correction costs more.

Strapivo stores the strategy as a structure your team works in every week. One Ambition sits at the top. Every success criterion carries today's light: green, yellow or red. Every activity has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible.

01.BHierarchy
  • AmbitionOne
  • Success criteria7 to 12
  • Focus areasUp to 4
  • Objectives2 to 4 each
  • ActivitiesPerson, result, date
  • RisksImpact and likelihood
01.CWhat Strapivo says when you add a fifth focus area

The focus warning

"You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?"

Four focus areas is the limit. The Radar, the agenda and the session time all go to those four.

02 / 07Watch and triage

The radar readswhat you will nothave time to read.

02.A

The Radar watches the outside world for each focus area. Its search brief is the success criterion, its objectives and its risks. Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday.

Vivo, the AI in Strapivo, sorts each signal first: which criterion it concerns, whether it is relevant, and one line of reason. A person you delegate marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Signals you dismiss tell Vivo what counts as noise.

Vivo can propose a new position for a risk. A person confirms or rejects it. A move into the top right needs at least one primary source, so that one blog post can't raise an alarm.

03 / 07Agenda

The agenda is builtfrom what changed.

03.A

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before each session. It pulls in new signals, risks entering the top right, parked questions that have come back, items carried forward, activities at risk or off track, and asks from the people responsible.

Old business comes first, then new business. Every item has a one-line reason. The session lead edits the draft. The room approves it as the first item.

04 / 07Session

Write your positiondown first.

04.A

Each agenda item starts with the evidence. Then everyone writes a first take before anyone speaks. The takes stay sealed and open together, so nobody adjusts their view to the loudest voice in the room.

Then the room talks it through with Vivo. Ask it for the cost of doing nothing, a scenario, a challenge, the alternatives or a black swan. A session runs 60 minutes by default, weekly, fortnightly or monthly.

05 / 07Decide

Five options.Pick one.

  1. 05.11

    Hold

    The criterion is marked as reviewed on that date.

  2. 05.22

    Adapt

    Vivo drafts changes to the objectives and activities.

  3. 05.33

    Pivot

    Vivo drafts new wording for the criterion. It takes effect when someone with authority confirms it.

  4. 05.44

    Take advantage

    Detour activities are added. The criterion stays.

  5. 05.55

    Not yet

    The room asks what is missing. The question is parked with a trigger that brings it back, or let go with a written reason.

06 / 07Record

Every decisiongets a number.

06.A

Vivo reads back each entry for the Record of Decisions. The entry names who disagreed and why. The session lead confirms it, and it gets a permanent number.

A year later you can look up what was decided, the reason, who dissented and what happened next. Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver in the app, as an exported memo, or into the board pack.

07 / 07Trust

The AI does nothold the pen.

07.A

Vivo proposes. People decide. Vivo never changes a light. When two or more focus activities are off track, it asks "Light still green?" and the owner answers.

Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory your team approved. Changes to that memory wait for an Owner. Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure and is built for GDPR from the start.
