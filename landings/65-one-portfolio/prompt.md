# 65. One portfolio

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Group CEOs and CSOs running multiple business models under one Ambition
- Colours: House palette, plus deep slate (#1B2531) for the Ambition bar and muted steel blue (#4E6E8E) for individual business model scopes.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Multi-business corporates face a structural headache: one group Ambition sits above several distinct business models, each facing different competitors, suppliers and risks. Unlike concept 09 (a technical entity-relationship data model), this is a sleek, high-altitude executive landing page showing how Strapivo holds one portfolio Ambition across multiple business models, with every risk explicitly scoped either to the whole portfolio or to specific business models, and clear escalation when a call exceeds unit authority.

**Hero.** Clean two-column desktop hero or tight top copy over a full-width architectural **portfolio scope diagram** visible above the fold. Headline: "One group Ambition. Every business model watched on its own terms." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System where multi-unit leadership teams keep portfolio strategy current, scope risks to the whole group or single divisions, and run weekly 60-minute decisions. Primary call to action.
Drawing 1 (the hero portfolio diagram, labelled "Illustrative example"):
- Top full-width Navy bar: `PORTFOLIO AMBITION · €420M revenue at 14% margin by 2029`.
- Three crisp columns below: `Equipment Division`, `Service & Retrofit`, `Digital Fleet`.
- Two clean horizontal risk scope ribbons crossing them: one Azure ribbon spanning all three (`Risk R-01 · EU carbon tariff · Scope: Whole portfolio`) and one steel-blue ribbon scoped strictly under `Service & Retrofit` (`Risk R-04 · Regional parts lead time · Scope: 1 business model`).

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why group strategy fractures across units. Headquarters sees a single annual deck; business unit leaders fight different markets. Risks that affect the whole group get missed, while local shifts get buried.
2. Scoped signals, clear authority. Drawing 2: a clean governance strip showing how each unit runs its focus areas (up to 4), confirms risk moves with primary sources, and escalates decisions beyond local authority directly to an Owner or into the group board pack (Markdown or CSV exports).
3. One shared cadence across the group. 24-hour drafted agendas, 60-minute sessions with sealed first takes, five options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet), and a permanent Record of Decisions (SDS-2026-014) with dissent preserved by name.
4. Trust and onboarding. Vivo proposes; people decide. EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design. Start with one business model or public material, four to five hours to get running.
5. Closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Corporate, architectural, poised. Crisp grid alignment, restrained Navy and Paper contrast, zero clutter. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
