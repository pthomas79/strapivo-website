Dynamic Strategy for multi-model groups

One group Ambition. Every business model watched on its own terms.

Strapivo is the Strategic Design System for leadership teams running multiple business models under one group Ambition. Scope every risk to the whole portfolio or a single business model, and make weekly 60-minute decisions.

01 · The fracture

Why group strategy fractures across business models.

Headquarters reviews one annual deck while business unit leaders face separate markets. Strategy stays annual while conditions shift every week.

01 / Whole-portfolio risks

Shared threats fall between business models

A tariff or supply shock hits several business models at once. Teams see local symptoms, and headquarters sees the full exposure at the quarterly review.

02 / Local signals

Single-model shifts disappear in group updates

News arrives disconnected from what each business model must deliver. Teams miss early warnings, and every new analysis restarts the same argument.

"I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings."

02 · Governance

Scoped signals and clear authority.

Each team sets objectives, activities and up to four focus areas against the group Ambition. Radar monitors external shifts per business model, and teams escalate calls beyond local authority.

03 · Cadence

One shared cadence across the group.

Dynamic Strategy runs a weekly, fortnightly or monthly 60-minute session across headquarters and every business model, using one shared format.

  1. 24 hours before

    Agenda drafted from what changed

    Vivo drafts the agenda from confirmed signals, danger-zone risks, carried-forward items and off-track activities. The session lead edits it, and the room approves it first.

  2. 60-minute session

    Sealed first takes before discussion

    Participants write positions before discussion starts. Strapivo reveals them together so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room.

  3. Five options

    Every item ends in a named decision

    The room picks one of five options. Not yet parks an item with a return trigger or lets it go with a rationale.
