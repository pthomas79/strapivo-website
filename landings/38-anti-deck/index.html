01 · The structure

One ambition. Four focus areas at most.

The team reads everything against one Ambition. Under it sit 7 to 12 success criteria, each with today's light. Up to four of them are focus areas. "Fill a few glasses to the brim instead of all twelve a little."

  • ObjectivesEach success criterion has two to four.
  • ActivitiesEach has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible. Its status is on track, at risk, off track or done.
  • RisksEach sits on a radar of impact against likelihood, scoped to the portfolio or to one business model.

02 · Watch and move

The Radar watches the world for each focus area.

The search brief for each focus area is the criterion, its objectives and its risks. Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday. Vivo makes a first pass on each signal. A person you delegate marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure.

04 · The session

First takes stay sealed until everyone has one.

Each person states a position before the discussion. Vivo reveals all of them together, "without the loudest voice anchoring us." Then the room talks it through with Vivo.

The cost of doing nothingScenariosA challengeAlternativesA black swan

Every item ends in one of five decisions.

Hold

Strapivo marks the success criterion as reviewed on that date. Holding is a decision, and the record shows it.

Adapt

Vivo drafts changes to objectives and activities. The criterion stays as it is. "We're not changing strategy every week, but we are revising our tactics."

Pivot

Vivo drafts new wording for the criterion. It takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.

Take advantage

The team adds detour activities to use an opening. The criterion does not change.

Not yet

Vivo asks what is missing. The item ends as Parked, with at least one trigger that brings it back: new evidence, a person's view, a date or the next session. Or it ends as Let go, with a rationale. "So that it feels like parking, not losing."

05 · The record

Every decision gets a number and keeps its dissent.

Vivo reads back each entry for the Record of Decisions, including dissent by name with its reason. The session lead confirms it. Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver in the app, as an exported memo or into the board pack.

06 · Who decides

The human stays in the chair.

Vivo proposes; people decide. Every recommendation Vivo makes traces back to the strategic memory you approved. Strapivo is hardwired to your Business Model Canvas, so Vivo never works from a generic prompt with your PDF attached.

  • Moves are ghosts until confirmed.Vivo marks a proposed risk position on the radar. A person confirms or rejects it, and the trail keeps the signal, the date and the name.
  • Vivo never changes a light.When two focus activities go off track, Vivo asks "Light still green?" A person answers.
  • Accepted truth waits for an Owner.A Pivot or a change to the strategic memory takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.
  • Your data stays in the EU.Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure, with GDPR by design.
  • We do the reading first.Setup accepts a workshop report, a document, a pasted email or a plain prompt. Vivo proposes where each item belongs and an Owner accepts it. It takes four to five hours, and then you run it.
  • The board still gets its pack.The plan and the board pack export as Markdown, and the plan as CSV. Every decision in it carries its number.
