Why status turns red overnight

Quarterly reporting hides early trouble. When market signals and activity status stay separate from your success criteria, bad news waits for the next offsite.

What leadership teams tell us

  • Green lights stay green by default

    Criteria stay green when nobody tests them against off-track activities.

  • Market signals stay separate from the plan

    Competitor moves arrive in inboxes with no link to your agreed criteria. Small corrections wait for the quarterly review, when changes cost more.

02. The trigger rule

What triggers the question

Each portfolio has 7 to 12 success criteria, and up to four are focus areas. Two conditions put a green light under review.

One blog post cannot raise an alarm, and an old green light cannot hide missed work. People inspect the evidence and decide.

03. The Strategy Room

Reach a recorded decision in 60 minutes

Every flagged criterion goes onto the agenda of your next weekly, fortnightly, or monthly Strategy Room session.

24 hours before

Vivo drafts the agenda

Vivo builds the agenda from flagged criteria, off-track activities, radar moves, and parked items. The room approves it first.

Before the session

Contributors send a short update

Each Contributor reports a status, one line on the result, and an optional ask of leadership. Nobody builds a slide deck.

In the 60-minute session

Leaders submit sealed first takes

Each leader submits a position before discussion. Strapivo reveals every view together so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room.

04. Governance and setup

EU-only infrastructure and setup in four to five hours

Adopt Strapivo without changing your planning method or handing decisions to AI.

Governance and data residency

  • Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure with GDPR by design.

Method-agnostic onboarding

  • Bring a workshop report, document, pasted email, or plain prompt.
