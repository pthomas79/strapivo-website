# 55. Light still green?

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Business unit leaders and COOs tired of status reports that stay green too long
- Colours: House palette, plus status colours used strictly for criterion lights: green (#238056), amber (#C98A00) and red (#C94236).

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Every COO knows the status report that stays green for three quarters and turns red the week before the deadline. In Strapivo, each of a company's 7 to 12 success criteria carries today's light (green, yellow or red). When two or more focus activities slip off track, or a risk enters the top right of the radar, Vivo puts one calm question on the table: "Light still green?" Vivo never flips the light itself. People inspect the evidence and decide.

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero. Left column: headline "Green until the quarter it isn't." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System that watches your agreed strategy against market signals and execution status, and prompts your leadership team to test its assumptions every week. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a large, crisp **status rail** for a fictional business unit (labelled "Illustrative example") showing four focus criteria with clean status lights. On the second green criterion, an Azure prompt flag attaches cleanly: "Light still green? 2 activities off track. Risk R-03 entered top right." with two quiet action tags ("Keep green" / "Review in session").

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why status turns red overnight. Optimism in reporting meets slow quarterly cycles. Without a link between market signals, activity status and success criteria, bad news waits for the next offsite.
2. What triggers the question. Drawing 2: a clean two-input trigger diagram showing the exact two conditions in Strapivo (two or more focus activities off track, OR a confirmed risk entering the top right of the radar) feeding the "Light still green?" prompt — with an explicit note that Vivo never changes the light itself.
3. From question to decision in 60 minutes. How the item lands on the drafted agenda 24 hours before the session, how Contributors send a one-line status and optional ask, and how the team decides (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet) with a numbered record (SDS-2026-014).
4. Governance and setup. EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design, method-agnostic onboarding in four to five hours.
5. Closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Crisp, candid, operational. Clean white cards on Paper, strict typographic alignment, status dots small and disciplined so the page never looks like a noisy traffic-light dashboard. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
