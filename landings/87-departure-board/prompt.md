# 87. Departures

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A split-flap board where every open question flips to an answer
- Direction: Matte black board, off-white or amber characters in flap cells with a centre seam. A mono or condensed face that suits flaps. A plain wall around it.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** One split-flap board fills most of the screen. Build the flaps properly: each character sits in its own cell with a seam, and changes by flipping through a few characters with the top half falling.

Board header: "STRATEGY" and "NEXT SESSION THU 09:00".

Five rows, two columns: the question and its status. On load every status reads "NO DECISION". Then, row by row, the flaps turn:

- SUPPLIER PRICES → ADAPT
- NEW ENTRANT → HOLD
- NORDIC EXPANSION → NOT YET
- PARTNER OFFER → TAKE ADVANTAGE
- CORE SEGMENT → PIVOT

The cycle restarts after a long pause. No sound.

**The words.** The board is most of the copy.
- h1: "No strategic question stays on 'no decision'."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team brings what changed, decides, and keeps the record.
- Small, under the board: "Five possible answers. One per question."

**Phone.** Stack question over status in each row, with flaps large enough to read. Shorten nothing.

**Still.** The board with all five answers showing.
