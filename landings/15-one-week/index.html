People

Vivo
The Radar reads over 4,000 sources a day. It searches for each focus area using the criterion, its objectives and its risks as the brief. Vivo then makes a first pass on every signal: which success criterion it touches, whether it looks relevant, and one line of reason.
TuesdayTriage

One person sorts the signals.

People
The person delegated to triage marks each signal relevant, irrelevant or unsure. It takes minutes, because Vivo has already proposed a criterion and a reason for each one.
Vivo
Learns from every dismissed signal what counts as noise. Flags a relevant signal that matches no known risk as a possible new risk. Draws a proposed risk move as a ghost on the radar, with a one-line reason, for a person to confirm or reject.
Any dayInterruption

The red-line exception does not wait for Monday.

The radar reports on Monday and Thursday. A red-line exception breaks that schedule. It does not wait for the next report.

A risk can enter the top right of the radar, the danger zone, only with at least one primary source. One blog post cannot raise an alarm. If the matter can't wait for the next session, the session lead calls an extra one.

Wednesday24 hours before

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session.

People
Contributors report a status for each activity they own, one line on the result and an optional ask of leadership. Team members submit their first take on each open question. First takes stay sealed until the session.
Vivo
Builds the agenda from what changed since the last session. Old business comes first, then new business. Each item carries a one-line reason. The session lead edits the draft.
ThursdayRadar report · Session

The team meets for 60 minutes and decides.

People
The room approves the agenda as step one. For each item the team reviews the evidence, reveals the sealed first takes together, works through it with Vivo and then decides. There are five options: Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage and Not yet. Not yet ends as Parked, with a trigger that brings it back, or as Let go, with a rationale.
Vivo
When asked, Vivo sets out the cost of doing nothing, runs scenarios, argues the other side or names a black swan. It does not decide. The second radar report also lands today, and its signals go into next Tuesday's triage.
FridayRecord

The decision goes on record, with its dissent.

People
The session lead confirms each entry. Changes to accepted truth wait for an Owner. Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver in the app, as a memo or into the board pack. The lead then announces what changed and why.
Vivo
Reads back each entry for the Record of Decisions, including dissent by name with its reason. After an Adapt, Vivo drafts the changes to objectives and activities. Each decision gets a permanent number.

What the week runs on

Strapivo reads every signal against the strategy your team agreed.

Each signal, risk and decision in the week belongs to one part of that strategy. Strapivo holds the structure, and the Business Model Canvas sits underneath it.

  1. 01AmbitionOne per portfolio, with optional targets such as turnover, margin and a year. Everything is read against it.
  2. 02Success criteriaUsually 7 to 12 goals, each with today's light: green, yellow or red.
  3. 03Focus areasUp to four success criteria. Try to add a fifth and Vivo asks: You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?
  4. 04Objectives and activitiesTwo to four objectives under each criterion. Each activity has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible.
  5. 05RisksEach risk has a position on a radar of impact against likelihood, and a trend: easing, stable or worsening.

Setup accepts a workshop report, a document, a pasted email or a plain prompt. Vivo proposes where each item belongs, for example "This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down?" An Owner accepts or rejects it.

Who decides

Vivo proposes. People decide.

At each step of the week, a person confirms what Vivo drafts. Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory you approved.

  • LightsVivo never changes a light. When two or more focus activities are off track, or a risk enters the top right, it asks: "Light still green?"
  • RisksA risk moves when a person confirms it. Each confirmed move joins the trail with its signal, date and confirmer.
  • PivotNew wording waits for authority. Vivo drafts the revised criterion. It takes effect when someone with authority confirms it.
  • RecordDissent stays on the record. Each entry names who disagreed and why.
  • ExportsYou can export the plan. The plan and the board pack export as Markdown, and the plan exports as CSV.
  • HostingEU-only infrastructure. GDPR by design.
