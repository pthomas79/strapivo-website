What slides cannot do

A deck records the strategy. Then nobody opens it.

  1. Slides don't notice when the market moves.

    The world changes in March. The team finds out at the annual review in December, when a correction costs far more.

  2. Slides don't remember why.

    A year later, nobody can find what was decided, who disagreed, or what happened next.

  3. Nobody opens them.

    The strategy gets agreed at the offsite and goes into a drawer. Day-to-day work carries on without it.
