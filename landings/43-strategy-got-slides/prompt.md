# 43. Strategy got slides

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Leadership teams and heads of strategy who have never thought of strategy as needing its own software
- Colours: House palette, plus graphite greys (#3A424B, #8A929B) for the other functions and a faded yellow (#F3E6B3) for the slide deck thumbnail.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

The category page. The deck's sharpest slide says finance got its software, sales got its software, and strategy got slides.

**Hero.** Headline: "Finance got a ledger. Sales got a pipeline. Strategy got slides." The sentence under it: Strapivo gives strategy what the other functions have had for decades, one place where it lives, stays current and gets used. The visual is a row of four small, equally sized cards, each a simple drawing of one function's system: a ledger with rows, a pipeline with stages, a service queue with tickets, and for strategy a slide deck thumbnail with a file name and a "last opened" date long ago. Three cards look alive. The fourth looks abandoned. Name no real software product.

**The page.**
1. What slides cannot do. They do not notice when the market moves, they do not remember why a decision was made, and nobody opens them. Use two of the executive quotes.
2. What every function's system has, and strategy's version of it. This is the centre of the page: one comparison table, drawn large and readable on a phone. Rows such as: a current state (success criteria with a green, yellow or red light), a feed of what changed (signals read against each focus area), a rhythm (a 60-minute session), a record (numbered decisions), and controls (a person confirms every change).
3. The fourth card, redrawn. The same card frame as in the hero, now showing Strapivo's version: a few criteria with lights, the next session, the last decision number.
4. How a week runs, in three beats: signal, discuss, decide.
5. Trust, getting started, closing call to action.

**Look.** Light, typographic and exact. The comparison is the design. No decoration that does not help the comparison.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
