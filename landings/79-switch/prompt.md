# 79. The switch

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: One large switch that turns the whole page from off to on
- Direction: Off: flat greys, no colour. On: your own palette, warm and alive. The switch itself is beautifully made, with real depth. Any typeface.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A single large switch in the middle of the screen. It is the only control on the page and it changes everything.

**Off** is how the page loads. Everything is grey and still. The headline reads "Strategy: off". Three small frozen details sit around the switch: a date in the past, a clock hand that does not move, a dot that does not blink.

**On.** The whole page changes in about half a second. Colour arrives, the date becomes today, the hand moves, the dot becomes a ring that pulses slowly. The headline reads "Strategy: on".

Build the switch as a real checkbox or `role="switch"` button so it works by keyboard, and drive the page state from CSS so it works without JavaScript. Add a small prompt at first: "Turn it on."

**The words.**
- h1 off: "Strategy: off". Under it: "Agreed in March. Not opened since."
- h1 on: "Strategy: on". Under it: "Watched daily. Discussed weekly. Decided on the record."
- Plain sentence, visible in both states: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy running between offsites.

The page is one screen, or one screen and a short ending.

**Phone.** The switch is thumb-sized and central.

**Still.** The off state. It has to be handsome in grey.
