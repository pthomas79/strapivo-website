# 81. The line map

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A transit map where lines are focus areas and interchanges are sessions
- Direction: White ground, four strong transit colours plus black. A humanist sans made for signage. Thick lines, 45 and 90 degree angles only, round stations.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A transit map that fills the screen and draws itself on load.

- Four coloured lines are four focus areas. Name them plainly, for example "Service revenue", "Northern markets", "Supply", "People". Mark the map "Illustrative".
- All four run to one terminus: "Ambition 2030".
- Where lines meet, an interchange ring: a session.
- Stations carry the answers: "Hold", "Adapt", "Pivot".
- "Take advantage" is a short spur off one line that rejoins it.
- "Not yet" is a siding that ends in a dashed stub, with a small note: "Returns in May."
- One pulsing marker: "You are here."

Keep the map sparse. About twelve stations in all. It should read as a real network map at a glance and reward a second look.

**The words.** The station names are most of the copy.
- h1: "Know where your strategy is, and which stop comes next."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and decides, week by week, how to move it.

**Phone.** Redraw the map vertically, top to bottom, with the terminus at the bottom. Do not shrink the desktop map.

**Still.** The finished map.
