01 · The governance problem

Why European leadership teams hesitate on strategy AI

Strategy holds a company's most sensitive thinking: margin targets, pricing assumptions, market bets, and where the business chooses not to play. Pasting that material into consumer AI tools or opaque platforms is unacceptable to European boards, owners, and legal counsel.

Leaving strategy in workshop slides carries its own cost. Markets move between offsites while external news arrives disconnected from agreed priorities. Teams then restart the same argument whenever a new report lands.

First risk

Opaque consumer AI tools

Consumer AI tools give generic answers detached from your business model. They carry unclear data boundaries and rewrite strategic text without authority.

Second risk

Annual slides left in a drawer

Teams agree a deck once a year and review it too late for small course corrections. No clear record shows the board how strategy moved between meetings.

03 · Weekly discipline

How Dynamic Strategy runs each week

Leadership teams run Dynamic Strategy on a weekly, fortnightly, or monthly cadence. Vivo prepares the evidence and drafts the agenda. Named people debate the trade-offs and confirm every entry.

04 · Architectural guarantees

Four non-negotiable rules

  1. Rule 01

    EU-only infrastructure

    Your strategic memory and Strategy Room run on European Union infrastructure built for European corporate governance.

  2. Rule 02

    GDPR by design

    Strapivo enforces GDPR by design through role boundaries, data minimisation, and a public-material start.

  3. Rule 03

    Humans decide, humans validate

    Vivo proposes agendas, risk moves, and revised wording. Only named people confirm updates to accepted truth or record a decision.

  4. Rule 04

    Every recommendation traces back to approved strategic memory

    Each proposal points to the strategic memory your team approved and the dated sources it reviewed, so boards can audit every decision.
