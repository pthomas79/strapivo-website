# 68. EU-only, human-approved

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: European SME owners, corporate boards and risk-conscious executives
- Colours: House palette, plus deep Prussian navy (#0D1B2A) for governance borders and muted steel (#5A6B7D) for audit trail connectors.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Unlike concept 07 (which read like an IT security datasheet), this is an executive-grade main landing page for European SMEs and corporates whose first question about AI in strategy is sovereignty and governance. Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure with GDPR by design, lets teams start with public and non-sensitive material and widen from there, traces every recommendation back to approved strategic memory, and guarantees that humans validate and decide every step.

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero on crisp Paper. Left column: headline "Strategy software built for European governance. Humans approve every move." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System where leadership teams keep an agreed strategy current against live market shifts on EU-only infrastructure, with every AI proposal checked and decided by named people. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a vertical architectural **provenance chain** (labelled "Illustrative example") of four linked blocks:
1. `Approved strategic memory` (EU-only infrastructure · GDPR by design)
2. `Dated primary signal` (Source class verified · Triaged by Member)
3. `Human confirmation gate` (Owner confirms changes to accepted truth)
4. `Permanent record` (`SDS-2026-014` · Rationale + dissent by name)

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why European leadership teams hesitate on strategy AI. Strategy holds a company's most sensitive thinking. Pasting it into consumer AI tools or opaque platforms is unacceptable to boards, owners and legal counsel.
2. Start safely, widen on your terms. Highlight the onboarding data boundary from the brief: start with public and non-sensitive material, test how Dynamic Strategy works over four to five hours, and widen only when your team trusts the system.
3. How Dynamic Strategy runs each week. Drawing 2: a clean, restrained three-beat flow (Signal on focus areas -> 60-minute session with sealed first takes -> Five options recorded under permanent numbers) with clear role boundaries (Owner, Admin, Member, Contributor) and exportable Markdown board packs and CSV plans.
4. Four non-negotiable rules. Short, four-row grid: EU-only infrastructure; GDPR by design; Humans decide, humans validate; Every recommendation traces back to approved strategic memory.
5. Getting started and closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Conservative, institutional European minimalism. Crisp Prussian-navy hairlines, calm Paper ground, impeccable typography, zero sales hype. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
