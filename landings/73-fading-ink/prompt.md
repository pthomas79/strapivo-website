# 73. Fading ink

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A strategy statement whose ink fades as the days pass
- Direction: Warm paper that yellows, black ink, a high-contrast serif at very large size. A small mono counter.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A sheet of paper fills the screen. On it, one strategy statement in a large serif, for example: "By 2030 we are the first choice for industrial pumps in Northern Europe." Mark it "Illustrative" in small type. Above it, a counter: "Agreed 0 days ago".

As the visitor scrolls, the counter climbs to 214 and the statement ages. The ink loses contrast, the edges soften, the paper yellows and picks up grain. At the bottom of the fade the sentence is almost gone and the page is nearly empty. That emptiness is the point.

Then one line: "Reviewed today." The ink returns, sharp and black, the counter reads 0, and a thin Azure ring appears beside the date.

**The words.**
- h1: "A strategy fades when nobody opens it."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo brings a leadership team back to its strategy every week, so it stays true.
- Mid-fade, small: "Nothing was decided. It just got old."

**Phone.** The statement breaks over more lines and stays large. Same fade.

**Still.** The statement set once, with the ink fading from full black at the first word to almost nothing at the last.
