Dynamic Strategy · 14-day escalation rule

Nothing stays dangerous and undiscussed.

Strapivo is the Strategic Design System that tracks live risks against your agreed strategy. Unresolved threats in the danger zone move to a weekly 60-minute leadership decision.

01 / The quiet failure mode

"I know there's a threat coming. I don't know what it costs us to wait."

What leadership teams tell us.

Known risks wait in the inbox.

Leadership teams agree on a strategy in a workshop and leave it in a drawer. Early warnings sit in email threads unconnected to the success criteria the team agreed.

Each unreviewed week narrows your options. Strapivo puts a 14-day clock on every confirmed danger-zone risk so the team reviews it before options close.

Inertia

Threats linger in email threads until the quarterly review, after cheap corrections have passed.

Thrash

Meetings react to the loudest headline without checking it against the business model.

02 / How a risk earns the danger zone

One blog post cannot raise an alarm.

The Radar monitors outside signals for each focus area. When evidence shifts, Vivo proposes a ghost move on the radar with a one-line reason.

  1. 01

    Primary source required

    Entering the top right requires at least one primary source. Commentary stays watching or dismissed.

  2. 02

    Named confirmation

    Vivo proposes; people decide. A person confirms or rejects the move. Dismissed signals train Vivo on noise.

  3. 03

    Trend and clock

    Every confirmed move logs its signal, date, confirmer, days held and trend: Easing, Stable or Worsening.

03 / When it hits Needs you

Sixty minutes, one decision, one record.

At day 14 with no decision, the risk lands in the session lead's Needs you list for the next 60-minute session.

  1. 24 hours ahead

    Vivo drafts the agenda

    Vivo puts old business first, then top-right risks, new signals, returning parked items and off-track activities. Each carries a one-line reason. The lead edits it; the room approves it first.

  2. Before debate

    Sealed first takes

    Leaders submit positions privately before discussion. All takes reveal together so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room.

  3. In the room

    Test the cost of waiting

    Ask Vivo to test scenarios, challenge assumptions or weigh the cost of doing nothing against your approved strategy.

  4. Close the loop

    Record the decision and dissent

    Choose Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Vivo reads back the decision and named dissent under a permanent number such as SDS-2026-014.

04 / Authority and getting started

The human stays in the chair.

  • Human authority

    Humans validate every change

    Vivo proposes; people decide. It never changes a status light, moves a risk or edits accepted strategy without confirmation.

  • EU infrastructure

    GDPR by design

    Strapivo runs on European Union infrastructure. Start with public and non-sensitive material, then widen scope at your pace.

  • Exports

    Take the record to the board

    Export the plan as Markdown or CSV, pull escalation memos, or export a board pack showing what moved.

Onboarding · Four to five hours

We do the reading first.

  1. 01
    Bring existing material

    Share a workshop report, document, email or prompt. Session one starts with a draft to correct.

  2. 02
    Show what we got wrong

    Sharpen your ambition, success criteria, up to four focus areas and active risks.

  3. 03
    Teach the radar

    Dismiss noise and confirm primary sources so signals match your business model.

  4. 04
    Run one session together

    Four to five hours total, then your leadership team runs the Strategy Room.
