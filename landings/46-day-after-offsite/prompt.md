# 46. The day after the offsite

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Leadership teams that have just agreed a strategy, or are about to, often with outside advisers
- Colours: House palette, plus sticky-note yellow (#F6E58D) and a soft coral (#F2A48B) for the workshop wall. A warm amber (#C98A00) for emphasis.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

This page meets the buyer at one moment: the strategy work is done, the slides are good, and Monday is coming.

**Hero.** Headline in the spirit of "The offsite went well. What happens on Monday?" The sentence under it: Strapivo takes the strategy your team just agreed and keeps it in use. It is watched against the market, discussed for an hour a week and corrected early. The visual shows a handover. On the left is the workshop wall, a loose field of sticky notes and a flip chart, drawn simply. On the right the same content sits in Strapivo's structure: one Ambition, a column of success criteria with lights, four of them marked as focus. A few thin lines connect notes to the place they ended up.

**The page.**
1. "The results were fine." What leadership teams tell Strapivo after a strategy project: the strategy is good and the slides exist. What is missing is commitment, ownership and the feeling that any of it moves. Present this as what teams say. Name no company and no consultancy.
2. Three things happen after you set the direction. Three paired rows, "Today" against "With Strapivo", using the deck's wording.
   - "We finish the strategy, and then we forget it." It is on the record, and Vivo brings it back up.
   - "They built it, so nobody owns it." Your team decides. Every call keeps its name and its reasoning.
   - "Between offsites, nothing moves." Vivo works while you do, and reports what touched the plan.
3. Bring what you already have. Setup accepts a workshop report, a document, a pasted email or a plain prompt. Vivo proposes where each item belongs ("This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down?") and an Owner accepts. Strapivo works with any method.
4. The first weeks after. The Radar starts reading against your focus areas, the first session runs, the first decision gets a number.
5. Trust, getting started, closing call to action.

**Tone.** Respectful of the work the team has just done. The strategy is not the problem. What happens to it afterwards is.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
