How it starts

The first weeks after the offsite.

  1. Before session one

    The Radar starts reading

    It watches the market for each focus area. Its search brief is the success criterion with its objectives and risks, so the signals it finds concern your plan.

  2. Session one

    The first session runs

    Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours ahead from what changed. Your session lead edits it. The session lasts 60 minutes, and after that you meet weekly, fortnightly or monthly.

  3. After session one

    The first decision gets a number

    The team chooses Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Vivo writes each decision into the Record of Decisions with its reasons.
