The problem

Constant alerts ruin strategic judgement.

Market news arrives every hour as noise, disconnected from what your leadership team agreed must succeed. When every channel claims urgency, executives mute the feed and return to the annual offsite.

Between offsites, nothing moves. Strategy sits unread until the yearly review, when course correction costs more.

Failure mode 01

Inertia

The team reviews strategy once a year while competitors and regulators move between meetings.

Failure mode 02

Thrash

The team reacts to every headline. Priorities shift before activities finish, and the same argument repeats.

Dynamic Strategy avoids both. Keep strategy steady while revising tactics on a calm schedule.

How the rhythm works

Over 4,000 sources a day, reduced to two calm briefings.

Strapivo holds your business model, ambition, and up to four focus areas. Vivo reads the market against that strategic memory and stays silent when nothing affects it.

Step 02 Exception only

Red-line interruption

One blog post cannot raise an alarm. A risk enters the top right danger zone only with a primary source and human confirmation. That threshold interrupts the schedule.

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours ahead from what changed. Old business comes first, every item carries a one-line reason, and the session lead edits it before the room meets.

One focused hour to weigh evidence and record the call.

Each item follows one order: evidence, sealed first takes, discussion with Vivo, and a decision. Nobody anchors the room by speaking first.
