Why watch the model

The strategy goes into a drawer. The market keeps moving.

"Almost like an antivirus for your business model."

How our founders describe it

A leadership team agrees its strategy in a workshop. Then the news arrives one article at a time, and nobody connects it to what the team said must succeed. The gap shows up at the quarterly or annual review, when a correction costs the most.

Strapivo watches the outside world for anything that touches your model and flags it on the block it affects. It does not change the model. Your people do. That is Dynamic Strategy: small, early corrections, and occasionally a change of course.

What leadership teams tell us
  • "We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."
  • "Between offsites, nothing moves."
  • "I know there's a threat coming. I don't know what it costs us to wait."
  • "We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands."
The backbone

Hardwired to the business model. Not a generic prompt with your PDF attached.

Every item in Strapivo links to a block of your Business Model Canvas. A success criterion protects a block. Objectives sit under the criterion, activities under the objectives, and risks carry a scope: the whole portfolio, or named business models. So Vivo can match a signal about compressors to the partner note, the criterion that depends on it and the person responsible for the activity.

  1. Canvas noteSingle compressor supplier
  2. Success criterionDelivery reliability, light green
  3. ObjectiveShip 95 of every 100 orders in the promised week
  4. ActivityQualify a second compressor by June. Head of purchasing is responsible. At risk
  5. RiskCompressor supply interruption, scope: residential models

Illustrative example

Strategic memory

What's true inside your company, approved by people.

We do the reading first. Setup takes four to five hours of your team's time, and then you run it.

Ambition

One per portfolio, with optional targets for turnover, margin and a year. Vivo reads everything against it.

Success criteria

Usually seven to twelve, each with today's light: green, yellow or red.

Focus areas

Up to four criteria. Try a fifth and Strapivo asks: "You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?"

Objectives, activities, risks

Each activity has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible. Each risk has a place on the radar.

Vivo, during setup"This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down?" Bring a workshop report, a document, a pasted email or a plain prompt. Vivo proposes where each item belongs. An Owner accepts it.

You write the search brief by writing your strategy.

  • Needs youA risk that sits in the top right for 14 days with no decision lands in the session lead's Needs you list.

The session

The scan ends in a decision with a number.

The Strategy Room meets for 60 minutes, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, with extra sessions when needed.

  1. Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before

    It works from what changed: new signals, risks entering the top right, activities off track and asks from the people responsible. Each item carries a one-line reason. The room approves the agenda first.

  2. Sealed first takes

    Each person records a position before discussion. The takes are revealed together, so the loudest voice doesn't anchor the room.

  3. Five ways to decide

    Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Not yet means parked with a trigger that brings it back, or let go with a reason.

Who decides

Vivo proposes. People decide.

  • Every move is confirmed

    Vivo shows a risk move as a ghost with a reason. It joins the trail only when a person confirms it, with the signal, the date and the confirmer's name.

  • People set the lights

    Vivo can ask "Light still green?" It cannot change the light.

  • Traceable answers

    Every recommendation Vivo makes traces back to the strategic memory you approved.

  • EU-only infrastructure

    GDPR by design. Your strategic memory stays on infrastructure inside the EU.
