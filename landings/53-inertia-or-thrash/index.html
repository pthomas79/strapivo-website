Two expensive habits

Annual reviews move too late. Headline meetings move too often.

Leadership teams know an annual review is too slow. Many resist continuous review because they expect weekly disruption.

Inertia

Annual cycle

"Between offsites, nothing moves."

The team agrees a strategy in a workshop and files the slides away. Market changes go unconnected to the approved strategy. By the next annual review, small shifts require a costly reset.

Thrash

Headline cycle

Every headline starts an unrecorded debate.

Each new report restarts the same argument. The loudest voice sets the room's view. Tactics swing week to week, and nobody can find what the team decided three months later.

"We are not changing strategy every week, but we are revising our tactics." Strapivo holds leadership teams between inertia and thrash.

Protecting stability

How Strapivo filters noise before it reaches the room

Strapivo blocks overreaction with three rules between market noise and any change to your strategy.

Stability filter Three rules against thrash
Filter 01 Scope

Focus limit of up to 4 success criteria

A team flags up to four success criteria as focus areas. If someone adds a fifth, Strapivo warns that extra focus creates noise.

Filter 02 Evidence

Primary source required for danger-zone moves

Vivo proposes a new risk position on the radar. A person confirms or rejects it. Moving a risk into the top right danger zone requires at least one primary source.

Filter 03 Room

Sealed first takes before open discussion

Before debate begins, each executive submits an independent position. All takes appear together so the loudest voice cannot set the room's view.

Preventing drift

One 60-minute session. Five disciplined options.

The Radar collects market signals for your focus areas. Vivo drafts an agenda 24 hours before each weekly, fortnightly or monthly session. Every item carries a one-line reason and ends in one of five options.
