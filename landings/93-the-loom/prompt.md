# 93. The loom

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A cloth woven one row at a time, with one flaw caught and mended
- Direction: Natural linen ground, threads in indigo, madder red, ochre and one Azure. Over and under shading so it reads as textile. A humanist serif or sans, small.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A length of cloth grows down the page as the visitor scrolls. Vertical warp threads hang from the top edge. Four of them are thick and coloured: the focus areas. Each step of scrolling passes one weft row across. One row is one week.

A pattern builds slowly, clear only after many rows. Part of the way down, one row goes wrong: a loose loop, a thread in the wrong colour. In the next row it is caught, and a small visible mend remains. Further down the pattern carries on, unbroken.

At the bottom: the selvedge, a fringe, and a small woven label with a ring.

Draw each crossing with a little shadow so the threads go over and under. From a distance it should look like a photograph of cloth. Canvas or SVG.

**The words.**
- h1: "A strategy is made one row at a time."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and works on it every week.
- At the flaw: "Something slipped."
- At the mend: "Caught the week after, not the year after."
- Low on the cloth: "The pattern only shows over time."

**Phone.** A narrower cloth with the same threads. Text sits beside the cloth on desktop and on plain bands between sections on a phone.

**Still.** The finished cloth, flaw and mend included.
