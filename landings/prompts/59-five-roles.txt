# 59. Five roles in the room

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Chief strategy officers and strategy teams evaluating what Vivo actually does
- Colours: House palette, plus one muted ink-tint surface (#ECF1F6) for the active role panel and subtle steel borders (#D5DCE4).

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

When senior strategists hear "AI in the strategy room", they imagine a chat box giving vague opinions. Strapivo's AI, Vivo, has five defined jobs in the room: Auditor ("Where did this number come from?"), Advisor ("Does this fit our principles?"), Scout ("What moved against the plan?"), Colleague ("Think this through with me."), and Researcher. Plus Lenses that apply the perspective of a specific strategy practitioner to a question.

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero. Left column: headline "Five jobs in the strategy room. None of them is deciding for you." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System where your leadership team keeps its agreed strategy current, backed by Vivo, an AI that takes five disciplined roles anchored to your strategic memory. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a crisp, interactive **five-role index card** (works via keyboard tabs with JS, and displays all five roles cleanly stacked with JS off — never hidden at opacity 0). Each role displays its name, its exact core question, and a one-line illustrative output grounded in strategic memory (labelled "Illustrative example").

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why a general chatbot fails in a strategy session. A generic prompt flatters the room or invents context. Vivo knows your Ambition, your guardrails, your Business Model Canvas and every numbered decision already on the record.
2. The five roles, side by side. Five clean, concise horizontal entries (Auditor, Advisor, Scout, Colleague, Researcher) showing the exact prompt Vivo answers and the artefact it checks. Keep each entry to two tight sentences.
3. Plus strategic Lenses and hard questions. Explain Lenses (applying a specific strategy practitioner's perspective) and show the two real sample prompts from the brief ("Will my business survive AI?" and "Roast my business model") inside a clean, understated prompt specimen block (Drawing 2).
4. How Vivo fits the 60-minute weekly loop. Radar signals, 24-hour drafted agenda, sealed first takes, five decision options, permanent Record of Decisions (SDS-2026-014). Vivo proposes; people decide.
5. Trust (EU-only, GDPR by design, traceable recommendations), getting started (four to five hours) and closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Analytical, poised, clear. Crisp tabular typography, mono role tags, calm Paper and Navy contrast. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
