# 53. Between inertia and thrash

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: CEOs and CSOs balancing strategic stability with speed
- Colours: House palette, plus rust (#B84735) for the thrash band and warm stone (#E7E4DC) for the inertia band.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Built on the two failure modes in the brief: inertia (doing nothing until the annual review) and thrash (reacting to every headline). "We are not changing strategy every week, but we are revising our tactics." Most executives resist continuous strategy because they fear chasing noise. Strapivo holds the Ambition steady while making small, evidence-led tactical adjustments.

**Hero.** Tight editorial headline at top left paired with concise explanation and CTA on top right, sitting directly above a full-width, crisp SVG trajectory chart visible above the fold. Headline: "Hold the strategy steady. Revise the tactics early." Subtitle explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System that keeps a leadership team out of both annual inertia and weekly thrash.
Drawing 1 (the hero chart): a clean 12-month horizontal chart split into three bands:
- Top band (subtle rust tint), labelled "Thrash": an erratic jagged line reacting to every news spike.
- Middle band (clean sky/Azure corridor), labelled "Dynamic Strategy": a steady Ambition line with four small, calm tactical bends marked with decision IDs (SDS-2026-004, 009, 014, 019).
- Bottom band (warm stone), labelled "Inertia": a flat line from January to November followed by one sharp late drop at the annual review.

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Two expensive habits. Two concise side-by-side columns contrasting inertia ("Between offsites, nothing moves") and thrash (every headline triggers an unrecorded debate).
2. How Strapivo protects stability. Drawing 2: a clear diagram of the filter that prevents thrash: focus limit of up to 4 success criteria, primary-source rule for danger-zone moves, and sealed first takes before discussion.
3. How Strapivo prevents drift. The 60-minute session with five disciplined options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet). Emphasise that Hold is a recorded decision and Adapt changes tactics without rewriting the Ambition.
4. Human control, trust and onboarding. Vivo proposes, people decide, EU-only infrastructure, four to five hours to start.
5. Closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Confident, senior, analytical. Think Financial Times graphic precision meets Swiss typography. Crisp horizontal rules, calm geometry, generous margins. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
