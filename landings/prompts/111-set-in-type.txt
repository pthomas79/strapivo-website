# 111. Set in type

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: Two lines of metal type, and one word lifted and reset
- Direction: A printer's forme seen from above: steel-grey bed (#C9CDD1), dark iron frame (#2A2C2F), boxwood-tan blocks (#C8A26B) with black inked letters, brass spacers (#C9A24A). One condensed wood-type grotesque.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** Built in HTML and CSS. A steel-grey bed fills the screen. On it, an iron frame about 72vw wide, with bars 18px thick, holds two lines of type. Every letter is its own block: tan, with a lighter top edge, a soft shadow and the letter inked in black on its face. Blank brass blocks separate the words. Letters read the right way round.

- Line 1, blocks 4vw wide and 8vw tall: "LEAD IN SERVICE", a success criterion.
- Line 2, blocks 2.4vw wide and 5vw tall: "THROUGH DIRECT SALES", an objective.
- A paper slip tucked under the frame: "Illustrative".

The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits below the frame on the left.

Pin the stage for about 280vh.

1. Adapt. The blocks of "DIRECT SALES" lift (6% larger, deeper shadow), slide to the right edge of the screen and lie there in a loose row. New blocks spelling "PARTNERS" drop into the gap. Line 1 does not move.
2. Pivot. The blocks of "SERVICE" lift away. New blocks spelling "SOFTWARE" hover over the gap without ink: tan faces, letters in outline only. They wait until a small brass tag reading "Confirmed" appears beside them. Then they drop in and turn black.

**The words.**
- h1: "A strategy is set in type, not carved in stone."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and resets a line when the facts change.
- With step 1: "Adapt. The objective is reset. The line above holds."
- With step 2: "Pivot. New wording waits until someone with authority confirms it."

**Phone.** One word per row, with blocks sized so that "SOFTWARE" fits 84vw. The lifted blocks lie below the frame.

**Still.** The state after step 1: "PARTNERS" in place, the old blocks lying to the side, line 1 untouched, the first caption showing.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
