# 92. Depth of field

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: Twelve discs of light, and a focus pull that keeps four sharp
- Direction: A neutral dark or pale ground, twelve luminous discs in a restrained palette, real lens blur with soft bokeh edges and a slight vignette. Small, plain type.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** Twelve discs of colour sit at different depths, like lights seen through a lens. At first all twelve are equally sharp and about the same size. Nothing stands out.

As the visitor scrolls, the lens pulls focus. Eight discs drift back and soften into large, pale bokeh. Four come forward and become sharp, saturated and larger. Each of the four gets a two-word label, for example "Service revenue". Mark the labels "Illustrative".

Make it photographic: blur that grows with depth, a faint bright rim on the bokeh, slight colour fringing at the edges, a gentle vignette. The eight in the background must still be visible. They are behind, not gone.

**The words.**
- h1: "Twelve goals. Four in focus."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and agrees what gets its attention this quarter.
- Before the pull: "Everything matters equally. So nothing moves."
- After: "The other eight are still there. They wait."

**Phone.** Arrange the discs for portrait. The four sharp ones should fill most of the screen.

**Still.** After the pull: four sharp, eight soft.
