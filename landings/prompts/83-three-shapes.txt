# 83. Three shapes

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A triangle, a circle and a square carry the whole story
- Direction: Off-white ground, primary yellow, blue and red, black type. A geometric sans. Shapes overlap with multiply. The school of the 1920s, done with restraint.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** Three flat shapes, very large, composed like a poster.

- A yellow triangle: a signal. It enters from outside the frame, pointing in.
- A blue circle: a session. It is a ring, not a disc, with room inside it.
- A red square: a decision. It lands and stays.

First screen: all three in one tight composition, overlapping, cropped by the edges. Then each shape gets one screen where it is alone and enormous, with a single line of text. Last screen: the squares from many decisions stacked into a wall, with one circle and one triangle beside it.

Composition is everything here. Use the edges of the screen, strong diagonals and real tension between the shapes. Nothing centred by default.

**The words.**
- h1: "Strategy, reduced to three shapes."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team sees what changed, talks it through and decides.
- Triangle: "A signal. Something outside changed."
- Circle: "A session. Sixty minutes, every view heard."
- Square: "A decision. Numbered and kept."

**Phone.** Recompose for portrait. The shapes get bigger, not smaller.

**Still.** The opening composition.
