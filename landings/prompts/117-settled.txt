# 117. Settled

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A jar of stirred sediment that settles into an agenda
- Direction: A laboratory still life. Pale grey-green ground (#DDE5DF), a tall glass cylinder in thin white and grey lines, grains in slate (#4B5A68), rust (#B5532F), ochre (#D7A441) and chalk (#F1EADB). A neat technical sans.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A tall, straight-sided glass cylinder stands right of centre: 26vw wide and 72vh tall, with slightly rounded bottom corners, a thin rim, one long white highlight down its left side and a soft shadow at its foot. The water inside is a faint blue-grey tint. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits on the left.

Inside the glass, a 2D canvas holds about 1,400 grains: squares of 2px to 3px in the four colours, in equal numbers. On the first screen they hang at random through the whole height and drift slowly, like stirred sediment. Above the jar, small: "The week, stirred."

Pin the stage for about 250vh. The grains sink and sort into four level bands that fill the lower 60% of the jar, with clear water above. Slate settles first, then rust, then ochre, then chalk on top. No physics is needed: give each grain a random start and a target inside its band, and ease it between them, starting later for each colour.

When the bands are settled, a fine line leads from each to a label on the left of the jar, bottom to top:

1. "Carried forward"
2. "Parked questions, returning"
3. "Risks that moved"
4. "New signals"

Above the jar the small line now reads: "24 hours before the session."

**The words.**
- h1: "By the time you sit down, the week has settled."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy, and it drafts the agenda for every session from what changed.
- Last line: "Old business first. The session lead edits it. The room approves it."

**Phone.** The jar is 62vw wide and centred under the h1 block. The four labels become a key below the jar, each with a square of its colour.

**Still.** The four settled bands with their labels.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
