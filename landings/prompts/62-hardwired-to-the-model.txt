# 62. Hardwired to the model

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Business unit leaders and CSOs who think in Business Model Canvas terms
- Colours: House palette, plus soft blueprint grid (#DCE6F2) for the canvas lines and muted copper (#B35C37) for the external signal impacting two blocks.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Built on Strapivo's structural backbone: "Hardwired to the business model. Not a generic prompt with your PDF attached." Unlike concept 31 (an experimental scanner), this is a calm, architectural main landing page built around the Business Model Canvas. Because Strapivo's Strategic Design System encodes your actual business model, an external shift does not land as generic industry news — it lights up the exact building blocks of your model that are exposed, connects to your focus criteria, and frames the weekly decision.

You MAY name Dr. Alex Osterwalder as "co-founder and advisor, creator of the Business Model Canvas" (and add no biography beyond that exact role). Because you draw the Business Model Canvas, you MUST include the exact credit line: `Business Model Canvas adapted from Strategyzer.com, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.`

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero. Left column: headline "Hardwired to your business model. Not a prompt with your PDF attached." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System built on the Business Model Canvas that keeps your strategy current against live market shifts and turns them into weekly 60-minute leadership decisions. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a clean, architectural **9-block Business Model Canvas schematic** (labelled "Illustrative example", with the CC BY-SA 3.0 credit line right beneath it). One copper external signal arrow touches two highlighted Azure blocks (Key Partners + Cost Structure), tracing cleanly down to a linked focus criterion and risk tag (`Risk R-02 · Worsening`).

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why structure beats prose. A 60-page strategy document cannot tell you which part of your business model a supplier shift or competitor move actually breaks.
2. From business model to Dynamic Strategy. Drawing 2: a clean structural bridge showing how your approved Business Model Canvas connects to one Ambition, 7 to 12 success criteria (up to 4 in focus), objectives, activities and risks on the Radar.
3. The weekly session built on your model. Radar signals triaged by people, 24-hour drafted agenda, 60-minute session with sealed first takes, five options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet), and a numbered Record of Decisions (SDS-2026-014).
4. Grounded in real practice and European governance. Note Dr. Alex Osterwalder's role (co-founder and advisor, creator of the Business Model Canvas), Vivo proposes while people decide, EU-only infrastructure and GDPR by design.
5. Onboarding (Strapivo drafts your business model first from the public record and your documents; four to five hours, then you run it) and closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Architectural, authoritative, conservative. Crisp blueprint hairlines, disciplined geometric blocks, calm Paper and Navy typography. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
