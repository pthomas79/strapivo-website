# 24 · The drawer

Range: Experimental (concept 24 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Anyone who has shelved a strategy document
Colours: Paper, ink, Azure. Two additional colours: manila #E6D3A3 for folders and steel grey #8B949E for the cabinet.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/24-drawer/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

"The strategy goes into a drawer." The page opens on a closed filing-cabinet drawer drawn in flat CSS and SVG, labelled 'Strategy 2026. Approved.' Scrolling pulls the drawer open and the visitor finds what is in it.

Inside are folders with tabs, each a short, dry exhibit of what happens to a shelved strategy: the workshop report nobody opened again; the risk list last updated at the offsite; the minutes of the meeting where it was all discussed again; the action list with no owners left. Each folder opens to a few lines.

Then the turn: Strapivo takes the same contents out of the drawer and puts them to work. The same items reappear as living objects: the report becomes Ambition, success criteria and objectives that Owners accept; the risk list becomes the radar; the minutes become the numbered Record of Decisions; the action list becomes activities with a person responsible who reports back. Close with setup: bring a workshop report, a document, an email or nothing at all.

Signature element: the drawer and its folders. Flat, graphic, a little wry, drawn with care. The opening is driven by scroll position with a reduced-motion fallback that shows it open. The second half of the page changes to clean product-like surfaces to mark the turn.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
- Finish with the two steps in section 9 of the brief: screenshot and fix, then the unslop pass using `.claude/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`.
