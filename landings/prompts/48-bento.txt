# 48. The product page

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Evaluators used to modern software sites who say 'show me the product'
- Colours: Navy surfaces (#050C14, #141B24) with Azure, plus status colours used only for lights and states: green (#2FA36B), yellow (#E0B341), red (#D8503F).

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

The modern product-led landing page that round one never produced: a tight hero, then the product shown as a grid of small living tiles.

**Hero.** "Strategy finally has a home." The sentence under it says in plain words what Strapivo does for a leadership team. Primary call to action. Directly below, still partly visible on the first screen, the grid begins.

**The grid.** Six or seven tiles of different sizes in one grid. Each tile shows exactly one mechanism of Dynamic Strategy, drawn large, with a short title and one line of caption. A tile is one idea, never a screen full of interface.
- The Radar, the largest tile: a risk and its proposed new position as a ghost, with "Confirm" and "Reject".
- The light: one success criterion and the question "Light still green?"
- The agenda: one item, its one-line reason, "drafted 24 hours before".
- First takes: five sealed cards that turn over together.
- Five options: Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet, with one selected.
- The record: a decision number such as SDS-2026-014 being added to the list.
- Next session: a countdown.

Motion is slow and loops quietly. Each tile also reads as a still image, and motion stops under reduced motion. On a phone the grid becomes one column and every tile stays legible.

**Below the grid.** Three short sections: the problem in two executive quotes, how a week runs in three beats (signal, discuss, decide), and who decides (Vivo proposes, people decide, trust facts). Then getting started and a closing call to action.

**Look.** Dark, flat and exact. Tiles have a one-pixel border and no glow, no glass, no gradient mesh. The brief's brand rules still apply, so this must not look like a generic software template. The drawings inside the tiles make it Strapivo's.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
