# 86. In the dark

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A black page and a torch that shows what moved
- Direction: True black with deep greys. Warm torchlight. One Azure ring. A plain sans. Things in the dark are drawn in thin lines, large and simple.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** The page is dark. A soft circle of light follows the pointer or the finger. When nobody touches the page, the light drifts slowly on its own path so that things come into view by themselves.

Hidden in the dark are four simple things, each large and widely spaced, each something that moved while nobody was looking:

- A date: "Last reviewed 214 days ago."
- A dot that has crept into the top right corner of a faint square.
- A small light that used to be green and is now yellow.
- One line: "A new competitor. Cheaper."

The headline is always lit. At the end the lights come up over the whole scene, slowly, and everything is visible at once, calm and ordered, with a ring around the dot.

Use a CSS mask or a radial gradient driven by custom properties. Keep it fast.

**The words.**
- h1: "What moved while nobody was looking?"
- Plain sentence: Strapivo watches the world against your strategy and calls the leadership team together when something changes.
- At the end: "Strapivo keeps the lights on."

**Phone.** The light follows the finger and drifts when idle. Lay the four things out vertically.

**Still.** The lit scene.
