# 47. The formula

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Executives who ask why this is not an AI tool or a monitoring service they already have
- Colours: House palette, with one colour per term: Azure for strategic memory, teal (#0F8B8D) for market intelligence, amber (#C98A00) for the strategic session, and Navy for the result.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

Strapivo's own formula: Strategic memory + Market intelligence + Strategic session = Lasting advantage.

**Hero.** The equation is the visual, set very large across the page, each term in its colour with its caption underneath: "what's true inside your company", "live signals from outside it", "the conversation where the two meet". On a phone it stacks as a column sum. The headline makes the argument from the deck in your own wording: you can buy monitoring and you can buy an AI conversation, and what you cannot buy elsewhere is the combination, because nobody else holds your approved strategy. The sentence under it says what Strapivo is.

**The page.**
1. One section per term, each with one simple drawing and at most 110 words.
   - Strategic memory. One Ambition, success criteria with lights, focus areas, objectives, activities, risks, and the decisions already made. Approved by people.
   - Market intelligence. The Radar reads the outside world for each focus area. Signals carry a date and a source. A person confirms each risk move.
   - Strategic session. The agenda is drafted from what changed. The session lasts 60 minutes, with sealed first takes and five options.
2. Take one away. This is the signature element: a control that removes one term from the equation and shows what is left. Without memory, news is noise. Without intelligence, the plan sits in a drawer. Without the session, there are alerts and nobody decides. With JavaScript off, show the three cases as three plain lines.
3. The result. What "lasting advantage" means in practice: a numbered Record of Decisions that holds what was decided, why and who disagreed.
4. Vivo proposes, people decide. Trust facts.
5. Getting started, closing call to action.

**Look.** Typographic and confident. The plus and equals signs are design elements. Keep the three colours tied to their terms everywhere on the page.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
