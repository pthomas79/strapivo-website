# 44. The design system

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Chief strategy officers, strategy teams and design-literate executives who want to know what 'Strategic Design System' means
- Colours: House palette for Strapivo. For the fictional brand in the left half of the analogy, three flat swatches: coral (#E4572E), forest (#2F6B4F) and sand (#E9DFC9).

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

This page explains the product through the analogy in its name. A brand design system has one logo, one type scale and one palette, and everything downstream plugs into it. Strapivo does that for strategy.

**Hero.** Headline in the spirit of "Your brand has a design system. Your strategy needs one too." The sentence under it states the analogy and what Strapivo is. The visual is one specification sheet split in two.
- Left, "Brand design system" for a made-up brand: a logo mark ("one mark, everywhere"), a type specimen ("one scale, everywhere"), three swatches ("one palette, everywhere"). Below it, four outputs plug in: brochure, campaign, website, newsletter. Caption: "One source of truth. Everything downstream plugs into it."
- Right, "Strategic Design System": the business model and its environment, principles ("guardrails for every call"), where to play and where you don't. Below it, four uses plug in: board meeting, team alignment, business model portfolio, defend strategy. Caption: "One source of truth. Every decision plugs into it."

**The page.**
1. What happens without one. Most companies rebuild the argument from scratch for every meeting. The same slides are redone and the same debate restarts.
2. The parts of a strategy, specified. Show the Dynamic Strategy structure the way a style guide shows components: Ambition, success criteria with lights, focus areas, objectives, activities, risks. One clean sheet with short annotations.
3. A system that stays current. A design system gets versioned. So does a strategy in Strapivo: the Radar reads the outside world, a 60-minute session decides, and each change gets a decision number such as SDS-2026-014.
4. Approved by you. Vivo proposes, people decide. Trust facts.
5. Getting started, closing call to action.

**Look.** A style guide: specimen blocks, swatches, thin dimension lines, mono labels. Keep the annotations in plain words an executive understands, not developer jargon. Name no real design tool or AI product.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
