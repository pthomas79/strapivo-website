# 49. By the numbers

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Time-poor executives who skim
- Colours: House palette, plus a warm paper (#FAF7F1) for alternating sections and a muted gold (#B8892B) as the second accent.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

Dynamic Strategy has fixed numbers in it. They are rules of the product, not performance claims, and together they explain the whole thing. This page is built from them.

**Hero.** Headline in the spirit of "One Ambition. Four in focus. Sixty minutes. Five answers." The sentence under it says what Strapivo is. The visual is the row of numerals itself, set large, acting as the table of contents: 1, 12, 4, 24, 60, 5, 14, 1.

**The page.** One short section per number. Each has a very large numeral, a unit, and 50 to 80 words. Each numeral is drawn so that it does some of the explaining.
- 1 Ambition. Everything is read against it.
- 12 success criteria, usually 7 to 12, each with a green, yellow or red light. Show twelve small lights.
- 4 in focus. Four of the twelve lights filled. "Fill a few glasses to the brim instead of all twelve a little."
- 24 hours before each session, Vivo drafts the agenda from what changed.
- 60 minutes. Draw the numeral inside a ring divided like a clock.
- 5 options: Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet. Five segments.
- 14 days. A risk that sits in the danger zone that long with no decision lands in the session lead's "Needs you" list. Fourteen ticks.
- 1 record. Every decision gets a permanent number such as SDS-2026-014.

Close with one more number as the way in: four to five hours of your team's time to get started. Then trust and the call to action.

**Truth.** Say once, near the top, that these are the rules the product runs on. Add no statistic, percentage or result figure. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.

**Look.** Editorial and generous. One column, big numerals, lots of air. This is not concept 04, which used section numbers 01 to 05 on a strict grid. Here the numerals are quantities and each one is illustrated.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
