# 12 · Hard questions

Range: Confident (concept 12 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Sceptical executives with objections
Colours: Navy ground for the questions, Paper for the answers, Azure. One additional colour: amber #E0A12B for the one-word answers.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/12-hard-questions/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

The entire page is questions and answers. No feature sections at all. Every question is one a sceptical executive would put in the first meeting, and every answer starts with one word or a very short sentence, then explains.

Fifteen to eighteen questions, grouped loosely: about the AI ("Will it rewrite our strategy?" "No."), about the work ("Is this another meeting?" "It replaces one."), about fit ("We already have a strategy from a consultant. Does it fit?" "Yes."), about control ("Who decides?" "You do."), about noise ("How do you stop one blog post raising an alarm?"), about what it is not ("Is this a KPI dashboard?" "No."), about getting started. Take the answers from the brief. Where the brief does not answer, leave the question out.

Signature element: scale and rhythm. Questions set very large, one per screen-height band, with the short answer in amber mono beside it. The explanation opens on click, tap or Enter, and the first three are open by default. A slim index of all questions sits at the side on wide screens. With JavaScript off, every answer is visible.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
- Finish with the two steps in section 9 of the brief: screenshot and fix, then the unslop pass using `.claude/skills/unslop/SKILL.md`.
