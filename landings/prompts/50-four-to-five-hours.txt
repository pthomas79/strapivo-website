# 50. Four to five hours

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Buyers who like the idea and fear the effort: 'What does this take from me?'
- Colours: House palette, plus sand (#F1EADB) for the stretches where Strapivo does the work and a green (#2E7D5B) for 'you run it'. Azure marks the hours the customer spends.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

Most software pages hide the effort. This one leads with it, because the answer is good: three live sessions, four to five hours of the team's time, then they run it themselves.

**Hero.** Headline in the spirit of "Four to five hours of your time. Then you run your strategy in Strapivo." The sentence under it says what Strapivo is and that Strapivo's team does the reading first. The visual is one horizontal timeline across a few weeks. Long sand bars show Strapivo working. Short Azure blocks show the customer's hours: session 1, session 2, session 3. The timeline ends in a green stretch, "You run it". On a phone it turns vertical.

**The page.** First, in about 100 words, what the team has at the end: an agreed strategy kept current by Dynamic Strategy, with the Radar, a 60-minute session and a numbered Record of Decisions. Then the steps. Give each a "Your part" and a "What you get".
1. Before we meet. "We do the reading first." You send what already exists: strategy documents, board material, plans. Nothing needs tidying. Strapivo drafts your business model, first from the public record and then with your material. "You walk into session one with a draft you can correct, not a blank page."
2. Session 1, one hour: strategic memory. "We show you what we got wrong." You say what is true, wrong, missing or out of date. You leave with a baseline you approved.
3. Between the sessions. "The waiting is by design." The scan starts against your plan and evidence builds over days. It takes no time from you.
4. Session 2, one hour: market intelligence. "You teach it what relevant means." You rank signals up, down or ignore.
5. Session 3, one to two hours: the first strategic session. "We use it, once, together." A real question, one decision made, the reasoning kept with it.
6. After. You open it because there is a decision to make. No facilitator is in the room.

Then a short trust section. Include the deck's advice on data: start with public and non-sensitive material, and widen from there. Close with "It knows you. It watches your world. You decide in it." and the call to action.

**Truth.** No customer names, no prices, no founding-customer offer. Describe the onboarding as it runs today.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
