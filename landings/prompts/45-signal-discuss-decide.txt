# 45. Signal, discuss, decide

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Any executive who wants the product explained in one breath
- Colours: House palette, plus two panel tints: sand (#F4EEDF) and a deeper sky (#D4EBFF). One amber (#E0A100) for the single signal dot.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

The plainest page of all. The deck reduces Dynamic Strategy to three beats and gives each a caption in everyday words: something changed, you need to talk about it, maybe you do something about it and maybe you don't.

**Hero.** Headline in the spirit of "Something changed. You talk about it. You decide." The sentence under it: Strapivo runs that loop for your strategy every week, so the plan your leadership team agreed stays current. The visual is a triptych: three tall panels side by side on desktop, stacked on a phone. Each has one large line drawing and one word.
- Signal. A radar with one dot that has moved, and its earlier position shown as a ghost.
- Discuss. A table seen from above with five sealed cards on it.
- Decide. Five option tabs with one selected, and a decision number below.

Each panel links to its section.

**The page.** One section per beat, each with at most 130 words and its drawing shown again, larger.
1. Signal. The Radar watches each focus area. Vivo sorts what is relevant. A person confirms a risk move. A move into the danger zone needs a primary source.
2. Discuss. The agenda arrives 24 hours before, with a reason on every item. The session lasts 60 minutes. Everyone writes a first take before anyone speaks.
3. Decide. Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet. Say plainly that Hold is a decision and that "Not yet" needs a trigger that brings the question back.

Then: "And next week it runs again" (the rhythm and the Record of Decisions), what stays human, getting started, closing call to action.

**Language.** Write for someone reading in a second language on a phone. Short words, short sentences, no product jargon before it has been explained. This page should be the easiest of all 50 to understand.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
