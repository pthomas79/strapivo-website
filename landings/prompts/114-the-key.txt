# 114. The key

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A blank brass key, cut notch by notch into yours
- Direction: Oxblood ground (#5A1A1F), one large brass key (#C9A24A) with a lighter upper edge and a darker lower edge, fine cream labels. A classical serif, labels in small capitals.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG. One key lies across the screen, 80vw long, vertically centred, with a soft shadow below it. The bow is on the left: a rounded rectangle with a short slot in it, not a ring. The blade runs to the right, 8vh high, with one groove along its length. The h1 and the plain sentence sit top left, the button bottom left.

The top edge of the blade is a polygon. On the first screen it is a straight line, except for one notch near the bow that is already cut. Under the blade, small: "A blank key. Any question, anyone's answer."

Pin the stage for about 250vh. Four more notches are cut, one at a time, from left to right. Each is a V of a different depth, between 20% and 55% of the blade height. Animate the depth of each from zero. As each notch finishes, a fine cream line rises from it to a label above the blade:

1. "Your business model"
2. "Your principles"
3. "Where you play"
4. "Where you do not"
5. "Your decisions"

When the fifth is cut, the key slides 6vw to the right, and its tip enters a narrow slot in a small brass plate at the right edge of the screen.

**The words.**
- h1: "Any AI can answer a question. Only Strapivo answers it as you."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy, so its AI answers from what the team has approved.
- Last line: "Cut from the strategic memory you approved."

**Phone.** Turn the key upright: bow at the top, blade pointing down, 70vh long, notches on its right side. The labels sit to the right of the notches.

**Still.** All five notches cut and labelled, the key not yet in the plate.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
