# 110. The balance

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A balance that rumours cannot tip and one primary source can
- Direction: Deep teal ground (#123F45), a balance drawn in brass (#C9A24A) with thin lines and a few flat fills, cream paper slips, one iron block. An engraver's serif in cream, labels in small capitals.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG. A beam balance in front elevation, about 70vh tall, slightly right of centre: a column on a stepped base, a beam 56vw wide with a short pointer at its centre, and two flat trays, each hanging from three fine lines. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits top left.

- Left tray: one dark iron block (#1B2326) engraved "ALARM". It sits low. The beam tilts 9 degrees towards it.
- Right tray: on the first screen three cream paper slips already lie on it, each at a slight angle: "Blog post", "Repost", "Rumour".

Pin the stage for about 250vh.

1. Two more slips drift down onto the right tray: "Opinion" and "Another repost". The beam does not move.
2. One small solid brass weight is set on top of the slips. A fine line leads from it to the label "Primary source".
3. The beam swings slowly through level to 9 degrees the other way, in one eased movement with no bounce. The alarm rises.

The trays stay level while the beam turns: hang them from the beam ends and counter-rotate them.

**The words.**
- h1: "One blog post cannot raise an alarm."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team watches the risks to its strategy, with a dated source behind every signal.
- At the end: "A risk moves into the danger zone only on a primary source, and only when a person confirms."

**Phone.** The beam is 84vw wide. The balance sits in the upper 60% of the screen and the h1 block below it.

**Still.** The alarm raised, the brass weight on the pile of slips, the label and the last line showing.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
