# 116. Hallmarks

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: Five marks struck into silver, one for each proof
- Direction: An extreme close-up of brushed silver: a cool grey gradient (#B9BDC2 to #EEF0F2) with fine horizontal grain. A sharp engraver's serif inside the marks, captions in spaced small capitals in graphite (#2B2E33).

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A full-screen metal surface: a diagonal `linear-gradient` from the darker to the lighter grey, with a `repeating-linear-gradient` of 1px horizontal lines at 4% black over it. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits top left, in graphite.

Across the middle, a row of five struck marks in SVG, each about 11vw wide. Each is a sunken cartouche with a different outline: a shield, a rectangle with cut corners, a hexagon, a lozenge, an arch-topped panel. Draw each as a shape filled two shades darker than the surface, with a 2px light line along its lower right edge and a 2px dark line along its upper left. Inside each, raised lettering in light grey with a thin dark shadow:

1. "12 MAR". Caption below: "Dated".
2. "P". Caption: "Primary source".
3. "ADAPT". Caption: "Decided".
4. A plain tick. Caption: "Confirmed by a person".
5. "014". Caption: "Numbered for good".

First screen: marks 1 and 2 are struck. The other three are thin scribed outlines only.

Pin the stage for about 200vh. Marks 3, 4 and 5 are struck in turn. For each: the whole surface brightens for 120ms, the mark appears sunken, and its caption fades in. Under the row, at the end, in mono: "SDS-2026-014. Illustrative."

**The words.**
- h1: "Every decision carries its marks."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team decides on its strategy and can trace each decision back to its evidence.
- Last line: "Ask where a number came from. The record answers."

**Phone.** The marks stand in a column, each 34vw wide, with its caption to the right.

**Still.** All five marks struck, all captions, the decision number.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
