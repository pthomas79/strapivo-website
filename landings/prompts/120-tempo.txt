# 120. Tempo

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A metronome whose weight sets how often strategy is reviewed
- Direction: A flat lilac ground (#C9BEDD), a black lacquer metronome (#17151A) with a brass pendulum (#C9A24A), one soft floor shadow. A high-contrast serif with a true italic, as on printed tempo markings.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG. One metronome in front elevation, right of centre, standing on a soft shadow. Its body is a tall tapered block, 22vw wide at the base, 9vw at the top and 58vh tall, with a slightly lighter front panel. Down the middle of the panel runs a brass scale with fine tick marks. The pendulum is a thin brass rod that pivots near the bottom of the panel and reaches above the top of the body. A small brass weight slides on it. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits on the left.

Three positions on the scale are named in italic to its right. From top to bottom: "Monthly", "Fortnightly", "Weekly". The higher the weight, the slower the beat.

Below everything, a thin line runs the full width of the screen with 52 short marks on it, one per week. Marks for weeks with a session are black and tall. The others are faint.

First screen: the weight is at "Monthly". The pendulum swings 22 degrees each way, once every 3.2 seconds. Twelve of the marks, evenly spread, are black.

Pin the stage for about 220vh. The weight slides down to "Fortnightly" and then to "Weekly". The swing quickens to 1.6 and then 0.8 seconds. Then every second mark is black, then every mark. Under the line a caption changes: "12 sessions a year", "26 sessions a year", "52 sessions a year".

**The words.**
- h1: "Strategy needs a tempo."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and reviews it in a session every week, fortnight or month.
- Last line: "Sixty minutes each. An extra session when something cannot wait."

**Phone.** The body is 52vw wide at the base and 50vh tall, centred. The year line wraps into two rows of 26 marks.

**Still.** The weight at "Weekly", the pendulum held at 15 degrees, all 52 marks black.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
