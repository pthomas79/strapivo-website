# 113. The shadow

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: One post, its moving shadow, and a stud for every session
- Direction: A sunlit stone square seen from directly above. Pale warm paving (#EFE6D6) with faint joints, one violet-blue shadow (#33346B), small brass studs. A thin geometric sans, mono for the dates.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG, full screen, seen from above. The paving is a flat warm tone with a faint grid of joints every 12vw. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits bottom right, clear of the shadow.

A small dark square, 28px, is the top of a post. It stands 30% from the left and 70% from the top. Its shadow is one bar, 28px wide and 48vw long, that starts at the post and turns about it.

Where the tip of the shadow stood at each session, a brass stud is set in the paving: a 10px square turned 45 degrees, with a date beside it in mono. The six sessions sit at 12, 21, 30, 39, 48 and 57 degrees above the horizontal, to the right of the post. Their dates: "5 Mar", "12 Mar", "19 Mar", "26 Mar", "2 Apr", "9 Apr".

First screen: the first three studs are in place and the shadow has just passed the third.

Pin the stage for about 250vh. The shadow turns slowly to 57 degrees. As its tip passes each remaining position, a stud and its date appear there. At the end two thin dimension lines with end ticks are drawn: a short one between the last two studs, labelled "Since the last session", and a long one from the first stud to the last, labelled "Since the start". Add "Illustrative", small.

**The words.**
- h1: "You cannot watch a shadow move. You can mark where it was."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and records what moved between one session and the next.
- Last line: "Show the board what moved between meetings."

**Phone.** The post stands near the bottom left corner. The shadow is 78vw long and sweeps the middle of the screen. Show dates on the first and last studs only.

**Still.** Six studs, both dimension lines, the shadow resting on the sixth.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
