# 76. One line

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A single continuous line drawing that draws itself down the page
- Direction: Cream paper, one black ink line of even weight with round ends, one Azure moment at the end. A light serif italic or a quiet sans for captions.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** One line, never lifted, runs from the top of a tall page to the bottom and draws itself as the visitor scrolls. Along the way the same line forms four simple figures, in the manner of a single-line drawing:

1. A drawer, closed.
2. An eye or a radar dish, looking outward.
3. A table seen from above with chairs around it.
4. A signature flourish that ends by closing into a ring. The ring turns Azure. It is the Strapivo mark.

Draw the path by hand as SVG. It has to be elegant: confident curves, generous loops between the figures, no wobble, no clip-art outlines. Spend your time here.

**The words.** A caption of six words or fewer beside each figure.
- h1: "From the drawer to a decision, in one line."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy, watches what changes and decides.
- Captions: "Where strategy usually lives." "Something outside moved." "Sixty minutes, everyone's view." "Decided. On the record."

**Phone.** Design the path in a tall, narrow view box so the same drawing works at 390 px.

**Still.** The whole line, fully drawn.
