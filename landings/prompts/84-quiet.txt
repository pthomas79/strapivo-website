# 84. Quiet

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A hushed page of very large serif type and one hairline
- Direction: Bone paper (#F4F1EA), near-black ink, one high-contrast serif with a true italic, set at 8 to 12 percent of the screen width. Small caps with wide tracking for the few labels.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** Typography and space, nothing else. One sentence per screen, set very large, with tight leading and careful line breaks. Italic picks out one word per sentence. The feel is a fashion house or a private bank, not software.

One hairline runs down the whole page like a plumb line, slightly off-centre. It passes behind each sentence and ends at the bottom in a small ring, which is the only colour on the page.

No boxes, no buttons with fills, no icons. The call to action is an underlined line of type.

**The words.** Five sentences, one per screen.
1. h1: "Strategy finally has a home."
2. "Not a slide. Not a drawer."
3. "One place, kept current by the people who own it."
4. "A session when something changes."
5. "You decide. It remembers."

- Plain sentence, small, under the h1: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and returns to it every week.

Kern and break every line by hand. A bad rag ruins this page.

**Phone.** The type stays as large as the width allows, three or four words a line.

**Still.** The page as it is. Motion is limited to the hairline drawing down.
