# 51. Answers as you

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: CEOs sceptical of generic AI tools applied to strategy
- Colours: House palette, plus cool slate (#D8DFE7) for the generic AI side and a calm pine (#1F6F54) for verified citations from strategic memory.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Built on Strapivo's own slogan: "Any AI can answer a question. Only Strapivo answers it as you." Sceptical CEOs have already pasted a strategy PDF into a general AI tool and received smooth, generic advice that could belong to any company. Strapivo is hardwired to your approved Business Model Canvas, your Ambition, your focus areas and your past decisions.

**Hero.** Headline: "Any AI can answer a question. Only Strapivo answers it as you." Below it, two plain sentences explaining what Strapivo is: the Strategic Design System where a leadership team keeps its agreed strategy current, watched against live market signals and updated in a 60-minute session. Primary call to action. Filling the right half (or spanning cleanly across the bottom of the hero above the fold on desktop) is one large split comparison card for a fictional mid-sized manufacturer (labelled "Illustrative example") answering one real question: "Should we add a second European warehouse this year?"
- Left card, muted slate, labelled "Generic AI + attached PDF": three bland, interchangeable lines ("Consider regional demand, evaluate capex, align stakeholders").
- Right card, crisp paper with Azure border, labelled "Vivo in Strapivo": three concrete lines anchored to named artefacts ("Checks Focus Area 2: 14% service margin", "Flags Risk R-04 confirmed Monday from primary supplier notice", "Cites SDS-2026-009: expansion parked until Q3 lead-time review").

**The page.** Keep to at most three product drawings total (including the hero comparison). Sections in order:
1. Why attaching a PDF fails. A static document goes stale the week after the offsite, and a generic prompt does not know what your leadership team agreed last month or what it chose not to do. Include one unattributed executive quote from the brief.
2. What Vivo is anchored to. Drawing 2: a clean structural diagram showing the three inputs behind every answer Vivo gives (your approved strategic memory, confirmed signals on your focus areas, and your numbered Record of Decisions).
3. How the weekly loop runs. Signal, discuss, decide: the Radar watches your focus areas, Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the 60-minute session, everyone gives a sealed first take, and the room picks one of five options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet).
4. The human stays in the chair. Vivo proposes; people decide. Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory you approved. EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design.
5. Getting started (four to five hours, "We do the reading first", start with public and non-sensitive material) and a closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Sharp, restrained, modern editorial. Crisp hairline cards, generous whitespace, high contrast between the washed-out generic side and the precise Azure-anchored Strapivo side. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page. Do not name any real AI vendor.
