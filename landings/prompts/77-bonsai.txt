# 77. Tended

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: An ink tree that is pruned from twelve branches to four
- Direction: Warm off-white, soot-black ink with tapered strokes, one small vermilion square. A refined serif or a thin sans. No brush-script fonts, no bamboo, no borrowed characters.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A small tree drawn in ink, grown by code: a trunk with tapered strokes, branching with slight irregularity so it looks drawn, not generated. It grows as the visitor scrolls.

At full growth it has twelve branches. Then eight are cut, one clean stroke each, and fall away. The four that remain thicken and carry the only foliage. The tree is now smaller, clearer and better. A small vermilion square in the corner holds a ring, like a maker's seal.

Leave most of the page empty. The tree sits low and off-centre.

**The words.**
- h1: "A strategy is not built once. It is tended."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and trims it every week.
- At the cut: "Twelve goals. Four in focus."
- At the end: "Small cuts. Early. On the record."

**Phone.** The tree is taller and narrower. Text sits in the empty space above it.

**Still.** The pruned tree with four branches, and faint marks where the eight were cut.
