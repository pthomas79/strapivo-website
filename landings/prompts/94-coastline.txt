# 94. The coastline

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A map that stays the same while the coast it shows keeps moving
- Direction: Chart paper white, a fine black survey line, a living Azure line, vermilion hatching for the gap. A small engraved-looking serif or a light mono for labels. No compass rose, no ships.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A chart of one stretch of coast fills the screen.

- A fine black line is the coast as it was drawn. Label: "Surveyed in spring."
- An Azure line is the coast as it is. It is generated from noise and it keeps moving, slowly.
- Wherever the two differ, the gap is hatched in vermilion. The hatched area grows as the visitor scrolls.

Then a thin vertical line sweeps across the chart: a new survey. Behind it the black line is redrawn on top of the Azure one, and the hatching clears. Label: "Resurveyed this week."

Give it the manners of a real chart: an even line weight, small soundings or contour ticks, a scale bar measured in weeks, a neat border. Keep it sparse. The two lines and the hatching are the whole picture.

**The words.**
- h1: "The coast moved. The map did not."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and checks it against the world every week.
- At the widest gap: "Nobody was wrong. The plan was just old."

**Phone.** Turn the coast so it runs top to bottom.

**Still.** Both lines with a wide hatched gap on one half and a clean, resurveyed coast on the other.
