# 101. Keystone

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A stone arch that stands only once the keystone is in
- Direction: Flat front elevation, like a poster. Pale sky ground (#D5E4EC), warm stone (#E9DFCB) with darker joints (#B9AD95), timber brown (#B98A55), one vermilion keystone (#D8412F). A sturdy serif, labels in small capitals.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** One stone arch in front elevation, drawn in SVG with flat fills. It stands on a ground line at 82% of the screen height, about 62vh tall and 46vw wide, right of centre. The h1, sentence and button sit bottom left.

- Two piers, each of four stacked blocks.
- A round arch of nine wedge stones: four on the left, four on the right, and a gap at the top for the ninth.
- Inside the opening, a timber frame (a curved rib on five struts) holds the stones up.
- The keystone, in vermilion, hangs on a rope just above the gap.

That is the first screen. The left stones carry the label "Strategic memory", the right stones "Market intelligence", the keystone "The session".

Pin the stage for about 250vh of scrolling. In the first half the keystone lowers into the gap. In the second half the timber frame drops 12vh and fades out. The arch stands alone, and the words "Lasting advantage" appear in the open archway.

**The words.**
- h1: "Two halves do not make an arch."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team brings what it knows and what has changed, and decides.
- Under the left label: "What is true inside your company."
- Under the right label: "What is moving outside it."
- Under the keystone label: "Where the two meet."

**Phone.** The arch fills 86% of the width and sits in the upper two thirds. The h1 block sits below it. Keep the three names on the arch and move the three short lines into a list under it.

**Still.** The finished arch with the vermilion keystone in place, no timber, all labels showing.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
