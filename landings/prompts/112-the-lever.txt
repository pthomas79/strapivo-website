# 112. The lever

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A row of signal levers, and one that only a hand moves
- Direction: A signal-box lever frame in front elevation. Pale industrial green wall (#C4D2C6), black iron floor frame, levers enamelled green (#2E8B57), yellow (#E8B923) and red (#C8372D), steel handles. A railway-style sans in capitals.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG or CSS. A pale green wall fills the screen. Along the bottom runs a black iron floor frame, 14vh high. Nine levers rise from it in an evenly spaced row across the full width. Each is a bar 2.4vw wide and 56vh tall, leaning back 8 degrees (rotate it about its foot). Each has a steel handle on top (a lighter rounded bar with one highlight), an enamelled colour band over its upper half and a small brass number plate at its foot, 1 to 9. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits on the wall above the levers, top left.

Lever 3 is yellow and lever 7 is red. The other seven are green.

Tied to the handle of lever 5 with string, a manila tag: "Light still green?" That is the first screen.

Pin the stage for about 250vh.

1. Two smaller tags are tied to lever 5 below the first: "Two activities off track" and "A risk entered the top right".
2. Lever 5 is pulled. It rotates forward to 14 degrees the other way in one slow movement. Halfway, its band changes from green to yellow. The tags swing a little and settle.
3. A brass plate appears on the floor frame under it: "Changed by a person."

**The words.**
- h1: "Vivo asks. It never touches the lever."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy, with a light on every goal that only people can change.
- On the floor frame, small: "Nine success criteria. Illustrative."

**Phone.** Five levers, each 9vw wide and 44vh tall, with the tagged one in the middle. The outer two are cut by the screen edges.

**Still.** Lever 5 forward and yellow, three tags hanging from it, the brass plate showing.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
