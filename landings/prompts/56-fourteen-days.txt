# 56. Fourteen days

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Risk-minded CEOs and general managers who refuse to let known threats linger
- Colours: Dark Navy hero (#050C14 and #141B24) transitioning cleanly to Paper (#F6F9FB) below, plus warm amber (#D4911C) strictly for the 14-day escalation threshold.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

The most expensive strategic mistakes rarely come from surprises. They come from known risks that everyone saw, nobody scheduled, and nobody decided. Strapivo has a built-in accountability rule: any risk that sits in the top right of the radar (the danger zone) for 14 days with no decision automatically lands in the session lead's "Needs you" list, "so that nothing stays dangerous and undiscussed."

**Hero.** Set on a crisp, flat Navy surface (#050C14) with generous breathing room. Top row: left has headline "Nothing stays dangerous and undiscussed." Right has two plain sentences stating what Strapivo is (the Strategic Design System that tracks live risks against your agreed strategy and brings unresolved threats to a weekly 60-minute leadership decision) plus primary Azure call to action. Directly beneath, spanning full container width above the fold: Drawing 1, a sharp **14-day horizontal escalation bar** (labelled "Illustrative example"). Fourteen clean vertical day ticks run left to right: Day 1 marks "Confirmed primary source, top right"; Days 2 to 13 show steady elapsed ticks ("top right · 13 days · worsening"); Day 14 breaks sharply into an amber-bordered Azure card: "Day 14: Escalated to Needs you. Added to Thursday agenda."

**The page.** Shifts to clean Paper (#F6F9FB) after the hero. At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. The quiet failure mode. Use the executive quote: "I know there's a threat coming. I don't know what it costs us to wait." Explain how threats sit in email threads while options narrow.
2. How a risk earns the danger zone. Drawing 2: a compact, large-type radar quadrant with one ghost move confirmed by a named person on the strength of a primary source, showing the trend label (Easing, Stable or Worsening) and time held ("top right · 14 days").
3. What happens when it hits "Needs you". Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the 60-minute session with the reason attached. The team submits sealed first takes, tests the cost of doing nothing with Vivo, and records one of five decisions (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet) under a permanent number such as SDS-2026-014.
4. Human authority, EU infrastructure and getting started (four to five hours, we do the reading first).
5. Closing Navy call-to-action band matching the hero's visual weight.

**Tone and look.** Bold, disciplined, executive. High contrast dark hero and closing band framing crisp, well-spaced light sections. Zero glow, zero neon, sharp 2 to 4 px corners. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
