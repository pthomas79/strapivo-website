# 90. The poster

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: Everything on one screen. No scrolling at all.
- Direction: One full-bleed colour of your choice and two inks on it. Type so large it is cropped by the edges. The discipline of an exhibition poster.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** The page is exactly one screen high and does not scroll. Everything is on it.

The word "Strategy" is set so large that it bleeds off the edges, and the rest of the line, "finally has a home.", is small beside it. The contrast in scale is the image. A small credit block sits in one corner, the way a poster carries its date and venue: the plain sentence, who it is for, the button. The logo and the legal links sit on the bottom edge, tiny.

Choose one strong ground colour and commit to it. Compose with the edges. Nothing is centred by default.

It must fit without scrolling at 1440 by 900, 1280 by 720, 390 by 844 and 360 by 640. Size everything from the viewport.

**The words.** 60 at most on this page.
- h1: "Strategy finally has a home."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and corrects it every week.
- Credit line: "For chief executives and their teams."

**Phone.** A different composition, made for portrait. The big word may run up the side of the screen.

**Still.** The page is already still. One slow detail is allowed, nothing more.
