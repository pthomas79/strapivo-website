# 70. Built for the strategy room

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Sceptical corporate CEOs, chairs and CSOs asking who built Strapivo and why
- Colours: House palette, plus warm ivory paper (#FAF9F6) for the main canvas and subtle graphite (#CBD2DA) for monograph hairlines.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

When a senior corporate executive looks at a new strategy software company, their second question after "What does it do?" is "Who built this, and do they actually know how strategy works?" This is the calm, monograph-grade credibility and lineage landing page. It presents Strapivo as the Strategic Design System built on the Business Model Canvas and Dynamic Strategy — and explicitly names the five founders and advisors behind it.

**Authorized team list (use these exact names and roles only; add NO biography or claims beyond these exact words):**
- Paris Thomas (CEO and founder)
- Mathias Maisberger (CTO and co-founder)
- Michael N. Wilkens (CCO and co-founder)
- Paul Cubbon (co-founder and advisor)
- Dr. Alex Osterwalder (co-founder and advisor, creator of the Business Model Canvas)

If you draw the Business Model Canvas anywhere on the page, include the exact credit line: `Business Model Canvas adapted from Strategyzer.com, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0.`

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero on warm ivory (#FAF9F6). Left column: headline "Strategy software built from the Business Model Canvas, not from a chat prompt." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System where a leadership team keeps its agreed strategy current, watches live signals against its focus areas and records every weekly decision. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a refined architectural **System Specification Card** displaying the exact structure of Strapivo in crisp typographic rows:
- Backbone: Business Model Canvas
- Rhythm: Dynamic Strategy (Signal -> Discuss -> Decide, 60 minutes)
- Principle: Vivo proposes; people decide
- Governance: EU-only infrastructure · GDPR by design
- Record: Permanent numbered decisions (`SDS-2026-014`)

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why we built a home for strategy. Every other function got software; strategy got slides that went into a drawer. Strapivo combines strategic memory, live market intelligence and the strategic session in one system.
2. How Dynamic Strategy works in practice. Drawing 2: one clear, spacious horizontal loop diagram (vertical on phone) showing the four core mechanisms — Focus areas (up to 4 of 7 to 12 criteria with lights), Radar with primary-source confirmation, 60-minute session with sealed first takes and five options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet), and the Record of Decisions with dissent by name.
3. The people behind Strapivo. A calm, clean five-row architectural roster listing the five founders and advisors with their exact authorized roles above, plus one understated sentence on why Strapivo works alongside leadership teams ("We do the reading first. Four to five hours, and then you run it.").
4. Governance and trust. Humans decide, humans validate, every recommendation traces back to approved strategic memory, start with public and non-sensitive material.
5. Closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Conservative, understated, monograph-grade European editorial. Warm ivory surface, crisp ink typography, disciplined horizontal hairlines, Azure reserved strictly for key actions and structural highlights. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
