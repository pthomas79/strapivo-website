# 99. The napkin

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A strategy sketched in pen on a paper napkin
- Direction: A dark table, a white paper napkin with an embossed border, blue or black pen. One handwriting face that looks like a real pen and not like a comic, plus one clean sans for the typeset part.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A square paper napkin lies on a dark table, turned a few degrees. It has the embossed dotted border and soft folds of a real one, and a faint ring left by a cup.

As the visitor scrolls, a strategy is sketched on it in pen, stroke by stroke: a circle with "where we want to be" in it, four arrows leading to it, a few words underlined twice, one thing crossed out, a star. The strokes should look drawn: slight wobble, uneven pressure, lines that overshoot.

Then the pen stops. Beside the napkin, the same four things appear set in clean type, in order, with a small ring next to today's date.

The napkin is not the joke. It is how good strategies often start. Treat it with respect.

**The words.** The handwriting on the napkin is part of the copy. Keep it to about fifteen words.
- h1: "Good strategies start on a napkin. They should not stay there."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy once it is agreed, and returns to it every week.
- At the end: "Bring a workshop report, a document, an email. Or this."

**Phone.** The napkin fills the width. The typeset version follows below it.

**Still.** The finished sketch on the napkin.
