# 88. A film in five shots

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A letterboxed film that the scroll plays, with subtitles
- Direction: Black page, a wide frame, a restrained cinema palette of your choice, fine grain. Subtitles in a plain sans, a mono timecode in the corner.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A wide frame, about 2.39 to 1, pinned in the middle of a black page. Scrolling plays the film. There are five shots. Each is an abstract composition of flat shapes with one slow camera move, a push or a drift.

1. A table from above, chairs as circles, all empty. Subtitle: "In spring, the strategy is agreed."
2. A rectangle slides shut and the frame goes almost dark. "Then it goes in a drawer."
3. A single point, far away, growing. "Outside, something changes."
4. The table again. The chairs fill one by one and a ring lights in the centre. "Strapivo calls the session."
5. One line is written and a number lands beside it. "Decided. On the record."

Around the frame: a timecode, a shot number such as "3/5", and a thin scrub bar. After the last shot, an end card like a title card, with the name, the plain sentence and the button.

Cut between shots cleanly. Give each one time.

**The words.** The five subtitles, plus:
- h1 on the opening card: "What happens to a strategy after it is agreed."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy alive between offsites.

**Phone.** Use a taller frame, 16 to 9 or 4 to 5, full width, pinned the same way.

**Still.** Five frames as a strip, each with its subtitle.
