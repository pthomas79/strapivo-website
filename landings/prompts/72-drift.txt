# 72. Drift

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A dark field of lines that wander, and one that holds
- Direction: Near black (#0B0D10), bone-white hairlines at low opacity, one Azure line. A light, wide sans or a mono for the few words.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A full-bleed 2D canvas. Thousands of very thin pale lines enter from the left and flow right through a slow noise field. They start parallel and fan out, wandering further the further they travel. One Azure line runs through the same field and arrives at a small ring at the right edge, because it gets a small correction at regular intervals. Show the corrections: tiny ticks along the Azure line, evenly spaced.

A pointer or a finger disturbs the field around it. The pale lines scatter and keep drifting. The Azure line bends and comes back.

**The words.** Three moments, far apart.
- h1: "Nobody decides to drift."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy on course, with one small correction a week.
- Near the ring: "Small corrections. Early. Every week."

The page is one tall screen or two. The canvas is the page.

**Phone.** Lines flow top to bottom, the ring sits at the bottom, text overlays the calm areas.

**Still.** Render the finished field once and stop. It must look like a drawing, not a frozen animation.
