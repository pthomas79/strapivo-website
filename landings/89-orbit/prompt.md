# 89. Orbit

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: An orrery: everything turns around one ambition
- Direction: Deep navy to black, fine brass hairlines (#C8A96A), one Azure accent. Tiny wide-tracked capitals for labels, a light serif or sans for the headline.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A mechanical model of a small solar system, drawn in fine lines and moving slowly.

- Centre: a small solid disc, the Ambition.
- Around it: a few concentric orbits carrying twelve small bodies, the success criteria. Four are larger and brighter: the focus areas.
- A comet on a long, eccentric path: a risk. It comes in from the edge of the screen.
- When the comet crosses the inner orbits, an Azure ring pulses once around the centre. Label: "Session called."

Tilt the system so the orbits read as ellipses. Draw thin brass arms or tick marks if they help it feel like an instrument, but keep it sparse. No star field wallpaper, no planets with faces, no space clichés.

**The words.**
- h1: "Everything turns around one ambition."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo keeps a leadership team's goals in view and calls a session when something heads their way.
- Three tiny labels: "Ambition", "Four in focus", "Risk, approaching".

The page is one screen and a short ending.

**Phone.** Tilt the system more so it fits a tall screen, with the centre a little above the middle.

**Still.** The system at rest with the comet close and the ring visible.
