Strategy finally has a home.

Every other function got software. Strategy never did. Strapivo is the Strategic Design System, one place where a company's strategy lives, stays current and gets used.

It runs Dynamic Strategy. Your team makes small, early corrections to the strategy it agreed, and occasionally changes course. The plan shows how, room by room.

EXExisting condition

Today the strategy goes into a drawer.

A leadership team agrees it in a workshop. The market keeps moving, and nobody notices until the quarterly review, when a correction is already expensive. News arrives unconnected to what must succeed. Meetings anchor on the loudest voice, and decisions end up in slides nobody reads.

What leadership teams tell us

  • "We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."
  • "Between offsites, nothing moves."
1
4 to 5 hours, then you run it

01Entrance

Setup starts with what you already have.

Setup accepts a workshop report, a document, a pasted email or a plain prompt. Vivo, Strapivo's AI, proposes where each item belongs: one Ambition, its success criteria, up to four focus areas, objectives, activities and risks. An Owner accepts each placement or moves it.

"This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down?"

2
Strategic memory

02Library

The Library is the strategic memory.

It keeps the business model and its environment, the principles and guardrails for every call, where you play and where you don't, and the decisions already made. Strapivo is hardwired to the Business Model Canvas. It is not a generic prompt with your PDF attached. Every recommendation traces back to memory your people approved.

3
Over 4,000 sources a day

03Observatory

The Radar watches the outside world for each focus area.

Its search brief is the success criterion, its objectives and its risks. Market intelligence reports on Monday and Thursday, and a red-line exception interrupts that schedule.

When a signal moves a risk, Vivo draws the new position as a ghost with a one-line reason. A person confirms or rejects it. A move into the top right needs at least one primary source, so that one blog post can't raise an alarm.

4
Four signal states

04Sorting room

Each signal is read against what must succeed.

Vivo makes the first pass: which success criterion it touches, relevant or not, and one line of reason. A delegated person then marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.

A relevant signal that matches no known risk arrives with a question.

"Possible new risk?"

5
60 minutes by default

05Strategy Room

Every item on the agenda ends in a decision.

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session from what changed, with a one-line reason per item. The session lead edits it, and the room approves it as step one. Before discussion, each person gives a sealed first take. The takes are revealed together, so the loudest voice can't anchor the room.

Five options per item

  • Hold
  • Adapt
  • Pivot
  • Take advantage
  • Not yet
6
One permanent number per decision

06Archive

Every decision gets a permanent number.

Vivo reads back each entry for the Record of Decisions, including dissent by name with its reason. The session lead confirms it, and the entry gets a number such as SDS-2026-014. A question marked Not yet is parked with a trigger that brings it back, or let go with a rationale. Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver in the app, as an exported memo or into the board pack.

7
One screen

07Hall

Mission Control puts everything that matters on one screen.

It shows the next session with a countdown, the Needs you list, each success criterion with today's light, and the recent decisions. A risk that sits in the top right for 14 days without a decision lands in Needs you, so nothing stays dangerous and undiscussed.

General notes · apply to every room

Vivo proposes. People decide.

  1. N.01Vivo proposes. A named person confirms or rejects every change to the strategy.
  2. N.02Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory your people approved.
  3. N.03Vivo never changes a light. When two focus activities go off track, or a risk on that success criterion enters the top right, it asks "Light still green?"
  4. N.04A Pivot takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it. Changes to accepted truth wait for an Owner.
  5. N.05A risk moves into the top right only with at least one primary source.
  6. N.06Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure, with GDPR by design.
  7. N.07The plan and the board pack export as Markdown. The plan also exports as CSV.
