# 41. One degree

- Range: Second round: landing page candidates
- Audience: CEOs and leadership teams who suspect the plan has drifted since it was agreed
- Colours: House palette, plus one vermilion (#D9482B) used only for the off-course line and its labels. A faint grid in #E1E5EA.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of ten second-round concepts. Each is meant to work as the company's main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other nine and from the 40 pages of round one.

## The concept

The page is built on one line from Strapivo's own deck: "A one degree course deviation today means a different continent in six months."

**Hero.** The headline carries that thought in your own best wording. The sentence under it says in plain words what Strapivo is and what it does about drift: a leadership team keeps its agreed strategy in Strapivo and corrects it once a week. Next to or behind the text sits one precise drawing. Two hairlines leave the same point at a small angle. The Azure line runs to a ring labelled "Destination", and the ring is the Strapivo O-mark. The vermilion line drifts away from it, and the gap between the two lines is marked at one week, three months and six months.

**The page.** Use three drawings at most, all in the same hairline style.
1. The hero divergence.
2. Two routes to the same ring, one above the other. The first drifts for a year and then makes one "late, hard turn". The second makes many "early, small corrections" and arrives. Those two labels come from the deck.
3. One correction, enlarged. A signal arrives, the team discusses it in a 60-minute session, and the decision gets a number such as SDS-2026-014.

Sections, in this order: why nobody notices one degree (the annual review, two of the executive quotes), small corrections once a week (Dynamic Strategy, "Helping you correct course once a week, so you never miss the mark."), what one correction is made of (signal, discuss, decide, the five options), who holds the wheel (Vivo proposes, people decide, the trust facts), getting started, and a closing call to action where the line reaches the ring.

**Tone and look.** Calm, exact, lots of white space, the feel of an engineering drawing. This is not concept 23: no compass rose, no chart paper, no parchment, no nautical props. Geometry only.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` and then `../BRIEF-ROUND-2.md`. Where they differ, round two wins. This page is a candidate for Strapivo's real main landing page, so it has to pass the five-second test, stay between 600 and 900 visible words, and be designed for a phone as carefully as for a desktop.
