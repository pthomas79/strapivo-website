The drift

Nobody notices one degree.

A strategy is agreed at an offsite and reviewed once a year. In between, a competitor launches and an assumption stops being true. Each change looks too small to call a meeting about.

By the annual review the gap is wide, and closing it is expensive.

What leadership teams tell us

  • "We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."
  • "I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings."

Strategy gets reviewed once a year, but today's world doesn't wait that long.

Dynamic Strategy

Small corrections, once a week.

Dynamic Strategy is how a leadership team keeps its agreed strategy current. Your ambition, the success criteria that measure it and up to four focus areas live in Strapivo. Each criterion shows today's light: green, yellow or red.

The Radar reads the outside world for each focus area. When something moves against the plan, it goes on the agenda of your next session. Most weeks the correction is small. Occasionally it is a change of course.

"Helping you correct course once a week, so you never miss the mark."

One correction

What one correction is made of.

Something changed. You talk it through. You decide whether to act, and Strapivo keeps the record.

  1. Signal

    The Radar flags a shift.

    Vivo, Strapivo's AI, links each signal to a success criterion with one line of reason. A person marks it relevant or not.

  2. Discuss

    Your team works it through.

    Vivo drafts the agenda from what changed. Each person gives a first take before the discussion, so the loudest voice doesn't set the answer.

  3. Decide

    The decision gets a number.

    It goes into the Record of Decisions as SDS-2026-014, with the reason and any dissent by name.

Five ways to decide

Doing nothing is allowed. It has to be a decision.

  • HoldThe criterion stands and is marked reviewed today.
  • AdaptVivo drafts changes to objectives and activities.
  • PivotThe criterion changes once someone with authority confirms.
  • Take advantageAdd activities for an opening without changing the criterion.
  • Not yetPark it with a trigger that brings it back, or let it go with a reason.

Who decides

Vivo proposes. People decide.

Vivo reads the signals, drafts the agenda and suggests where a risk has moved. It never changes your strategy on its own. Leadership teams tell us they want help, not a co-author with the pen.

  • A proposed risk move shows as a ghost on the radar until a person confirms or rejects it.
  • Moving a risk into the danger zone needs at least one primary source, so one blog post can't raise an alarm.
  • Every recommendation traces back to the strategy your team approved.
  • Vivo never changes a criterion's light. It asks whether the light is still green.
  • EU-only infrastructure. GDPR by design.

Getting started

We do the reading first.

Send us what you already have: a workshop report, a strategy document, an email. Start with public and non-sensitive material and widen from there.

  1. We draft your strategy in Strapivo.

    Ambition, success criteria, focus areas and risks, taken from your own material.

  2. You correct the draft.

    You walk into session one with a draft, not a blank page. We show you what we got wrong.

  3. You teach it what relevant means.

    You mark the first signals. Dismissed ones teach Vivo what counts as noise for you.

  4. We run one session together.

    After that, your team runs its own sessions.
