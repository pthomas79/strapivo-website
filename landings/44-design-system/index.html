Versioning

A system that stays current.

A design system ships a new version when the brand changes. So does your strategy in Strapivo, and every change gets a permanent number.

  1. Signal

    Something changed.

    The Radar reads over 4,000 sources a day for your focus areas. A person on your team confirms what matters.

  2. Discuss

    You talk it through.

    Your team meets with Vivo for 60 minutes. First takes are sealed and revealed together, so nobody adjusts to the loudest voice.

  3. Decide

    You act, or you don't.

    There are five options: hold, adapt, pivot, take advantage or not yet. The session lead confirms each decision, with dissent on record.
