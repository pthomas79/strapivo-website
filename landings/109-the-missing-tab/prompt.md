# 109. The missing tab

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A card index with a tab for every function except one
- Direction: Office stationery colours at poster scale: buff (#E6D3A3), salmon (#EFA58E), mint (#A8D4C0), sky (#A9C7E8), grey board (#D9D6CF), then Azure. Heavy grotesque capitals on the tabs, like typed labels.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A card index seen from the front, filling the screen, built in HTML and CSS. Five dividers stand one behind another. Each is a card the full width of the screen with a tab on its top edge, 18vw wide and 7vh tall, with rounded top corners and a soft shadow. Each divider sits 9vh lower than the one behind it, so only its tab and a strip of its colour show. The tabs step from left to right.

From back to front: "FINANCE" in buff, "SALES" in salmon, "PEOPLE" in mint, "OPERATIONS" in sky. The fifth divider, at the front, is plain grey board and its tab is blank. Its face fills the lower half of the screen. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits on the left of that face, in near black.

That is the first screen: four labelled tabs and one empty one.

Pin the stage for about 200vh.

1. The word "STRATEGY" is typed onto the blank tab, letter by letter.
2. The front divider turns white. Its tab turns Azure (#0082E6) with white letters and rises 6vh.
3. On the right of its face, five ruled lines appear, like index entries: "Ambition", "Success criteria", "Objectives", "Activities", "Risks".

**The words.**
- h1: "Every other function got software. Strategy never did."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is the software a leadership team uses to keep its strategy and work on it every week.
- Once the tab is labelled: "Strategy finally has a home."

**Phone.** Tabs are 40vw wide and overlap, each stepping 14vw to the right, so every label stays readable at 14px. The front card takes the lower 60% and the five entries sit under the h1 block.

**Still.** The tab labelled "STRATEGY" in Azure, the card white, the five entries on it.

## Standards

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action. Then read `../BRIEF-ROUND-4.md`, which replaces every design and build rule from earlier rounds. 60 to 200 visible words. The first screen says in one plain sentence what Strapivo is. The copy lines above are a starting point: keep the meaning, improve the wording, and cut whatever the visual already says.
