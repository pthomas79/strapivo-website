# 80. Exploded view

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: Three glass layers pulled apart and put back together
- Direction: Dark studio backdrop with soft light, translucent planes, fine highlights. Thin type with wide tracking, small. The feel of a hardware launch film.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** One object floating in a dark space: three thin slabs stacked close, seen in perspective (CSS 3D or drawn isometric). Light catches the edges.

As the visitor scrolls, the slabs separate along the vertical axis into an exploded view. Each one gets a hairline leader and a two-word label:

- Bottom: "Strategic memory". Its surface carries an abstract engraved grid. Do not draw the Business Model Canvas.
- Middle: "Market intelligence". A fine dot matrix with a few points lit.
- Top: "Strategic session". One ring.

Then the slabs close again into a single object, and it glows slightly. Label: "Lasting advantage."

This lives or dies on the rendering. Edges, reflections, depth and the easing of the movement all have to be right.

**The words.**
- h1: "Three things. Only useful together."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo holds a company's approved strategy, watches the market against it, and brings the two into one session.
- One line per slab, eight words at most: "What is true inside your company." "What changed outside it." "The hour where the two meet."

**Phone.** The object is centred and narrower, with labels below each slab instead of beside it.

**Still.** The exploded view with all three labels.
