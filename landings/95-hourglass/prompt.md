# 95. The hourglass

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: One hourglass, and sand that does not wait for a decision
- Direction: A pale, still ground. The glass in very fine lines with a soft highlight, sand in one warm colour, one Azure detail. Thin, wide-set type.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** One large hourglass in the middle of the screen, drawn in thin lines with a single soft highlight on the glass. Sand falls in a fine stream and builds a real pile below: simulate the grains, let the pile slope and slide.

The upper bulb is labelled "Still open". The lower one: "Already decided for you".

Beside the glass, level with the sand still in the upper bulb, a small Azure marker: "Parked. Returns in May." It shows that a question can wait on purpose, with a date, while there is still sand above it.

When the visitor reaches the end, the glass turns over once, slowly.

Nothing else on the page. Do not add numbers, percentages or any claim about money.

**The words.**
- h1: "Waiting is a decision too. It just has no owner."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo puts every open strategic question in front of the leadership team while there is still time to choose.
- Low on the page: "Hold, adapt, pivot, take advantage, or not yet. On purpose."

**Phone.** The glass fills most of the height. Labels sit left and right of the neck.

**Still.** The glass half run, the pile formed, the marker in place.
