Strapivo · Dynamic Strategy

The same strategy, seen from five heights.

The board reads a decision record. The people doing the work read their activities. In most companies these are two documents that no longer match. Strapivo keeps one strategy current and shows each level the part it needs.

Level 05 · The board

The board sees that strategy moved between meetings.

"I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings."What leadership teams tell us

Sees
The board pack arrives as Markdown. Every decision since the last meeting has a permanent number, a date and the evidence behind it.
Asked for
The board approves decisions beyond the leadership team's authority. Each one arrives as an escalation memo or inside the board pack.
Gets back
The record names dissent and gives its reason. The board can see who disagreed and what happened next.
Level 04 · The Owner

The Owner keeps the accepted truth.

"We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands."What leadership teams tell us

Sees
The Owner sees the Ambition, each success criterion with today's light, and the business model the strategy is built on.
Asked for
The Owner says yes or no to each change to accepted truth. Vivo drafts the change. Nothing enters the strategy until an Owner accepts it.
Gets back
The strategy stays settled between sessions. Every recommendation Vivo makes traces back to the strategic memory the Owner approved.
Level 03 · The session lead

The session lead walks in with the agenda already drafted.

"Between offsites, nothing moves."What leadership teams tell us

Sees
Mission Control shows the next session with a countdown, the "Needs you" list, the success criteria and recent decisions.
Asked for
The lead edits the draft agenda and runs 60 minutes with the team. After each decision, the lead confirms Vivo's read-back for the record.
Gets back
Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours ahead from what changed. Old business comes first, and each item has a one-line reason. A risk left in the top right for 14 days without a decision lands in "Needs you".
Level 02 · The member

Members give their view before anyone else's.

"I know there's a threat coming. I don't know what it costs us to wait."What leadership teams tell us

Sees
Members see signals for the focus areas they cover. Each signal has a date and a source. Vivo has already named a criterion and given one line of reason.
Asked for
Each member marks a signal relevant, irrelevant or unsure. In the session, each member seals a first take before the discussion starts.
Gets back
The room reveals first takes together, "without the loudest voice anchoring us." Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.
Level 01 · The Contributor

Contributors report one line and get an answer back.

"We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."What leadership teams tell us

Sees
The Contributor sees My activities. Each has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible, and each sits under one objective.
Asked for
Before each session, the Contributor reports a status and one line on the result. An ask of leadership is optional.
Gets back
The answer to the ask comes back linked to the decision that produced it. The Contributor sees what leadership decided and why.
Level 00 · Ground rules

Vivo proposes. People decide.

Vivo is Strapivo's AI. It reads, drafts and asks questions at every level. It never edits the agreed strategy on its own.

  • R1Vivo never changes a light.When two or more focus activities go off track, Vivo asks "Light still green?" A person answers.
  • R2Alarms need a primary source.A risk moves into the top right only with at least one primary source, so one blog post can't raise an alarm.
  • R3A Pivot waits for authority.Vivo drafts the new wording. It takes effect when someone with authority confirms it.
  • R4Every recommendation has a source.It traces back to the strategic memory your Owners approved, built on your Business Model Canvas.
  • R5Parked questions come back.A "Not yet" carries at least one trigger, so it feels like parking, not losing.
  • R6Your data stays in the EU.EU-only infrastructure. GDPR by design.
