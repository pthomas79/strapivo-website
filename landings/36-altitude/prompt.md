# 36 · Altitude

Audience: Corporates where the board, leadership and operations all see strategy differently

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/36-altitude/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

The same strategy, seen from five heights. The page descends from the boardroom to the people doing the work, and at each level shows what that person sees in Strapivo, what they are asked for, and what they get back. The colour of the page changes with the descent, dark at altitude and light at ground level.

Levels, top to bottom. The board: the board pack, decisions escalated for approval, a record that shows strategy moved between meetings. The Owner: accepted truth, the queue of changes waiting for acceptance, the Ambition. The session lead: the drafted agenda, the read-back, 'Needs you', the 14-day nudge. The member: signals to triage, a sealed first take, the session. The Contributor: 'My activities', a status, one line on the result and an optional ask of leadership, and an answer that comes back linked to the decision.

Then turn around and go back up in one short section: how a status from the ground reaches the agenda, becomes a decision, and reaches the board pack. That loop is the point: execution informs strategy without leadership managing to-do lists.

Signature element: an altimeter. A fixed vertical gauge at the side with the five levels marked, a gold marker that tracks scroll position, and mono readouts of the level name. The background shifts between levels by scroll position, so the descent is felt. Each level has one drawn interface fragment showing that person's view. Text contrast must hold at every point of the gradient.
