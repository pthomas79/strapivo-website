Strategic Design System

Five jobs in the strategy room. None of them is deciding for you.

Strapivo is the Strategic Design System where your leadership team keeps strategy current. Its AI, Vivo, works in five defined roles linked to your approved strategic memory.

Start the conversation EU-only infrastructure. Four to five hours to start.

01 Strategic memory

A general chatbot invents context. Vivo checks the record.

Generic chatbots offer plausible opinions without knowing what your team agreed. They miss broken guardrails and settled calls.

Strapivo stores strategy as structured truth. Vivo reads four approved artefacts first.

What Vivo reads Approved by Owners
Ambition

One portfolio target with optional turnover, margin and year figures.

Guardrails

Where you play, where you do not, and trade-off principles.

Business Model Canvas

Your operating model, built into Vivo instead of an attached PDF.

Record of Decisions

Every numbered call, rationale and named dissent on file.

02 Five roles

Five defined jobs, linked to real artefacts

Teams want help, not a co-author with the pen. Each role checks named artefacts.

Role and artefact Core question What Vivo does
01

Auditor

Primary sources, Record of Decisions

"Where did this number come from?"

Vivo traces claims to primary evidence or past decisions. It flags weak assumptions before you commit resources.

02

Advisor

Ambition, guardrails

"Does this fit our principles?"

Vivo tests proposals against your Ambition and stated boundaries. It catches drift before exceptions turn routine.

03

Scout

Focus areas, risk radar

"What moved against the plan?"

Vivo tracks outside shifts against focus areas in one line. It proposes ghost risk moves for people to confirm.

04

Colleague

Objectives, activities, risks

"Think this through with me."

Vivo prompts on scenarios, alternatives, black swans and waiting costs. Leaders test options while Vivo never rewrites strategy.

05

Researcher

Primary filings, market signals

"What evidence sits behind this shift?"

Vivo separates primary sources from secondary commentary. Debate starts from verified facts instead of headlines.
