Range: A bit nuts (concept 37 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: CEOs who learn from stories more than from features

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/37-two-companies/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

A split screen and a calendar. Two fictional companies in the same industry receive the same signal on the same day. The left company runs the annual cycle. The right company runs Dynamic Strategy. The page follows both for 120 days, day by day, in parallel. Label it clearly as an illustrative story.

Choose a credible, specific signal: a regulator's consultation paper, a key supplier's quiet price change, a competitor's hiring pattern. On the right: the signal is triaged on day 1, a ghost move is confirmed on day 2 with a primary source, the item is on the agenda on day 4, the team decides Adapt, one member dissents on the record, two activities change, and on day 60 a parked question returns with new evidence and becomes a Pivot that the Owner confirms. On the left, mostly nothing: the signal is forwarded twice, mentioned in a corridor, and reaches the quarterly review on day 97, when the options are fewer and dearer. Do not invent financial figures. Show the difference in options and time, not in money.

Be fair to the left company. Its people are capable. The practice fails them.

Signature element: the synchronised split. A shared day counter runs down the centre line in mono. Both columns scroll together. Long empty stretches on the left are left visibly empty, with only the day numbers passing, and that silence is the argument. On mobile the two columns interleave by day with clear A and B markers. Close with a full-width section on what made the difference and the call to action.
