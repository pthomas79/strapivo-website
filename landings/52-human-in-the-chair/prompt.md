# 52. The human stays in the chair

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: CEOs and boards wary of autonomous AI touching strategy
- Colours: House palette, plus deep slate (#222B36) for structural rules and muted sage (#2A6F58) for human confirmation stamps.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Built on Strapivo's founding governance rule: "Help, not a co-author with the pen." Many senior leaders reject AI in strategy because they will not hand judgment or wording to software. This page leads with the exact boundary between what Vivo prepares and what only a named person with authority can approve.

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero. Left column: headline "Vivo prepares the ground. Your leadership team makes every call." Plain two-sentence explanation of Strapivo as the Strategic Design System that keeps a company's agreed strategy current through Dynamic Strategy, where AI proposes and humans confirm every change. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a tall architectural **approval gate** diagram. A vertical centre line labelled "HUMAN CONFIRMATION" divides five crisp rows:
- Left ("Vivo proposes"): Ghost risk move / Drafts 24-hour agenda / Asks "Light still green?" / Drafts Pivot wording / Reads back SDS-2026-014.
- Right ("People decide"): Member confirms or rejects / Room approves step one / Owner sets the light / Authority confirms change / Lead records decision and dissent.

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why autonomous AI does not belong in strategy. Teams want evidence and challenge, not software rewriting their direction overnight. Name the two rules plainly: Vivo never changes a status light, and a risk cannot enter the danger zone without a primary source and human confirmation.
2. Four gates in one weekly cycle. Drawing 2: a clean horizontal (vertical on phone) four-step gate bar covering Triage, Risk move, Session decision (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet), and Record read-back with dissent by name.
3. Authority and roles. Short, clear breakdown of Owner, Admin, Member and Contributor so a board or CEO sees how accepted truth stays protected while execution reports back.
4. European governance and onboarding. EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design, every recommendation traceable to approved strategic memory. Start with public and non-sensitive material; four to five hours with Strapivo doing the reading first.
5. Closing call to action anchored on "The human stays in the chair."

**Tone and look.** Calm, conservative, architectural. Thin rules, disciplined two-column symmetry, muted confirmation badges, zero hype. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
