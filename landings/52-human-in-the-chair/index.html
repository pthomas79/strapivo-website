01 · The governance boundary

Help, not a co-author with the pen

Most agreed strategies go into a drawer until the next annual review. Leadership teams still reject autonomous AI that rewrites direction without approval.

Strapivo separates preparation from authority. Vivo brings evidence and challenge into the Strategy Room. Named people hold every approval over accepted truth.

Rule 01 Owner confirmed

Vivo never changes a status light

When two or more focus activities are off track, or a risk enters the top right, Vivo asks "Light still green?" and shows the evidence. Only an Owner changes the light.

Rule 02 Primary source and person

No single blog post raises an alarm

Vivo proposes a risk move as a ghost on the radar with a one-line reason. Entering the top-right danger zone requires at least one primary source and human confirmation.

02 · Weekly Dynamic Strategy

Four gates in one weekly cycle

Dynamic Strategy keeps an agreed strategy current through 60-minute sessions and small course corrections. Every step requires human confirmation.

Gate 01

Triage

Vivo proposes

The Radar tracks outside signals for your focus areas and adds a one-line reason.

Person triages
People decide

A delegated person marks each signal relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Dismissed signals show Vivo what counts as noise.

Gate 02

Risk move

Vivo proposes

Vivo places a ghost position on the impact and likelihood radar beside the linked signal.

Member confirms
People decide

A Member confirms or rejects the move. Any move into the top right needs a primary source before it joins the trail.

Gate 03

Session decision

Vivo proposes

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours ahead from recent changes and prompts on scenarios or delay.

Room decides
People decide

Members give sealed first takes before discussion and reveal them together. Each item ends in one of five decisions:

Hold Adapt Pivot Take advantage Not yet
Gate 04

Record read-back

Vivo proposes

Vivo reads back each entry for the Record of Decisions. It includes dissent by name with its reason.

Lead confirms
People decide

The session lead confirms the wording before the entry gets a permanent number like SDS-2026-014.

03 · Authority and roles

Accepted truth stays protected while execution reports back

Four roles separate authority over accepted truth from operational reporting. Boards see who approved each change and why.

Role Scope Responsibility Authority
Owner
Accepted truth
An Owner approves Ambition, success criteria, Pivot wording and status lights. Accepted truth changes only when an Owner confirms.
Final sign-off
Admin
Room setup
An Admin manages Strategy Room access, role assignments and session rhythm. Admins cannot alter accepted truth alone.
Workspace control
Member
Leadership sessions
A Member triages signals, confirms or rejects risk moves, gives sealed first takes and works through agenda decisions with Vivo.
Session gate
Contributor
Assigned activities
A Contributor reports activity status before each session, with one line on the result and an optional ask of leadership.
Execution input

Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver in the app, export as a Markdown memo or enter the board pack.

04 · Governance and onboarding

European governance, live in four to five hours

Data and provenance

EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design

  • Every recommendation Vivo makes traces back to approved strategic memory, dated sources and named confirmers.
  • Start with public and non-sensitive material. Widen what Strapivo holds once your team has tested it.
  • Export the plan, escalation memos or board pack as Markdown, and the plan as CSV.
Onboarding

We do the reading first

  • Send a workshop report, strategy document or plain notes. You walk into session one with a draft you can correct, not a blank page.
  • We show you what we got wrong and you set what counts as relevant. Then we run one session together.
  • Onboarding takes four to five hours, and then your leadership team runs it.
