What leadership teams tell us

Dynamic Strategy · the mechanism

How the field narrows

Your leadership team agrees one ambition and the success criteria that must hold for it. The team flags up to four criteria as focus. The Radar watches the outside world for each of the four. The team then meets for 60 minutes, weekly, fortnightly or monthly, and decides.

  1. 01 Search brief

    The Radar's search brief for each focus area is the success criterion, its objectives and its risks.

  2. 02 Source classes

    Each signal is a dated observation with a source. Strapivo classes the source as primary or secondary. Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday. A red-line exception interrupts that schedule.

  3. 03 Triage

    Vivo assigns each signal to a criterion, calls it relevant or not and gives one line of reason. A person you delegate confirms or overrules it. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise. A relevant signal that matches no known risk comes up as "Possible new risk?"

  4. 04 Primary-source rule

    Vivo shows a proposed risk move as a ghost on the radar, with a one-line reason. A person confirms or rejects it. A move into the top right needs at least one primary source. Every confirmed move joins the trail with its signal, date and confirmer.

  5. 05 Agenda

    Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session. Old business comes first, then new. A risk that sits in the top right for 14 days without a decision lands in the session lead's Needs you list.

  6. 06 Record

    Each item ends in Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Vivo reads back each entry, including dissent by name with its reason. The session lead confirms it, and the decision gets a permanent number.

Who decides

Vivo proposes. People decide.

  • A risk moves on the radar only when a person confirms the move.
  • Vivo never changes a success criterion's light. It asks "Light still green?" and a person answers.
  • A Pivot takes effect only when someone with authority confirms the new wording.
  • Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory you approved.
  • EU-only infrastructure. GDPR by design.
