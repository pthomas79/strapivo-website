# 28 · The broadsheet

Range: Experimental (concept 28 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Executives drowning in news
Colours: Newsprint #F4F1EA ground, ink, Azure used sparingly for marked passages. One additional colour: editorial red #B3261E for the masthead rule and 'relevant' marks.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/28-broadsheet/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

"News arrives as noise." The page is the front page of a broadsheet newspaper, and it makes the point by being one: a dense page of headlines, most of which do not matter to any given company. Then it shows what Strapivo does to that page.

Top: a masthead ('The Strapivo Signal' or better), today's date computed in the page, an edition line. Below: a real newspaper grid in Inter and JetBrains Mono, with a lead story, secondary stories, briefs, a column. The stories are the landing page content written as news: the lead reports that strategy has acquired a home; others cover the radar, the hour-long session, the five options, the record, the rule that one blog post cannot raise an alarm. Fill the remaining slots with short, obviously generic business-news briefs with no real company names.

Then the device: a control labelled 'Read against your strategy'. When switched on, most of the page fades to grey, two or three items are marked in Azure as relevant with a one-line reason and the success criterion they touch, and one is flagged 'Possible new risk?'. This is triage, shown.

Signature element: authentic newspaper typography and the triage filter. Column rules, justified text with sensible hyphenation, datelines, a weather-box sized 'Today's lights' panel showing green, yellow and red. On mobile it becomes a single column with the same filter.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
