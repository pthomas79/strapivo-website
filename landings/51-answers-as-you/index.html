Dynamic Strategy

How the weekly loop runs

Most teams review strategy once a year, yet the market changes every week. Dynamic Strategy updates your plan in 60-minute sessions.

01 Signal

Radar flags what moved

The Radar watches each focus area. A person marks signals relevant, unsure or dismissed. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.

02 Discuss

Sealed takes before debate

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session from what changed. Each leader writes a sealed first take. All takes open together before discussion begins.

03 Decide

One of five outcomes

The room chooses one of five outcomes. Vivo reads back the decision and named dissent. The session lead confirms the permanent record.

Five decision options

Hold

The criterion stays agreed and marked reviewed.

Adapt

Vivo drafts updates to objectives and activities.

Pivot

Vivo drafts revised wording for Owner confirmation.

Take advantage

The team adds detour activities without changing the criterion.

Not yet

The team parks the item with a return trigger, or lets it go.
