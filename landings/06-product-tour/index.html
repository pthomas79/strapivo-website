Why the screens exist

Every other function got software. Strategy never did.

A leadership team agrees a strategy at an offsite. Then nobody opens it again. The market keeps moving, and nobody notices until the annual review, when a correction has become expensive.

Strapivo keeps the agreed strategy in one place and checks it against the outside world every week. Leadership teams use it for Dynamic Strategy: small, early corrections, and occasionally a change of course. The tactics change often. The strategy changes when the evidence supports a change.

  • What leadership teams tell us"We finish the strategy, and then we forget it."
  • "We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands."
  • "I know there's a threat coming. I don't know what it costs us to wait."
  • "I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings."

Stop 01 · Strategy structure

Write down what must succeed

Owners set one Ambition and between 7 and 12 success criteria. Up to four become focus areas. Add a fifth and Vivo asks whether you want to dilute focus.

Each criterion holds objectives, and each objective holds activities with an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible.

Paste a workshop report, a document or an email. Vivo proposes where each item belongs and an Owner accepts or moves it.

Stop 02 · Radar

See a risk move before the review

Every risk sits on a radar of impact against likelihood. When a signal changes the picture, Vivo places a ghost where it thinks the risk belongs now, with one line of reason. A person confirms or rejects it.

A move into the top right needs at least one primary source, so a single blog post can't put a risk there. The Tracking view shows how each risk moved since the last session.

Stop 03 · Signal triage

Sort the news against your criteria

Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday. Its search brief for each focus area is the criterion, its objectives and its risks.

Vivo makes the first pass on every signal: which criterion, relevant or not, and one line of reason. A delegated person marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Each dismissed signal teaches Vivo what counts as noise for your company.

Stop 04 · Session room

Spend the hour on what changed

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours ahead from what changed, old business first. The session lead edits it and the room approves it as step one.

For each item the room sees the evidence. Then everyone seals a first take before anyone speaks, so the loudest voice doesn't anchor the room. Vivo can work out what waiting costs or argue the other side.

Stop 05 · Record of Decisions

Find what was decided, and why

Vivo reads back each entry before it is saved, with dissent recorded by name and reason. The session lead confirms it and the decision gets a permanent number.

Anything beyond the team's authority goes to an approver in the app, as an exported memo or into the board pack. Next quarter, anyone can open SDS-2026-014 and see the signal behind it and who disagreed.

Stop 04, in detail

Every agenda item ends in one of five decisions

DecisionWhat it meansWhat happens next
Hold

The criterion stays as it is.

Marks it reviewed on that date.

Adapt

The criterion stays. The plan under it changes.

Drafts changes to objectives and activities.

Pivot

The criterion itself changes.

Drafts new wording. It takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.

Take advantage

The room sees an opening worth a detour.

Adds detour activities and leaves the criterion unchanged.

Not yet

Something is missing. The room names it.

Parks it with a trigger that brings it back, or lets it go with a rationale.

Trust

The human stays in the chair

Vivo proposes. People decide. Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory your team approved.

Hardwired to the business model. The Business Model Canvas holds the structure underneath, so Vivo reads your strategy and doesn't treat it as a PDF attached to a generic prompt.

  • Vivo never sets a light

    When two focus activities go off track, Vivo asks "Light still green?" A person answers.

  • A blog post alone moves nothing

    A risk enters the top right only with at least one primary source.

  • Nothing stays dangerous and undiscussed

    A risk in the top right for 14 days without a decision lands in the session lead's Needs you list.

  • Accepted truth waits for an Owner

    Changes to the agreed strategy, including a Pivot, take effect only after a person with authority confirms them.

  • EU-only infrastructure

    GDPR by design. Your strategy is hosted in the EU.

  • Export everything

    Export the plan and the board pack as Markdown, the plan as CSV, and memos as Markdown or text.
