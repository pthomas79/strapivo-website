# 06 · The product tour

Range: Conservative (concept 6 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Evaluators who want to see the thing
Colours: Paper and Sky surfaces, ink, Azure. Status colours inside the mock interface only: green #1E9E6A, yellow #D9A300, red #D64545.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/06-product-tour/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

Product-led. The visitor leaves knowing what the screens are and what each one is for. Build the interface fragments yourself in HTML and CSS, at believable fidelity, in the style of a calm modern business application: light surfaces, hairline borders, Inter, mono labels.

Structure: a hero with a large composed view of Mission Control (next session with a countdown, Needs you, success criteria at a glance with their lights, recent decisions). Then a tabbed or stepped tour with one screen per stop: Strategy structure, Radar, Signal triage, Session room, Record of Decisions. Each stop has a mock screen on one side and three or four sentences on the other that say what the person does there and what Vivo does.

Signature element: the mock screens. They must be detailed and consistent with each other: the same fictional company (label it as an illustrative example), the same success criteria, the same risk appearing on the radar, in the agenda and in the record as SDS-2026-014. Tabs work by keyboard. On mobile the screens simplify, they do not shrink into illegibility.

## Standards

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
