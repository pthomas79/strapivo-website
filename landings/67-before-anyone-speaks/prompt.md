# 67. Before anyone speaks

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: CEOs and chairs who want independent judgement before the loudest voice anchors the room
- Colours: Deep Navy hero (#050C14 and #141B24) with crisp Paper (#F6F9FB) below, Azure for active controls, plus warm bronze (#C28B37) for sealed envelope marks.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Unlike concept 27 (an interactive first-take game exercise), this is a serious, bold executive landing page about decision quality in senior rooms. In most strategy meetings, the loudest or most senior voice speaks first and anchors the entire room. In Strapivo's 60-minute session, every agenda item follows one discipline: shared evidence first, then sealed first takes written independently and revealed together, then discussion with Vivo, then one of five decisions.

**Hero.** Set on a composed deep Navy surface (#050C14). Balanced two-column layout on desktop. Left column: headline "Five independent views. Revealed before anyone speaks." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System where leadership teams keep an agreed strategy current, bring verified market shifts to a 60-minute session and capture unanchored judgement before deciding. Primary Azure call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a refined **five-card simultaneous reveal frame** (labelled "Illustrative example"). Shows one shared evidence header ("Risk R-03 entered top right · Primary source confirmed") above five clean cards representing five executives. Include a subtle, keyboard-accessible toggle button ("Reveal first takes" / "Show sealed") that flips the cards cleanly between sealed bronze state and revealed positions (3 Adapt, 1 Hold, 1 Pivot — showing honest disagreement). Default to the revealed state OR ensure both states are legible and nothing is ever hidden at opacity 0; with JS off, show the revealed positions cleanly.

**The page.** Shifts cleanly to Paper (#F6F9FB) below the hero. At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. The anchoring problem in senior rooms. When five capable executives hear one strong opinion in minute two, four independent perspectives vanish before they are spoken.
2. One 60-minute session, four steps. Drawing 2: a crisp four-step horizontal sequence (vertical on phone) — Evidence -> Sealed first takes -> Conversation with Vivo (cost of doing nothing, scenarios, challenge) -> Decision (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet).
3. Nothing vanishes when the meeting ends. Vivo reads back the decision and named dissent for the session lead to confirm. Permanent record number (SDS-2026-014), with Radar signals and 24-hour drafted agendas driving the next session.
4. Human authority, EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design, and four-to-five-hour onboarding.
5. Bold Navy closing call-to-action band matching the hero.

**Tone and look.** Bold, dignified, high-contrast executive. Clean geometry, sharp 4 px borders, zero glow or gimmicks. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
