The problem in senior rooms

One early opinion anchors the whole room

Most strategy meetings lose independent judgement in minute two. The loudest or most senior voice opens first, and four capable executives hold back their disagreement before they speak.

An annual review leaves no way to catch that drift early. Between offsites, nothing moves. Teams restart the same argument whenever a new analysis lands. Months later, nobody can find what the room decided or who disagreed.

Open floor first

First voice sets the anchor

Four executives react to one confident opinion instead of the shared evidence. Polite consensus forms fast and breaks down weeks later.

Sealed first takes

Five positions open together

Every executive commits a view in private against the same evidence. All five open together so real disagreement enters the debate.

The 60-minute session

Four fixed steps inside every session

Leadership teams meet weekly, fortnightly or monthly, with extra sessions when a risk escalates. Every agenda item runs through four steps so discussion stays tied to approved strategy.

STEP 01

Evidence

The room reviews verified signals and risk movement before opinions begin.

STEP 02

Sealed first takes

Each executive submits a position in private. All five open together.

STEP 03

Conversation with Vivo

Vivo estimates the cost of doing nothing and models scenarios. It challenges assumptions against approved guardrails.

STEP 04

Decision

The room selects one of five outcomes. People decide while Vivo prepares the follow-up.

Five decision outcomes

Hold

Mark the success criterion reviewed today.

Adapt

Update objectives and activities under the criterion.

Pivot

Draft revised criterion wording for an Owner to confirm.

Take advantage

Add detour activities without changing the criterion.

Not yet

Park with a return trigger, or let go with a written reason.

Permanent record and next loop

Nothing vanishes when the meeting ends

Vivo reads back each decision and every named dissent with its reason. The session lead confirms the entry, and Strapivo assigns a permanent record number such as SDS-2026-014.

Between sessions, Radar tracks external shifts against your focus areas. A move into the top right danger zone requires at least one primary source so one blog post cannot raise an alarm.

Twenty-four hours before the next session, Vivo drafts the agenda from what changed. Old business comes first, followed by escalated risks and off-track activities. Every item carries a one-line reason. The room approves the agenda as step one.

Governance and setup

Built for senior scrutiny, live in four to five hours

01Authority

The human stays in the chair

Vivo proposes and people decide. Signal triage, risk moves and wording revisions wait for human confirmation. Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory you approved.

02Sovereignty

EU-only infrastructure

Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure with GDPR built into the architecture. Start with public and non-sensitive material, then widen access once your team sees how the room runs.

03Intake

Bring a workshop report, a strategy document or pasted emails. You walk into session one with a structured draft to correct instead of a blank page.

04Handover

We show you what we got wrong, and you teach Strapivo what counts as relevant. Then we run one real session together. After that, your leadership team runs Dynamic Strategy.
