# 98. Signal

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A ring made of characters that comes out of the noise
- Direction: Off-black with one phosphor colour, or paper with black ink. One monospace face for everything. No fake prompts, no boot sequences, no hacker clichés.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** The whole screen is a grid of monospace characters, drawn on a canvas so they do not count as words.

It opens as noise: every cell holds a random character, changing. Over about three seconds, or as the visitor scrolls, the noise thins out and what is left is one large ring in the middle of the screen: a torus rendered in characters by brightness, from `.` and `:` through `+` and `*` to `#` and `@`, turning slowly in three dimensions. It is the Strapivo mark, drawn in type.

A few stray characters keep flickering at the edges. One of them brightens, moves towards the ring and joins it: a signal that mattered.

The headline and the other words sit in the same grid, in the same face, as real text.

**The words.**
- h1: "Most of it is noise."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo watches the world against your strategy and brings the leadership team only what bears on it.
- Near the ring: "One signal. Tied to a goal you named."

Keep the frame rate honest. Render at a sensible cell size and pause when hidden.

**Phone.** Fewer, larger cells. The ring fills the width.

**Still.** The ring, clean, with a thin scatter of characters at the edges.
