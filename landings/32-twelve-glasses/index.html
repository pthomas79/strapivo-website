In Strapivo, a leadership team flags up to four success criteria as focus areas. Try to flag a fifth and Strapivo asks the question you get here, in the same words.

What a full glass changes

Flag a criterion as a focus area and Strapivo starts working on it.

A success criterion is one of the 7 to 12 goals your team measures against its Ambition. A focus area is one of the four you chose. Four things then happen for those four, and only for those.

  1. 01

    The Radar watches the outside world for it.

    Each focus area gets its own search brief: the criterion, its objectives and its risks. Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday. A red-line exception interrupts that schedule. Vivo makes a first pass on each signal, and a person you delegate marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure.

Where the glasses sit

Twelve criteria sit under one Ambition. Four are in focus.

Strapivo uses three words for the work: objective, activity, result. It is not a to-do list and not a KPI dashboard. Set-up accepts a workshop report, a document, a pasted email or a plain prompt. Vivo proposes where each item belongs, and an Owner accepts it.

"This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down?"

Ambition
One per portfolio, with optional targets such as turnover, margin and a year. Everything is read against it.
Success criteria
Usually 7 to 12 goals. Each carries today's light: green, yellow or red.
Focus areas
Up to four success criteria. The Radar, the agenda and the light check work on these.
Objectives
Usually 2 to 4 under each success criterion.
Activities
An action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible. On track, at risk, off track or done.
Risks
Placed on a radar of impact against likelihood, for the whole portfolio or for named business models.

The rest of Dynamic Strategy

A short session every week, fortnight or month.

The team makes small, early corrections and now and then changes course. Sessions run 60 minutes by default, with extra sessions when something can't wait.

  1. 01 Watch

    Signals arrive dated

    Each signal has a source and a source class, primary or secondary.

  2. 02 Triage

    People sort them

    New, relevant, watching or dismissed. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.

  3. 03 Move

    Risks shift on the radar

    Vivo shows a proposed position as a ghost with a one-line reason. A person confirms or rejects it.

  4. 04 Agenda

    Drafted from what changed

    Ready 24 hours ahead, with old business first.

  5. 05 Session

    First takes, sealed

    Each person gives a position before discussion. The room sees them all at once.

  6. 06 Decide

    Five options

    Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Not yet ends as Parked with a trigger, or Let go with a reason.

  7. 07 Record

    A permanent number

    Vivo reads back each entry, with dissent by name. The session lead confirms it.

  8. 08 Escalate

    Above the team, to an approver

    Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver. The lead is prompted to announce what changed and why.

Trust

The human stays in the chair.

Vivo proposes. People decide. Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory your team approved.

  • Vivo asks about the light. A person sets it.
  • A move into the top right of the radar needs at least one primary source. One blog post can't raise an alarm.
  • A risk left in the top right for 14 days without a decision lands in the session lead's "Needs you" list.
  • A Pivot takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it. Changes to accepted truth wait for an Owner.
  • The Record of Decisions keeps dissent. It names who disagreed and why.
  • Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure. GDPR is part of the design.
