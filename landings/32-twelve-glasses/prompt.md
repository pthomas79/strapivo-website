## The concept

"Fill a few glasses to the brim instead of all twelve a little." A page about focus, built on the spec's own image and on the visitor doing it.

Hero: twelve empty glasses in a row, drawn as simple SVG outlines, each labelled with an illustrative success criterion for a fictional company. One jug's worth of attention. The visitor chooses which to fill. Each chosen glass fills to the brim in Azure. At four, the page says that is the recommended limit. On the fifth attempt the product's own prompt appears: "You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?" If they insist, all filled glasses drop to a lower level, because the water is shared. A 'Spread it evenly' button shows the alternative: twelve glasses each with a centimetre of water.

Then the argument: what a focus area is and what it switches on. The radar watches the outside world for each focus area. The agenda puts focus areas first in new business. "Light still green?" is asked when focus activities slip. Display order carries no weight: there is no ranking, only in or out. Then the rest of the loop in brief.

Signature element: the glasses. Clean line drawings, water that rises with a slow ease, levels computed from one shared quantity. Keyboard operable as a group of toggle buttons with a live region announcing the state. With JavaScript off, show four filled and eight empty.
