# 85. First light

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A sky that goes from night to dawn as you scroll
- Direction: A full-bleed sky gradient, physically plausible: deep indigo, blue hour, a thin band of peach and gold. Thin white type. Dither the gradient so it does not band.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** The whole screen is sky above a flat, dark horizon in the lower third. No landscape detail beyond a plain line, perhaps one low rise.

It opens at night: near black at the top, a few faint stars, nothing at the horizon. As the visitor scrolls, the sky moves through the blue hour to first light. Colour gathers at the horizon. At the end the sun clears the line, and it is a ring: a thin bright circle, the Strapivo mark.

Pin the sky and let scrolling drive the time. Stars fade, the horizon glows, the ring rises. Keep it slow and exact. It should feel like a photograph that changes.

**The words.** Four lines, one at a time, small, low in the sky.
- h1: "A strategy goes dark the day after you agree it."
- "The market moved. Nobody was looking."
- "Then, once a week, someone turns to it again."
- Plain sentence, at dawn: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and looks at it every week.

**Phone.** Portrait suits this. Keep the horizon low and the ring large.

**Still.** Dawn, with the ring half risen, and all four lines stacked.
