Strategic memory and Radar

Structural bridge

How your business model connects to Dynamic Strategy

Strapivo connects your Business Model Canvas to one Ambition, focus criteria, activities, and Radar risks.

Structural bridge Objectives, activities, and results
01 · Backbone Business Model Canvas 9 approved blocks

It maps how your company creates and captures value.

02 · Direction Ambition 1 per portfolio

It sets optional turnover, margin, and year targets.

03 · Focus Success criteria 7 to 12, up to 4 in focus

Each carries a green, yellow, or red light.

04 · Delivery Objectives and activities 2 to 4 objectives each

Each activity sets a result, timeframe, and owner.

05 · Outside world Radar and risks Impact against likelihood

Each risk covers one model or the portfolio.

Verified signals link back to exposed blocks and focus criteria. A fifth focus area triggers a dilution warning.

Weekly session

One 60-minute weekly session built on your model

You do not rewrite strategy every week, and you do not wait until December.

01 · Triage

People filter the signal

Radar watches your focus areas. Vivo scores relevance; people confirm signals or dismiss noise. Top-right moves need a primary source.

02 · Agenda

Drafted 24 hours ahead

Vivo drafts the agenda from moved risks, signals, and off-track activities. The lead edits it and the room approves it.

03 · Session

Sealed first takes

Leaders enter positions before debate starts. Views reveal together so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room.

04 · Record

Numbered decisions

Vivo reads back each call and named dissent with its reason. Confirmed calls join the Record of Decisions.
