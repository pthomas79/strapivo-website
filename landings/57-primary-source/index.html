01 Why alerts fail

Why alerts fail leadership teams

News arrives as noise, unconnected to what the team said must succeed. Without a search brief tied to your success criteria and risks, monitoring creates work instead of clarity. Teams switch off alerts and stay in inertia, or chase recycled commentary and thrash.

Generic monitoring

Keyword feeds have no knowledge of your objectives. A speculative blog post triggers the same alert as a regulatory filing.

Strapivo

Strapivo holds your approved strategy first. It checks every observation against your focus areas and holds secondary commentary back from leadership.

02 Three-stage filter

Three filters before anything reaches the room

Strapivo reads over 4,000 sources a day against your focus areas, then applies three checks before leadership sees a signal.

Filter 03 Verification

Primary source gate

Primary source and Person confirms

Moving a risk into the top right requires at least one primary source. Vivo proposes a ghost move; a person confirms or rejects it.

03 Weekly cadence

From verified signal to recorded decision

Verified signals move on a fixed schedule into your leadership session and permanent decision record.

  1. 01

    Monday and Thursday reports

    Market intelligence reports twice a week against your focus areas. A red-line exception interrupts that schedule.

  2. 02

    24-hour drafted agenda

    Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours ahead from confirmed signals, top-right risks, parked questions and off-track activities. Old business comes first.

  3. 03

    60-minute session with sealed first takes

    Participants record individual positions before discussion. First takes reveal together so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room.

  4. 04

    Five decision options

    Each item closes as Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, or Not yet. Parked items keep a return trigger; let-go items keep a rationale.

  5. 05

    Numbered Record of Decisions

    Vivo reads back each decision with named dissent and its reason. When the lead confirms, it receives a permanent number such as SDS-2026-014.
