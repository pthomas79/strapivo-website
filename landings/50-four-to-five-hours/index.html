What you have at the end

A strategy your team reviews every week or month, not once a year.

We call this Dynamic Strategy. Your team makes small, early corrections while they are still cheap, and changes course only when the evidence says so.

  • An agreed strategy

    It holds your business model, ambition and success criteria. You approved every line.

  • The Radar

    It watches the outside world for each focus area and flags what moved against your plan.

  • A 60-minute session

    Vivo, our AI, drafts the agenda from what changed. Your team decides.

  • A Record of Decisions

    Each decision gets a permanent number, such as SDS-2026-014, with its reasoning and any dissent.

How onboarding runs today

Six steps. Three of them need you in the room.

  1. Step 1

    Before we meet

    Strapivo works

    We do the reading first.

    We draft your business model from the public record, then refine it with your material. You walk into session one with a draft you can correct, not a blank page.

    Your part
    Send what already exists: strategy documents, board material, plans. Nothing needs tidying.
    What you get
    A first draft of your business model.

  2. Step 2

    Session 1Strategic memory

    Your time · 1 hour

    We show you what we got wrong.

    We go through the draft together. You say what is true, wrong, missing or out of date. Vivo proposes where each correction belongs, and you accept it.

    Your part
    Correct the draft for one hour.
    What you get
    A baseline you approved, your strategic memory. Every later answer traces back to it.

  3. Step 3

    Between the sessions

    Strapivo works

    The waiting is by design.

    The Radar starts scanning against your plan. It reads over 4,000 sources a day, and evidence builds over several days.

    Your part
    Nothing. This step takes none of your time.
    What you get
    A first set of dated signals, each with its source.

  4. Step 4

    Session 2Market intelligence

    Your time · 1 hour

    You teach it what relevant means.

    Vivo shows the signals it found. You rank each one up, down or ignore. Every signal you ignore teaches Vivo what counts as noise for your company.

    Your part
    Rank signals for one hour.
    What you get
    A Radar tuned to your focus areas. It reports every Monday and Thursday.

  5. Step 5

    Session 3Your first strategic session

    Your time · 1 to 2 hours

    We use it, once, together.

    You bring a real question. Each person gives a first take before the discussion, so nobody simply follows the loudest voice. The room talks it through with Vivo and decides.

    Your part
    Bring one real question and the people who decide it.
    What you get
    One decision made, with the reasoning kept beside it.

  6. Step 6

    After

    You run it

    You open it because there is a decision to make.

    The Radar keeps watching. Vivo drafts each agenda 24 hours before the session. No facilitator is in the room. Your team runs the session weekly, fortnightly or monthly.

    Your part
    60 minutes per session, at the rhythm you choose.
    What you get
    A strategy that stays current between offsites.
