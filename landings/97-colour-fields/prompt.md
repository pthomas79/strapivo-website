# 97. Colour fields

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: Twelve painted fields, and one that changes colour while you look
- Direction: A gallery wall. Twelve soft-edged fields in muted greens, with feathered edges, layered washes and grain, in the manner of colour field painting. One turns amber, one a deep red. A light serif, small.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A painting. Twelve upright fields of colour sit on a warm ground, three rows of four or two rows of six. Their edges are soft and uneven, the colour is built from several translucent layers, and there is grain in the paint. Almost all of them are green, each a slightly different green.

Each field is one of the company's goals, and its colour is today's light.

While the visitor looks, one field warms slowly from green to amber. Later, lower on the page, the view moves in on that one field until it fills the screen. A second field, further away, has gone a deep red.

Each field carries only a small number, 01 to 12. Pointing at one, or tapping it, shows a single line, for example "07 · turned yellow three days ago".

**The words.**
- h1: "Twelve goals. One changed colour this week."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and sees, at a glance, what needs attention.
- On the close view: "Vivo asks whether the light is still right. People change it."

**Phone.** Three columns of four. The close view works especially well here.

**Still.** The painting with one amber field and one red.
