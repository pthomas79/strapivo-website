01 · The 90-day blind spot

Leadership agrees a strategy. Then it sits untouched until the board pack is due.

"I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings."

What leadership teams tell us

After an offsite, the slides go into a folder. Market signals arrive as noise and small adjustments happen in side conversations nobody records.

Two nights before the board meeting, someone reconstructs the quarter from emails. Directors read a summary instead of the calls your team made.

Dynamic Strategy replaces that scramble with a 60-minute weekly review against your Ambition and success criteria.

02 · Between meetings

Four records that build across the quarter.

Each weekly session updates one record, so every answer for the chair is dated and ready.

01 · Risk radar

Confirmed risk movements

Each signal carries a date and source. Moving a risk into the top-right danger zone requires a primary source and human confirmation.

02 · Record of Decisions

Weekly calls with named dissent

Leaders submit sealed first takes before discussion so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room. You log each call as Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet, with named dissent.

03 · Escalation

Memos beyond management authority

When a call exceeds management authority, Strapivo routes it to an approver or prepares an escalation memo for the board.

04 · Board export

A Markdown board pack ready to share

Export status lights, risk shifts, numbered decisions and open escalations as Markdown, ready for your board papers.

03 · Inside one board pack

Four sections exported from the Strategy Room.
