# 58. Between board meetings

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: CEOs reporting to a board and non-executive chairs
- Colours: House palette, plus deep slate (#1C2632) for board milestones and muted forest (#2B6B52) for confirmed decision markers.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Built on the executive voice in the brief: "I can't show the board that strategy actually moved between meetings." This is NOT concept 02 (which looked like a literal memorandum document). This is a polished, modern executive landing page centred on the 90-day span between Q1 and Q2 board meetings, showing how weekly Dynamic Strategy turns an invisible quarter into a clear, exportable board pack.

**Hero.** Clean two-column desktop hero or structured full-width header + architectural 90-day arc filling the lower half of the first screen cleanly. Headline: "Show the board how strategy moved between meetings." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System where your leadership team watches the market against your agreed plan, makes weekly 60-minute calls and exports a complete record for the board. Primary call to action.
Drawing 1 (filling the hero visual area): a crisp **90-day quarterly arc** (labelled "Illustrative example") running from "Q1 Board" on the left to "Q2 Board Pack" on the right. Along the line sit 12 weekly ticks, with three clean markers rising cleanly above: Week 3 ("SDS-2026-011 · Adapt · Supplier risk confirmed"), Week 7 ("SDS-2026-014 · Hold · Reviewed with named dissent"), Week 10 ("SDS-2026-017 · Escalated for board approval").

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. The 90-day blind spot. Strategy gets agreed at the offsite, then disappears until someone scrambles to assemble slides two nights before the board meeting.
2. What accumulates automatically between meetings. Four crisp, scannable rows: confirmed risk movements with dates and primary sources; weekly 60-minute decisions with dissent recorded by name; escalation memos for calls beyond management authority; and a Markdown board pack ready to export.
3. Inside one board pack. Drawing 2: a clean, high-legibility visual preview of the four sections inside an exported Strapivo board pack (Ambition and criterion lights, Risk movements since last quarter, Numbered decisions with dissent, Items requiring board approval). Keep text 12 px or larger, never a dense screenshot.
4. Human authority and European trust. Vivo proposes; people decide. Owner confirms changes to accepted truth. EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design.
5. Getting started (four to five hours, bring your existing board material) and closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Conservative, poised, boardroom-grade. Generous margins, razor-sharp hairlines, restrained palette, zero startup tropes. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
