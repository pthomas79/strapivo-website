# 107. The score

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A five-line stave where each week's decision is a note
- Direction: Music engraving. Cream paper (#FBF6E9), black ink, one ink-blue tie (#1D3F8F). An engraver's serif with a true italic for the markings, small capitals for the line names.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG. One five-line stave runs the full width of the screen, vertically centred, with 34px between its lines. The h1 and the plain sentence sit above it on the left, the button below it on the left.

- In place of a clef, each line is named at its left end, top to bottom: "Pivot", "Take advantage", "Adapt", "Hold", "Not yet".
- Bar lines divide the stave into twelve bars, one per week. "Week 1" is written under the first, "Week 12" under the last. A double bar ends the stave.
- Each bar holds one note on the line of that week's answer: a filled oval head, 26px by 18px, tilted 20 degrees, with a stem. The order is Hold, Hold, Adapt, Hold, Not yet, Hold, Adapt, Take advantage, Hold, Adapt, Hold, Pivot.
- The "Not yet" note in week 5 is hollow. A long ink-blue tie curves from it to the note in week 10, with the italic marking "Parked. Returns in week 10."
- Above the first bar, an italic tempo marking: "Weekly, sixty minutes." At the far end, small: "Illustrative".

On the first screen the first four notes are already printed. Pin the stage for about 200vh. A thin vertical line crosses the stave with the scroll, and each note appears as it passes. The tie draws itself from week 5 to week 10.

**The words.**
- h1: "Five lines. One note a week."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team reviews its strategy each week and ends every question with one of five answers.
- Under the stave: "Most weeks the answer is Hold. That is a decision too."

**Phone.** Break the stave into two systems of six bars, stacked. Lines are 22px apart. The line names sit at the left of the first system only.

**Still.** All twelve notes and the tie.

Read `../BRIEF.md` sections 1 to 5 for facts, audience, truth rules and calls to action.
