# 69. Stop restarting the argument

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Leadership teams tired of re-litigating the same strategic debate every quarter
- Colours: Stark white (#FFFFFF), deep ink (#050C14) and full-strength Azure (#0082E6), plus one muted charcoal (#525A63) for the closed loop.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Built squarely on the executive quote from the brief: "We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands." When a company has no shared strategic memory and no numbered Record of Decisions, every fresh report resets the conversation to zero. Strapivo breaks the circular loop into a forward, cumulative record where each 60-minute session starts from what was already decided, why, and who disagreed.

**Hero.** Crisp, bold editorial top section on stark white with sharp 0 to 2 px corners. Left column: headline "Stop restarting the same argument every time a new report lands." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System that holds your approved strategy, watches live market signals against it, and records every weekly leadership call so your team builds forward instead of repeating last quarter's debate. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a striking geometric SVG contrast:
- Top half (charcoal): a **closed circular loop** with four terse nodes (`New analysis` -> `Same debate` -> `Slides filed` -> `Forgotten` -> back to `New analysis`).
- Bottom half (bold Azure): the line **broken open into a forward stepped path** (`Signal confirmed` -> `60-min session` -> `SDS-2026-014 recorded` -> `Next call starts here`).

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why smart teams argue in circles. Decisions disappear into slide decks and meeting notes nobody reopens. Six months later, nobody remembers what was chosen, what assumption it rested on, or why an alternative was parked. Include a second unattributed executive quote ("We finish the strategy, and then we forget it.").
2. How cumulative memory works. Drawing 2: a clean vertical or horizontal chain of three connected decision entries for a fictional company (labelled "Illustrative example"): `SDS-2026-008 · Parked (waiting for Q2 audit)` -> `SDS-2026-014 · Adapt (audit landed, two activities revised)` -> `SDS-2026-019 · Hold (reviewed on date, risk easing)`. Each step visibly cites the prior number.
3. The weekly discipline behind the record. Radar signals on up to 4 focus areas, 24-hour drafted agenda (old business first), sealed first takes, five options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet), and read-back with dissent by name.
4. Human authority, EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design, and four-to-five-hour onboarding ("We do the reading first").
5. High-contrast Ink closing call-to-action band.

**Tone and look.** Bold, punchy, high-contrast typographic editorial. Stark white and deep ink, sharp geometry, generous negative space, crisp Azure line work. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
