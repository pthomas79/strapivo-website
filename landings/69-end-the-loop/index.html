01 / The loop

Why smart teams argue in circles

Most leadership teams finish a strategy workshop, circulate the slides and return to daily work. Decisions sit in decks and meeting notes that nobody reopens.

Six months later, a fresh market analysis arrives. Nobody remembers what the team chose, what assumption supported it, who disagreed or why the team parked another option. The room debates the same question from scratch.

Without shared strategic memory, leadership alternates between inertia and thrash. Teams either do nothing until the next annual offsite or react to whichever report landed last.

02 / Cumulative memory

Every decision cites the one before it

Strapivo assigns every decision a permanent number in the Record of Decisions. When new evidence arrives, your team starts from the prior entry instead of starting over.

03 / The weekly discipline

How a 60-minute session prevents repeat debates

Sessions run weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Vivo, Strapivo's AI, prepares the evidence. People make every decision.

  1. 01

    Radar on up to four focus areas

    The Radar monitors outside developments tied to your focus areas, objectives and risks. Limiting focus to at most four criteria keeps noise out of the room.

  2. 02

    Agenda drafted 24 hours ahead

    Vivo drafts the agenda from recent changes: confirmed signals, risks entering the danger zone, returning parked items and off-track activities. Old business comes first so open threads close before new debates begin.

  3. 03

    Sealed first takes

    Before discussion begins, each leader writes an independent position. Strapivo reveals all positions together so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room.

  4. 04

    Five clear decisions

    Every agenda item ends with Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Parked questions require a return trigger.

  5. 05

    Read-back with dissent by name

    Vivo reads back each decision entry and records any dissent by name with its reason. Once the session lead confirms the entry, it joins your permanent record.

Five options per agenda item Defined outcomes
01 Hold

The criterion stays intact and receives a review date.

02 Adapt

Vivo drafts revisions to objectives and activities for human approval.

03 Pivot

Revised wording for the criterion takes effect once an owner confirms it.

04 Take advantage

The team adds detour activities without altering the criterion.

05 Not yet

The team parks the question with a return trigger, or lets it go with a written reason.
