01 · The problem

Why rooms go quiet

When the most senior person speaks first, everyone else edits their view to match. The room appears aligned, yet commitment drops after the meeting ends.

Six months later, the market shifts and memory fails. Past decisions stay in slide decks nobody reopens, and the early objection is gone.

"We restart the same argument every time a new analysis lands."

What leadership teams tell us

Dissent margin

A doubt raised in a corridor never enters the record. Two quarters later, the same debate restarts.

02 · Before discussion

Sealed before the room speaks

Between sessions, the Radar monitors external sources against your focus areas. Twenty-four hours before the team meets, Vivo drafts the agenda from confirmed risk moves, off-track activities, and returning parked questions.

Before anyone speaks on an item, each leader writes a private first take against the evidence brief. Strapivo opens all five views together so seniority does not anchor the room.

03 · The session

Five outcomes, read back cleanly

A Dynamic Strategy session runs for 60 minutes, weekly, fortnightly, or monthly. After reviewing the evidence with Vivo, the team closes each item on one of five options.

  • Hold

    Mark the success criterion reviewed today and keep the current plan.

  • Adapt

    Revise objectives and activities under the criterion. Vivo drafts the edits for approval.

  • Pivot

    Draft new wording for the success criterion. It takes effect when an Owner confirms it.

  • Take advantage

    Add detour activities for an opening without changing the criterion.

  • Not yet

    Record what is missing, then park the question with a return trigger or let it go with a rationale.

Read-back

Vivo reads back the decision and names each dissenter with their reason. The session lead confirms before recording.
