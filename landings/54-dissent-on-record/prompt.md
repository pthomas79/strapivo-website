# 54. Dissent on the record

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Board chairs and CEOs who want candour instead of polite consensus
- Colours: House palette, plus warm cream (#FAF8F4) for the paper surface and muted burgundy (#8A3346) reserved strictly for dissent annotations and margin rules.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Serious leadership teams do not suffer from a lack of slides. They suffer from polite consensus in the room and selective memory six months later ("Who disagreed with this, and why?"). Strapivo builds candour directly into the workflow: sealed first takes prevent the loudest voice from anchoring the room, and the Record of Decisions preserves dissent by name with its reasoning alongside every numbered call.

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop layout. Left column: headline "Keep the decision. Keep who disagreed, and why." Two plain sentences stating what Strapivo is (the Strategic Design System for leadership teams running Dynamic Strategy) and how it turns strategic sessions into a living, honest record. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a large typographic decision artefact for a fictional company (labelled "Illustrative example") split cleanly into a main body (SDS-2026-014, Option: Adapt, Rationale, Confirmed by CEO) and a distinct right-hand burgundy-ruled **Dissent margin** naming one fictional executive and their two-line counter-argument preserved on the record.

**The page.** Carry the margin-note layout lightly through the body (wide main column with short mono/burgundy margin callouts on desktop, neatly stacked on mobile). At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. Why rooms go quiet. When the most senior person speaks first, everyone edits their view to match. Six months later, nobody remembers the objection that proved right.
2. Sealed before the room speaks. Drawing 2: a clean diagram of five sealed first-take cards alongside the evidence brief, revealed simultaneously before discussion starts.
3. Five outcomes, read back cleanly. How a 60-minute session closes on Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, or Not yet, followed by Vivo's read-back for the session lead to confirm with dissent intact.
4. Built for boards and leadership teams. Exportable board packs and escalation memos, EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design, Vivo proposes and people decide.
5. Getting started (four to five hours, we do the reading first) and closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Quiet, literary-corporate, refined. Warm off-white background, sharp hairline margin rules, restrained burgundy accent for dissent notes, generous breathing room. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
