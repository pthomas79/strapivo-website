# 64. Bring what you have

- Range: Third round: landing page candidates
- Audience: Owner-managed SMEs and pragmatic strategy leads who already have a plan
- Colours: House palette, plus cool linen (#F2F4F7) for input documents and muted sage (#2F7057) for Owner-accepted structure.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty third-round concepts. Each is meant to work as Strapivo's real main landing page, and each must look and read differently from the other 19 and from the 50 pages of rounds one and two.

## The concept

Pragmatic SME owners and strategy heads hate software that demands a six-month methodology overhaul before day one. Strapivo is method-agnostic: you bring whatever you already have — a workshop report, a strategy document, a pasted email or a plain prompt. Vivo proposes where each item belongs ("This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down?") and an Owner accepts or adjusts it. You can even start with public and non-sensitive material and widen from there.

**Hero.** Balanced two-column desktop hero. Left column: headline "Keep your strategy. Give it a system that stays current." Two plain sentences explaining Strapivo: the Strategic Design System that turns your existing strategy documents into a living Dynamic Strategy, watched against market signals and updated in a 60-minute weekly session. Primary call to action. Right column: Drawing 1, a clean **intake-to-structure diagram** (labelled "Illustrative example"). Three understated input chips on the left (Workshop report, Board email, Plain prompt) flow into a clean Azure sorting prompt ("This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down? -> Owner accepts") and emerge cleanly on the right as Ambition, 4 Focus Areas, Objectives and Activities.

**The page.** At most three product drawings total. Sections in order:
1. No proprietary framework required. Whether your plan came from an offsite, an annual board pack or a two-page memo from the managing director, Strapivo works with it. Nothing needs tidying first ("We do the reading first").
2. How your material becomes a working system. Drawing 2: a concise three-column (stacked on mobile) visual of the three onboarding steps — Draft from your material (you walk into session one with a draft to correct, not a blank page), Teach Vivo what relevant means, Run your first real strategic session together — four to five hours total, then you run it.
3. What runs every week once it is in. The Radar watches your focus areas, Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the 60-minute session, everyone submits a sealed first take, the team picks one of five options (Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet), and each call receives a number like SDS-2026-014.
4. Start small on data, stay in control. Start with public and non-sensitive material and widen from there. EU-only infrastructure, GDPR by design. Vivo proposes; people decide.
5. Closing call to action.

**Tone and look.** Pragmatic, understated, reassuring. Clean paper surfaces, generous whitespace, calm editorial typography, zero consultant jargon. Do not use "over 4,000 sources a day" on this page.
