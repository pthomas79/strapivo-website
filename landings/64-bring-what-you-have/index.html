Weekly cadence

What runs every week once your strategy is in.

Once your approved strategy is in Strapivo, the Radar matches market shifts to your focus areas and feeds a 60-minute weekly session. Every decision goes on record.

The 60-minute weekly loop Dynamic Strategy
  1. 01

    Radar

    The Radar monitors outside shifts for your focus areas and flags dated signals with sources.

  2. 02

    Agenda

    Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the 60-minute session from what changed.

  3. 03

    Sealed first takes

    Each leader enters a position before discussion. Everyone sees all positions together so nobody anchors the room.

  4. 04

    Five options

    The team chooses Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, or Not yet.

  5. 05

    Permanent record

    Each decision receives a permanent number such as SDS-2026-014, with dissent recorded by name.

SDS-2026-014 Illustrative example

Move two service hires into southern Germany before October. Vivo drafted the activity changes. The operations lead recorded dissent on onboarding capacity, with a review date set.
