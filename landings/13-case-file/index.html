Outside the case

This is what Strapivo does, every time.

Take away the trays and the names. The same eight steps run for every focus area, between every session. Most weeks that means a small correction. Now and then it means a change of course.

  1. 01WatchThe Radar reads over 4,000 sources a day against each focus area and reports on Monday and Thursday.Day 0 · 07:40
  2. 02TriageVivo proposes a criterion and a one-line reason. A person marks each signal relevant, irrelevant or unsure.Day 0 · 10:15
  3. 03MoveVivo proposes a new position for a risk as a ghost. A person confirms or rejects it.Day 3 · 09:10
  4. 04AgendaVivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session from what changed. The lead edits it. The room approves it.Day 8 · 09:00
  5. 05SessionSixty minutes by default. Per item, the room sees the evidence, gives sealed first takes and talks it through with Vivo.Day 9 · 09:14
  6. 06DecideThe room picks one of five options. Each option has a fixed effect on the plan, listed below.Day 9 · 09:31
  7. 07RecordVivo reads the entry back with dissent by name. The lead confirms. The decision gets a permanent number.Day 9 · 09:38
  8. 08Escalate and announceDecisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver, a memo or the board pack. The lead is prompted to announce what changed.Day 10 · 08:30

The five options

Hold
The criterion is marked reviewed on that date.
Adapt
Vivo drafts changes to objectives and activities.
Pivot
Vivo drafts new wording for the criterion. It takes effect when someone with authority confirms it.
Take advantage
Detour activities join the plan. The criterion stays as it is.
Not yet
The question is parked with a trigger that brings it back, or let go with a reason.

The guardrails

The human stays in the chair.

Vivo proposes. People decide. Every move and every decision in this file carries a person's name and a time.

  • 01
    One blog post can't raise an alarm.A move into the top right needs at least one primary source. Signal 0412 alone was not enough.
  • 02
    Vivo never changes a light.It asks "Light still green?" when two or more focus activities go off track or a risk on that criterion enters the top right. A person answers.
  • 03
    Nothing stays dangerous and undiscussed.A risk that sits in the top right for 14 days with no decision lands in the session lead's Needs you list.
  • 04
    Dissent is kept.The record names who disagreed and why. Tomás's reason is still there when the volume plan arrives.
  • 05
    Recommendations trace back.Every recommendation Vivo makes traces back to the strategic memory you approved. Vivo is hardwired to your business model. It does not work from a PDF pasted into a prompt.
  • 06
    EU-only infrastructure.GDPR by design.
