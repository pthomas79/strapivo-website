# 13 · The case file

Range: Confident (concept 13 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Anyone who wants to see one decision end to end
Colours: Paper, ink, Azure. One additional colour: archival green #3F6B55 for stamps and status marks.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/13-case-file/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

One decision, followed from the first signal to the entry in the record. The page is the case file of SDS-2026-014 at a fictional mid-sized company, clearly labelled as an illustrative example. Invent a plausible, undramatic case: for instance, a packaging manufacturer whose largest customer signals a move to a reusable format. Pick your own case, keep it specific and credible.

Structure: a vertical chain of dated documents, each a different artefact: the signal (source, source class, date); Vivo's triage note with its one-line reason; the ghost move on the radar and who confirmed it; the agenda line with its reason; the sealed first takes of five people and their reveal; the decision, one of the five options, with the dissent of one named fictional person and their reason; the read-back; the consequences drafted by Vivo; the announcement of what changed and why. Then a short section that steps out of the case and says: this is what Strapivo does, every time.

Signature element: the artefacts. Each is styled as its own small document or interface fragment, connected by a dated spine down the page with mono timestamps. Day counts between steps are visible, so the reader sees how little time passes between a signal and a recorded decision.

- Strapivo sells to SMEs and corporates. The result must be something a CEO would forward to their board: confident, well made, never playful for its own sake.
- Fonts and base colours are those of the current site (Inter 400 and 500, JetBrains Mono, Paper, Navy, Azure). The additional colours above are yours to use with a clear role.
- Craft matters more than volume. Spend your effort on the signature element and on the copy.
