# 74. Five words

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: Five giant words that each behave as they mean
- Direction: Black and white, or one flat colour per word. A very heavy variable grotesque at screen-filling size (a Google variable font with weight and width axes).

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** Type is the image. Five screens, one word each, set as large as the screen allows. Each word moves the way its meaning says:

- **Hold** stays exactly where it is while the page scrolls underneath it.
- **Adapt** changes its own weight and width as the visitor scrolls.
- **Pivot** turns ninety degrees around its first letter and carries on in the new direction.
- **Take advantage** leaves its line and moves ahead, off the grid.
- **Not yet** is an outline only, parked at the side, with a small tag: "Returns in May."

Before the five: one opening screen. After them: the button.

**The words.** One caption per word, eight words at most, in small type far from the word.
- h1: "Every strategic question ends in one of five answers."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team brings the question, decides, and keeps the record.
- Captions: "Reviewed. Nothing changes." "Same goal, new route." "The goal itself changes." "An opening. Take it." "Parked, with a date to come back."

**Phone.** Words run vertically or break by syllable if they must, but stay huge.

**Still.** The five words stacked, each frozen in its characteristic pose.
