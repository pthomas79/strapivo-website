A note from the curator

None of these objects failed. Each recorded one moment, as it was made to do. A strategy has to keep working after that moment.

Leadership teams run it like steering a ship. They make small, early corrections and, now and then, a change of course. We call that practice Dynamic Strategy. Strapivo is the software for it, and the next room shows it in use.

Strategy finally has a home

Strapivo is the Strategic Design System. It holds the ambition, the success criteria, their objectives, activities and risks, connected to your Business Model Canvas. Vivo, its AI, watches the market and drafts proposals. Your team decides. Each part below replaces an object from the collection.

The radar

Every risk sits on a radar of impact against likelihood. Vivo reads the signals and proposes a move as a ghost on the radar, with a one-line reason. A person confirms or rejects it.

A move into the top right needs at least one primary source, so that one blog post can't raise an alarm.

The agenda, built from what changed

Market intelligence watches the outside world for each focus area. It files every signal against the success criterion it touches, with a date and a source.

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session. Old business comes first, then new business, and every item carries a one-line reason. The session lead edits it, and the room approves it as step one.

Room 4 of 4House rules

The human stays in the chair

Teams want help from AI, not a co-author holding the pen. Vivo proposes. People decide.

  1. Vivo never changes a success criterion's light. When the evidence turns, it asks: "Light still green?"
  2. A Pivot takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.
  3. Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory you approved.
  4. Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver, as a memo or into the board pack.
  5. EU-only infrastructure. GDPR by design.
