## The concept

An exhibition. The visitor walks through a small museum whose collection is the artefacts of strategy as it is usually practised, each on a plinth with a museum label. Dry, affectionate, exact. It never mocks the people, only the objects.

Exhibits, each drawn as a simple flat object with a placard giving title, date, medium and a short curatorial note: 'Strategy deck, 120 slides. Presented once.' 'Flip chart from the offsite. Marker on paper. The handwriting is no longer attributable.' 'Risk register. Spreadsheet. Last modified the day it was created.' 'Minutes of the meeting. Read by their author.' 'Action list. Seven owners, three still with the company.' 'The loudest voice. Sound installation.' Write six to eight, at this level of dryness. Use an invented accession number on each placard.

Then the last room, signed 'Not part of the collection: in use.' It changes register completely: clean product surfaces in the house palette showing the living equivalents. The radar, the agenda built from what changed, the sealed first takes, the five options, the numbered record. Each is paired with the exhibit it retires. The exit is the call to action. A final placard: 'Please do not donate your strategy.'

Signature element: the gallery. Generous white space, objects on plinths with soft floor shadows, placards in mono with accession numbers, a small floor guide ('Room 1 of 4'). The objects are drawn in SVG with a consistent, slightly deadpan line.
