Strategic memorywhat's true inside your company

Your strategy, written down once and approved.

It holds one Ambition and seven to twelve success criteria, each with today's light. Up to four are flagged as focus. Under each sit objectives and activities, and every activity has a person responsible. It also holds your risks and past decisions.

Vivo, the AI in Strapivo, proposes where each item goes. An Owner approves it.

Market intelligencelive signals from outside it

The Radar reads the market for each focus area.

Its search brief is your success criterion, with its objectives and risks. Each signal carries a date and a source. Vivo matches it to a criterion with one line of reason, and a person marks it relevant or not.

When a signal changes a risk, Vivo proposes a new position on the radar and a person confirms it. A move into the top right needs a primary source, so one blog post can't raise an alarm.

Strategic sessionthe conversation where the two meet

Sixty minutes, with an agenda built from what changed.

Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours ahead from new signals, risks in the top right, activities off track and parked questions that are due. The session lead edits it.

Each person seals a first take before discussion, so nobody adjusts their view to the first speaker. Each item ends in one of five options.
