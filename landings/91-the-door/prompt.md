# 91. The door

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A dark wall, a door ajar, and light spilling out
- Direction: Night blues and near black, one warm light. Soft falloff, a real floor, a door with thickness and a handle. Thin, quiet type.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A dark wall fills the screen. In it, one tall door, a hand's width open. Warm light comes through the gap and falls as a long wedge across the floor towards the viewer.

As the visitor scrolls, the door swings open (a hinged panel in CSS 3D, with perspective). The wedge of light widens across the floor and up the frame. When the door stands open, the room beyond is almost pure light, and one thing is visible in it: a ring.

The rendering is the page. Get the light right: falloff across the floor, a soft edge on the wedge, a faint glow on the door's edge and the frame, deep shadow on the wall. No furniture, no people, no room detail.

**The words.**
- h1: "Strategy finally has a home."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is the room where a leadership team keeps its strategy and returns to it every week.
- With the door half open: "Someone is in."
- With the door open: "Come in." and the button.

**Phone.** Portrait suits a door. Let it fill the height.

**Still.** The door half open, light across the floor.
