# 14 · Five options

Range: Confident (concept 14 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: Leadership teams tired of meetings that end without a call
Colours: Paper, ink, Azure for Adapt. Four additional muted colours, one per option: Hold slate #5B6B7A, Pivot terracotta #B5553C, Take advantage green #2E7D5B, Not yet ochre #B88A2A.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/14-five-options/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

Built around the claim that every strategic question ends in one of five answers: Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet. The page argues that naming the options is what makes a meeting produce a decision.

Structure: a hero that sets the five words very large in a row; a short passage on meetings that end in "let's keep an eye on it"; then five full-width bands, one per option, each with its colour, its definition, what Vivo drafts afterwards, what a person with authority must confirm, and a one-line illustrative example. 'Not yet' gets the most room: the question "what's missing?", Parked with its triggers, Let go with its rationale, "so that it feels like parking, not losing." Then how a question reaches the table at all (radar, agenda, sealed first takes) and where the answer goes (the record).

Signature element: an interactive selector near the top. A sample question sits above five large buttons. Choosing one shows, in a panel that slides open, exactly what happens next for that option, as a short sequence. The chosen colour tints the panel. It works by keyboard, and all five outcomes are present in the HTML.
