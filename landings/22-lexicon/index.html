Dynamic Strategy

noun · the subject of this lexicon

How a leadership team keeps an agreed strategy honest, with small, early corrections and occasionally a change of course.

Usage "We're not changing strategy every week, but we are revising our tactics."

Every other function got software, and strategy never did.

Strapivo is the Strategic Design System, the one place where a company's strategy lives, stays current and gets used.

The 26 words below name its parts.

Each word means one thing, so a leadership team spends the session on the decision instead of the definition.

    ALetter A

    Activity

    noun

    A piece of work under one objective. It has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible, and its status is on track, at risk, off track or done.

    Usage "Two focus activities are off track, so Vivo asks whether the light is still green."

    See also Objective, Contributor, Light

    Adapt

    decision

    One of five decisions a session can reach. The success criterion stays. Vivo drafts changes to the objectives and activities beneath it.

    Usage "We adapt. The criterion stays, and two objectives change."

    See also Hold, Pivot, Take advantage, Not yet

    Agenda

    noun

    Vivo drafts it 24 hours before each session from what changed. That means new signals, risks entering the top right, parked questions coming back, carried-forward items, activities at risk or off track, and asks from contributors.

    Old business comes first. Every item carries a one-line reason. The session lead edits the draft and the room approves it as step one.

    Usage "Item three is here because a risk entered the top right on Tuesday."

    See also Back from parking, Signal, Time held

    Ambition

    noun

    The one statement a portfolio is read against, with optional targets such as turnover, margin and a year. Usually 7 to 12 success criteria measure progress toward it.

    Usage "Does this objective move us toward the Ambition, or only keep us busy?"

    See also Focus area, Light, Objective

    BLetter B

    Back from parking

    state

    A parked question returns to the agenda because its trigger fired. A trigger is new evidence, a person's view, a date, or the next session.

    Usage "The pricing question is back from parking. The evidence it waited for arrived on Monday."

    See also Parked, Not yet, Agenda

    CLetter C

    Contributor

    role

    The person responsible for an activity. Before each session a contributor reports a status, one line on the result and, if needed, an ask of leadership.

    Usage "Her ask went straight onto Thursday's agenda."

    See also Activity, Owner, Agenda

    DLetter D

    Dissent

    noun

    A disagreement kept on the record by name, with its reason. Vivo reads it back with the decision, and the session lead confirms both.

    Usage "SDS-2026-014 carries one dissent. The reason is written next to her name."

    See also Record of Decisions, First take

    FLetter F

    First take

    noun

    Each person's position on an item, given before discussion and sealed. All first takes are revealed together, so the loudest voice cannot anchor the room.

    Usage "Four first takes said hold. One said pivot, and the conversation started there."

    See also Dissent, Hold, Pivot

    Focus area

    rule

    A success criterion flagged for focus. A portfolio has four at most. Try to flag a fifth and Strapivo asks: "You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?"

    Usage "Fill a few glasses to the brim instead of all twelve a little."

    See also Ambition, Radar, Light

    GLetter G

    Ghost

    noun

    A new position for a risk that Vivo proposes, drawn as a faint outline on the radar with a one-line reason. It moves the risk only when a person confirms it.

    Usage "There's a ghost in the top right. Nobody has confirmed it yet."

    See also Radar, Trail, Signal

    HLetter H

    Hold

    decision

    The success criterion stays as written, and Strapivo marks it reviewed on that date. Holding is a decision, and the record shows it.

    Usage "Hold. We looked, and nothing needs to change."

    See also Adapt, Not yet, Record of Decisions

    LLetter L

    Let go

    state

    One of two ways a "not yet" ends. The question closes, and its rationale stays on the record.

    Usage "We let go of the second warehouse. The rationale is in the record."

    See also Not yet, Parked

    Light

    state

    Today's status of a success criterion: green, yellow or red. A person sets it.

    Vivo asks "Light still green?" when two or more focus activities are off track, or when a risk on that criterion enters the top right. Vivo never changes the light itself.

    Usage "Yellow since March, and the chief strategy officer still defends it."

    See also Activity, Focus area, Vivo

    MLetter M

    Mission Control

    noun

    The home screen. It shows the next session with a countdown, the Needs you list, the success criteria at a glance and recent decisions.

    Usage "Open Mission Control and the Needs you list says what is waiting for you."

    See also Time held, Light, Record of Decisions

    NLetter N

    Not yet

    decision

    A decision to wait, with a question attached: what's missing? It ends as Parked, with at least one trigger that brings it back, or as Let go, with a rationale.

    Usage "Not yet. We need the churn figures for the third quarter first."

    See also Parked, Let go, Back from parking

    OLetter O

    Objective

    noun

    A goal under one success criterion, usually one of two to four. Activities sit beneath it. During setup Vivo proposes where each item belongs, and an Owner accepts or rejects the proposal.

    Usage "This reads like an activity, not an objective. Move it down?"

    See also Activity, Ambition, Owner

    Owner

    role

    The role that accepts what counts as true. Changes to accepted truth wait for an Owner. The other roles are Admin, Member and Contributor.

    Usage "Vivo proposed the change. The Owner accepted it on Friday."

    See also Contributor, Pivot, Vivo

    PLetter P

    Parked

    state

    A "not yet" question set aside with at least one trigger that brings it back. The trigger is evidence, a person's view, a date, or the next session.

    Usage "It feels like parking, not losing."

    See also Back from parking, Let go, Not yet

    Pivot

    decision

    A change to the wording of a success criterion. Vivo drafts the new wording. It takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.

    Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver in the app, as an exported memo, or into the board pack.

    Usage "We pivot on criterion four, once the board confirms."

    See also Adapt, Owner, Record of Decisions

    RLetter R

    Radar

    noun
    1. The plot of risks by impact against likelihood. The top right is the danger zone. The Now view shows where each risk sits today. The Tracking view shows movement since the last session, over 30 days or since origin.
    2. The watch on the outside world for each focus area. Its search brief is the success criterion, its objectives and its risks.

    Usage "On Tracking you can see where the risk sat three weeks ago."

    See also Ghost, Signal, Time held

    Record of Decisions

    noun

    The permanent log of what a team decided. Vivo reads back each entry, including dissent by name with its reason, and the session lead confirms it. Each decision gets a permanent number, such as SDS-2026-014.

    Usage "We settled that in May. Look up SDS-2026-014."

    See also Dissent, Trail, Hold

    SLetter S

    Signal

    noun

    A dated observation from outside the company, with a source and a source class, primary or secondary. Its state is New, Relevant, Watching or Dismissed.

    Vivo makes the first pass and names the criterion it touches. A delegated person marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise. Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday.

    Usage "One blog post is a signal. It cannot move a risk into the top right without a primary source."

    See also Radar, Ghost, Agenda

    TLetter T

    Take advantage

    decision

    The team adds detour activities to use an opening. The success criterion does not change.

    Usage "A competitor left the region. We take advantage, and the criterion stays as written."

    See also Adapt, Activity, Hold

    Time held

    noun

    How long a risk has stayed where it is, shown as top right · 27 days, next to a trend word: Easing, Stable or Worsening.

    A risk that sits in the top right for 14 days with no decision lands in the session lead's Needs you list.

    Usage "Top right, 27 days, worsening. That goes first."

    See also Radar, Mission Control, Agenda

    Trail

    noun

    The history of every confirmed risk move. Each move carries its signal, its date and the person who confirmed it.

    Usage "The trail shows who moved it, when, and on what evidence."

    See also Ghost, Radar, Record of Decisions

    VLetter V

    Vivo

    name

    Strapivo's AI. Vivo drafts agendas, triages signals, proposes risk moves and reads back decisions. It never changes a light, a criterion or the record by itself. Vivo proposes; people decide.

    It takes five roles: Auditor, Advisor, Scout, Colleague and Researcher. Every recommendation traces back to the strategic memory the team approved.

    Usage "Where did this number come from?" Vivo, as Auditor.

    See also Ghost, Light, Owner

    Appendix

    Words we do not use

    The product says objective, activity and result. These three words never appear in it.

    KPI

    never say

    Strapivo is not a KPI dashboard. A success criterion carries a light that a person sets and defends in the room.

    Say instead Light, Ambition

    OKR

    never say

    OKR is a method with its own rules. Strapivo does not prescribe a method. Setup accepts a workshop report, a document, a pasted email or a plain prompt.

    Say instead Objective, Activity

    Key result

    never say

    A result belongs to one activity and one person responsible. It needs no other adjective.

    Say instead Activity, Contributor
