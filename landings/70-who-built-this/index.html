01 Why we built a home for strategy

Every other function got software. Strategy got slides.

Leadership teams agree a strategy at an offsite, then leave the slides in a folder. Months pass while competitors move and risks build, until the next annual review arrives too late to correct course cheaply.

You can buy market monitoring and you can buy an AI chat tool. Neither holds your approved strategy, so every new report restarts the same argument.

02 Dynamic Strategy in practice

Four steps that keep an agreed strategy current

Most organisations review strategy once a year while markets move every week. Dynamic Strategy prevents both inertia and weekly thrash through one structured loop.

Dynamic Strategy operating loop Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly sessions

Up to four focus areas

04 Governance and trust

Built for board-level scrutiny

Boards do not trust software that rewrites approved strategy without permission. Strapivo separates AI preparation from human authority at every step.

01 · Authority

Humans decide. Humans validate.

Vivo drafts agendas and proposes risk moves or revised wording. Nothing changes accepted strategy until an authorised person confirms it.

02 · Provenance

Every recommendation traces back

Each proposal links to the strategic memory your team approved and the dated sources behind the signal. Nothing arrives without a trail.

03 · Residency

EU-only infrastructure

Strapivo runs on European Union infrastructure and follows GDPR requirements by design. Your strategic memory stays inside the European Union.

04 · Scope

Start with non-sensitive material

Start with public documents and non-sensitive strategy context. Verify how Vivo works on your business model, then widen scope at your own pace.
