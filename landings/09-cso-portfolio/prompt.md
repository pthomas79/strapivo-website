# 09 · The chief strategy officer's page

Range: Conservative (concept 9 of 40, ordered from conservative to a bit nuts)
Audience: CSOs and strategy teams in multi-business corporates
Colours: Paper, ink, Azure. One additional colour: deep teal #0E6B74 to distinguish portfolio scope from business-model scope.

## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/09-cso-portfolio/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

For the strategy professional who has run the annual cycle for years and knows exactly where it breaks. Precise, structured, respectful of their expertise. It uses the real vocabulary: portfolio, business models, risk scope, focus areas, escalation, board pack.

Structure: a documentation-style layout with a sticky section navigator on the left and content on the right. Sections: the gap between the annual cycle and the weekly reality; the object model (one Ambition per portfolio, success criteria that apply to named business models, focus areas, objectives, activities, risks scoped to the portfolio or to one or more business models); the radar with its business-model filter and portfolio-risk toggle; how a session agenda is assembled; decisions and their consequences; escalation routes and the board pack; how execution reports back without the strategy team managing tasks; method-agnostic setup built on the Business Model Canvas.

Signature element: one large, exact entity diagram in SVG showing how Ambition, success criteria, objectives, activities, risks, signals, sessions and decisions relate, with portfolio scope and business-model scope in two colours. Hovering or focusing a node highlights its relations. Below it, small annotated fragments of the radar filter and the board pack.
