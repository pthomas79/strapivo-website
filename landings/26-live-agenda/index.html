  1. 01
    Old businessBack from parking

    Why did the last strategy end up in a drawer?

    ReasonParked at the last offsite with a trigger: the next session. This is the next session.

    1:00

    A strategy gets agreed in a workshop. Then it goes into a drawer. The market keeps moving, and nobody notices until the quarterly or annual review, when a correction is no longer cheap.

    There are two ways to fail.

    InertiaNothing changes, whatever the news says.
    ThrashEverything changes, whenever the news says.

    Dynamic Strategy avoids both. The leadership team makes small, early corrections, and changes course when the evidence calls for it. Tactics can change every week. The strategy changes only by a recorded decision.

    Every other function got software. Strategy never did. Strapivo holds one Ambition, usually 7 to 12 success criteria with today's light on each, up to four focus areas, and the objectives, activities and risks under them.

  2. 02
    New business

    What the radar is watching

    ReasonNews arrives as noise until something ties it to what must succeed.

    0:55

    The Radar watches the outside world for each focus area. Its search brief is the success criterion, its objectives and its risks. Market intelligence reads over 4,000 sources a day and reports on Monday and Thursday. A red-line exception interrupts that schedule.

    Each signal is a dated observation with a source, marked primary or secondary. Vivo makes the first pass: which criterion it touches, relevant or not, and one line of reason. A person you delegate marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure. Dismissed signals teach Vivo what counts as noise.

    When a signal shifts a risk, Vivo draws the proposed position as a ghost on the radar. A person confirms it or rejects it. A move into the top right needs at least one primary source, so that one blog post can't raise an alarm.

    If a risk sits in the top right for 14 days with no decision, it lands in the session lead's Needs you list.

  3. 03
    New business

    How the agenda gets built

    ReasonYou are reading one. You should know where it came from.

    0:50

    Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before each session, from what changed since the last one:

    • new relevant signals
    • risks that entered the top right
    • parked questions whose trigger has fired
    • items carried forward
    • activities at risk or off track
    • asks from the people responsible for activities

    Old business comes first, then new business. Every item carries a one-line reason. The session lead edits the draft, and approving it is step one of the session.

    Sessions run 60 minutes by default, weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Extra sessions can be called when something can't wait.

  4. 04
    New business

    Sealed first takes

    ReasonMeetings anchor on the loudest voice.

    0:45

    Each item opens with the evidence. Then everyone in the room writes a first take alone. The takes stay sealed until they are all in, and then they open together.

    The conversation starts after that. Vivo joins when asked. It can put a figure on the cost of doing nothing, lay out scenarios, challenge the room, offer alternatives or name a black swan.

    The item ends with a decision.

  5. 05
    New business

    The five options

    ReasonEvery item ends in a decision, and there are five to choose from.

    0:50
    Hold
    The success criterion is marked reviewed, with the date.
    Adapt
    Vivo drafts changes to the objectives and activities under it.
    Pivot
    Vivo drafts new wording for the criterion. It takes effect when someone with authority confirms it.
    Take advantage
    Detour activities are added. The criterion stays as it is.
    Not yet Parked
    The room asks what is missing. The item is parked with at least one trigger that brings it back: evidence, a person's view, a date or the next session. Or it is let go, with a rationale. "So that it feels like parking, not losing." This page parks items the same way.
  6. 06
    New business

    The Record of Decisions

    ReasonLater, nobody can find what was decided, why, or who disagreed.

    0:50

    When an item is decided, Vivo reads back the entry for the Record of Decisions. Dissent goes in by name, with its reason. The session lead confirms the entry, and it gets a permanent number.

    A year later, anyone in the room can look up what was decided, on which evidence, who disagreed and what happened next. The board can see that strategy moved between meetings.

    The plan and the board pack export as Markdown. The plan also exports as CSV.

  7. 07
    New business

    Who holds authority

    ReasonTeams want help from AI, not a co-author with the pen.

    0:50

    Vivo proposes. People decide.

    Vivo never changes a light, never moves a risk and never rewrites a success criterion on its own. When two focus activities go off track, it asks "Light still green?" and a person answers.

    Decisions beyond the room's authority go to an approver: in the app, as an exported memo or in the board pack. Then Vivo prompts the session lead to announce what changed and why.

    Strapivo is hardwired to the Business Model Canvas. It is not a generic prompt with your PDF attached. Every recommendation from Vivo traces back to the strategic memory you approved. The infrastructure is EU-only and GDPR by design.

    Roles in the Strategy Room

    OwnerAccepts changes to what the company holds as true.
    ContributorResponsible for activities. Reports before each session.

    Admin and Member are the other two roles.
