## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/26-live-agenda/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

The page is an agenda. Vivo drafted it for this visit, and the visitor is the session lead who edits and approves it. This is how the product treats every meeting, applied to the landing page itself.

At the top: 'Agenda for your visit. Drafted by Vivo. About six minutes.' Then the items, each a real section of the page with a one-line reason for being on the agenda, exactly as the product gives one. Old business first: 'Back from parking: why did the last strategy end up in a drawer?' Then new business: what the radar is watching, how the agenda gets built, sealed first takes, the five options, the record, who holds authority. Each item expands into its content.

The visitor can reorder items, park an item (it moves to a Parked area at the foot with a trigger: 'returns when you scroll back' or 'returns on your next visit', kept in memory only), mark an item as covered, and finally press 'Approve agenda', which is step one of every session. A read-back at the end lists what they covered and what they parked, as the product reads back its record. State stays in the page and is not stored.

Signature element: the working agenda. Reordering works by buttons and keyboard as well as drag. With JavaScript off it is a clean, readable agenda with all sections open.
