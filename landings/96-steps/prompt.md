# 96. Steps

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A wall and a staircase that reach the same height
- Direction: Isometric drawing, warm concrete and sand tones, long soft shadows from one sun, one Azure ball. A plain geometric sans, small.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of thirty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** Two solid forms on an empty ground, drawn in isometric view with flat, two-tone shading and long shadows.

On the left, a single wall, as tall as the composition allows. Label: "One review a year."

On the right, a staircase of fifty-two shallow treads that rises to exactly the same height. Label: "One session a week."

A small Azure ball sits at the foot of the wall. It cannot get up. As the visitor scrolls, the ball goes to the stairs and climbs, one tread at a time, to the top. A faint dotted line shows the path it has taken.

Make it feel like architecture: exact geometry, consistent light, clean edges, a soft ground shadow. No figures, no flags, no trophies.

**The words.**
- h1: "Same height. Smaller steps."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy and adjusts it every week, not once a year.
- At the top: "Small corrections, made early, cost almost nothing."

**Phone.** The staircase crosses the screen on the diagonal. The wall stands at the left edge, cropped.

**Still.** Both forms, the ball halfway up the stairs.
