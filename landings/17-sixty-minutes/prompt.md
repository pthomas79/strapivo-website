## The task

Build a complete landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder (`landings/17-sixty-minutes/`). It is one of 40 deliberately different concepts, so commit fully to this one and do not drift toward a generic software landing page.

Read `../BRIEF.md` first. It holds the facts about Strapivo and Dynamic Strategy, the truth rules, the brand (fonts, colour tokens, logo), the build rules and the finishing steps. The brief decides what is true and what the brand allows. This prompt decides everything else.

## The concept

The session, minute by minute. The page is one 60-minute Strategy Room session and the scroll position is the clock. It shows that the meeting has a shape and that the shape is why it ends with decisions.

Structure: a fixed time rail down one side running from 00 to 60, with the visitor's position shown as the current minute. Stations along the hour, with your own sensible timings marked as "a typical hour": the room approves the agenda Vivo drafted; old business (a parked question returns, with what is new since); the first new item, with its evidence; sealed first takes, revealed together; the conversation, with Vivo asked for the cost of doing nothing; the decision, one of five; the second item, shorter; items not reached carry forward; the read-back of the record, including dissent; close. Before minute 00, a short section on what happened in the 24 hours before. After minute 60, what happens next and the call to action.

Signature element: the clock. A large mono minute counter in the corner that changes as you scroll, a thin progress arc drawn like the O-mark filling over the hour, and a soft time guide that nudges but never cuts off, as in the product.
