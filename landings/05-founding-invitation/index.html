Why we built it

Every other function got software. Strategy never did.

A leadership team agrees a strategy in a workshop. Then the market keeps moving and nobody checks the strategy against it. The gap shows up at the quarterly or annual review, when a correction costs the most.

News arrives unconnected to what the team said must succeed. Meetings follow the loudest voice. Decisions end up in slides and minutes that nobody reads.

Dynamic Strategy

What your team would be running.

Dynamic Strategy is how a leadership team keeps an agreed strategy current. It makes small corrections early and changes direction rarely, with someone in authority confirming each change. Strapivo holds the structure, and Vivo, the AI in Strapivo, does the preparation.

Ambition
One per portfolio, with optional targets for turnover, margin and a year.
Success criteria
Usually seven to twelve. Each shows today's light: green, yellow or red.
Focus areas
Up to four criteria. Ask for a fifth and Vivo replies: "You're diluting focus and creating noise. Do you really want to?"
Objectives and activities
Each activity has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible.
Risks
Each sits on a radar of impact against likelihood. The top right is the danger zone.

The order of proceedings

Each cycle

  1. Watch.The Radar reads the outside world for each focus area and logs dated signals, each with its source.

  2. Triage.Vivo suggests which criterion a signal concerns, with a one-line reason. A person marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure.

  3. Move.Vivo proposes a new position for a risk. A person confirms or rejects it. A move into the danger zone needs at least one primary source, so one blog post can't raise an alarm.

  4. Agenda.Vivo drafts the agenda 24 hours before the session from what changed. The session lead edits it, and the room approves it as the first item.

  5. Session.A session runs sixty minutes by default. Each person gives a sealed first take before the discussion, and the takes are revealed together.

  6. Decide.The room chooses Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Not yet ends as Parked, with a trigger that brings it back, or as Let go, with a reason.

  7. Record.Vivo reads back each decision, with dissent by name. The session lead confirms it, and the decision gets a permanent number.

  8. Escalate and announce.Decisions beyond the team's authority go to an approver. The lead is prompted to announce what changed and why.

Who decides

The human stays in the chair.

Vivo proposes; people decide. Every recommendation Vivo makes traces back to the strategic memory your team approved.

  • Vivo never changes a light.When two or more focus activities go off track, Vivo asks "Light still green?" and a person answers.
  • A pivot needs authority.Vivo drafts the new wording. It takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it. Changes to accepted truth wait for an Owner.
  • A risk can't sit unattended.A risk that stays in the danger zone for 14 days without a decision goes on the session lead's "Needs you" list.
  • Your data stays in the EU.Strapivo runs on EU-only infrastructure. GDPR by design.
