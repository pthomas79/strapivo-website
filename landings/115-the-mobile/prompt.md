# 115. The mobile

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A hanging mobile that leans when one piece changes
- Direction: A gallery wall in warm grey (#E4E0D8) with soft cast shadows. Thin black wires, matte black plates (#1C1C1A), one vermilion (#D8412F), one Azure (#0082E6). A light geometric sans, very small.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of fifty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** SVG with nested groups. A mobile hangs from one point at the top centre of the screen. The h1 block (h1, sentence and button) sits bottom left.

- A wire drops from the hanging point to the middle of the main rod, 46vw wide.
- From each end of the main rod hangs a second rod, 18vw wide.
- From each end of those hangs a large plate, four in all, each about 8vw across. Make each a different flat four-sided shape with no right angles.
- Under each large plate hangs a short rod, 8vw wide, with a small plate at each end: eight in all.

Labels, each on a fine line: "Ambition" at the hanging point, "A success criterion" beside one large plate, "An activity" beside one small plate.

Every rod sways 2 degrees each way, each at its own slow pace. Whatever hangs from a rod stays upright (counter-rotate it). A blurred copy at 12% black, offset 40px right and 60px down, is its shadow on the wall.

Pin the stage for about 250vh.

1. One small plate turns vermilion. Its short rod tilts 14 degrees, the second rod above it 8 degrees, the main rod 4 degrees. The whole mobile hangs crooked.
2. An extra small Azure plate is hung on the other end of the same short rod. All three rods return to level.

**The words.**
- h1: "Everything hangs from one Ambition."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo is where a leadership team keeps its strategy as one connected structure and adjusts it every week.
- With step 1: "One activity goes off track. Everything above it leans."
- With step 2: "Adapt. A change is drafted, a person approves it, and it hangs level again."

**Phone.** The main rod is 80vw wide and the large plates 15vw. Leave out the small plates under the two outer large plates.

**Still.** The mobile level again, with the vermilion and Azure plates and all labels.
