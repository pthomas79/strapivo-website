Drawer 1 · Emptied

Strapivo takes the folders out of the drawer.

Strapivo is the Strategic Design System. It keeps your strategy in one place, current and in use. It is built on the Business Model Canvas, so every item is linked to the part of the business it concerns.

We call the method Dynamic Strategy. The same four folders become objects your leadership team works with every week. Vivo, the AI in Strapivo, drafts and proposes. People decide.

Workshop reportNow the plan

The report becomes a plan that Owners accept

Upload the report, paste an email or describe the plan in a prompt. Vivo proposes where each item belongs: one Ambition, success criteria measured against it, objectives under each criterion.

An Owner accepts or rejects every proposal. Up to four criteria become focus areas. Try to flag a fifth and Vivo asks whether you want to dilute focus.

Risk listNow the radar

The risk list becomes a radar that moves

The Radar reads outside sources for each focus area. Vivo sorts every signal to a criterion with one line of reason. A named person marks it relevant, irrelevant or unsure.

When the evidence shifts a risk, Vivo draws the new position as a ghost and a person confirms or rejects it. A move into the top right needs at least one primary source, so one blog post can't raise an alarm.

MinutesNow the Record of Decisions

The minutes become a numbered Record of Decisions

Each item in a session ends in one of five decisions: Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet. Vivo reads the entry back, including dissent by name with its reason. The session lead confirms it and it gets a permanent number.

"Not yet" ends as Parked, with a trigger that brings the question back, or as Let go, with a rationale.

Action listNow activities

The action list becomes activities with a name on each

Each activity sits under one objective and has an action, a result, a timeframe and a person responsible. Before every session that person reports a status, one line on the result and, if needed, an ask of leadership.

Vivo builds the session agenda from those reports and from what changed outside.

Every session starts from what changed

With Dynamic Strategy, the team makes small, early corrections and changes course now and then. Tactics change often. The strategy changes when the team decides the evidence requires it.

  1. WatchThe Radar reads outside sources against each focus area.
  2. TriageVivo makes a first pass. A named person marks each signal.
  3. MoveVivo proposes new risk positions. A person confirms them.
  4. AgendaVivo drafts it 24 hours before the session. The room approves it first.
  5. SessionEveryone records a sealed first take before discussion. The takes are revealed together.
  6. DecideThe room picks Hold, Adapt, Pivot, Take advantage or Not yet.
  7. RecordEach decision gets a number and keeps dissent with its reason.

Vivo proposes. People decide.

  • Proposals onlyVivo never changes a light, a criterion or a risk position. A Pivot takes effect only when someone with authority confirms it.
