# 78. Growth rings

- Range: Fourth round: visual, few words
- Visual idea: A record of decisions as rings, one added per decision
- Direction: Either warm wood tones on cream with one Azure ring, or pale rings on deep navy. Mono for the decision numbers, a calm sans or serif for the rest.

## The task

Build a landing page for Strapivo as a single `index.html` in this folder. It is one of twenty fourth-round concepts. This round is about one image, one sentence and one button: highly visual, very few words, free of the brand's design rules.

## The concept

**The visual.** A cross-section, seen from above. At the centre a small solid dot: the Ambition. Around it, rings. Each ring is one decision, and each has a slightly irregular hand, like wood: uneven spacing, a little wobble, a few darker rings where the decision was a pivot.

The rings are added as the visitor scrolls, from the centre outward, until about forty fill the screen. The outermost ring is Azure: this week.

Pointing at a ring, or tapping it, shows its number and answer in mono, for example "SDS-2026-014 · Adapt". Show at most one label at a time.

**The words.**
- h1: "Every decision leaves a ring."
- Plain sentence: Strapivo keeps a leadership team's strategy and a permanent record of every decision made on it.
- Small, near the centre: "The Ambition."
- Near the edge: "What was decided, why, and who disagreed."

**Phone.** The section is cropped so the centre sits at the bottom edge and the rings rise as arcs.

**Still.** The full cross-section with three rings labelled.
